# Three approaches to getting found by AI

You have three ways to move your business into AI answers:

**Passive ranking.** You publish content on your site and hope Google and AI engines eventually discover and cite it. This costs little upfront but takes months or years, and there's no guarantee your rivals won't occupy that space first.

**Reactive scanning.** You run a free scan to see where AI answers and Google search send buyers to your rivals instead of you. A scan shows the gap between where you rank and where you need to be. It's a one-time measurement—useful for understanding the problem, but it doesn't close the gap.

**Active page publishing.** You write and publish pages built to answer the questions buyers ask AI and Google.
