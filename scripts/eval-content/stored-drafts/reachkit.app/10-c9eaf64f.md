## What Makes SEO Software Free and Worth Using

Free SEO software must do one thing well: show you where you stand against the buyers' journey. Most free tools measure isolated metrics—keywords, backlinks, page speed. The ones worth using measure what matters: whether AI recommends you, whether search sends buyers to you, whether your rivals own the visibility you need.

The second mark of free software that works is honesty about the gap. You need to see not just your score, but the distance between where you are and where you should be. A scan that shows you're missing from AI answers when your rivals appear there tells you what to fix. A scan that stays silent about that gap is mostly noise.

The third thing free software must do is move you forward from what it shows. A measurement that doesn't lead to action is just a number. You need the scan to tell you what's missing, then you need a way to close that gap. Pages that rank in your market don't write themselves. Either the software writes them for you or it leaves you stranded.
