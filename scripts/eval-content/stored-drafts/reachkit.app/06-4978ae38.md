# Best Free Software for SEO

Free SEO tools come in four types: audit tools show you technical problems on your site, trackers show how your rank changes week to week, keyword tools show what buyers are searching for, and content tools show you what your competitors publish. Pick the type that matches what you need to know first.

## What free SEO software does, and why scanning matters before you choose

Before you choose any tool, you need to see where Google and AI send buyers today. A free scan shows you this snapshot. It tells you which of your pages rank, which ones don't, and where your competitors appear instead. This matters because ranking in Google search alone is no longer enough—you now need to appear in AI answer summaries too.

The choice of tool depends on your next move. If you're a startup or small team, you often need a weekly tracker plus one good audit tool: you want to know where you stand now, then watch whether your fixes move the needle. If you're growing and tracking competitors, you need a market scan that covers both search and AI, measured week to week so you see whether the market shifts.

## How to compare free SEO tools by what they measure

**Audit tools** scan your site once and show technical problems: broken links, missing titles, slow pages. They answer: what's broken today? They suit teams who want a single snapshot.

**Rank trackers** measure the same keywords every week and show you gains and losses. They answer: is my rank moving? They suit owners who want to know if their changes work.

**Keyword research tools** show search volume and competition for terms you could target. They answer: what should I write about next? They suit teams planning content.

**Content analysis tools** show what your competitors publish and how they structure it. They answer: what topics rank and why? They suit teams learning from rivals.

**Market scanners** combine site audit, rank tracking, and competitor visibility in one tool, re-measured weekly. They answer: where do I stand against my market, and is it changing? They suit owners who want to track progress and fix visibility gaps systematically.

## The tools the market trusts, and what each does best

The market names five categories of free software for SEO work:

- **Audit tools** show the technical state of your site—errors, warnings, and fixes you can make in hours. Use these when you need to know what's broken before you build a strategy.

- **Rank trackers** measure how specific keywords move week to week in Google search. Use these when you want proof that your content changes are working.

- **Keyword research tools** show monthly search volume and competition level for terms in your market. Use these when you're planning which topics to write about.

- **Content analyzers** show what your competitors rank for and how they structure winning pages. Use these when you want to learn patterns from pages that already rank.

- **Market scanners** give you a free initial scan showing where Google and AI send buyers, then re-measure weekly so you see movement. [ReachKit](https://reachkit.app/pricing) is one option here: it combines a free scan with weekly re-measurement and page publishing, suited to owners who want to track progress and fix visibility gaps systematically.

Each type serves a different question. Start with a free audit or scan to see where you stand now. Then pick a tracker or scanner based on whether you need weekly proof that your changes work, or monthly snapshots are enough.
