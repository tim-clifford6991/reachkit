## What mistakes should you avoid?

The most common mistake is writing generic pages instead of pages that answer the specific questions your buyers ask and that rivals currently own. A page that doesn't match a real buyer question gives an AI engine nothing to cite it for.

- Guessing at keywords instead of measuring questions. Without a scan, you're optimizing for what you assume buyers ask, not what they type into ChatGPT or Google AI Mode.
- Publishing once and stopping. Ranking is tracked by weekly re-measure, not a single snapshot; a single page rarely closes a gap that a rival has owned for months.
- Writing from nothing. Content that isn't written from facts on your own site, in your own voice, has nothing specific for an AI engine to quote back to a buyer.
- Skipping the review step. Every page waits a day for you to read it or stop it before it goes live — skip that check and you risk publishing something under your name that you didn't verify.
- Measuring only Google. Remember the scan reads Google's top ten results alongside Google AI Overview, Google AI Mode and ChatGPT, but not Perplexity — if Perplexity matters to your buyers, you'll need to watch that separately.
