# What is the best SEO software?

The best SEO software depends on what you're trying to find out about your visibility. Site auditors scan your pages for technical problems. Rank trackers watch where you appear in Google search results. Content planners help you write pages that match what buyers are searching for. AI answer monitors watch what ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and other AI systems tell buyers about your market. Each solves a different problem.

## How to choose the right tool for your market

Pick a site auditor if you need to know what's broken on your pages. They scan your site and tell you where buyers hit dead links, slow load times, or pages that don't work on mobile. This matters if your visibility is low because your site itself doesn't work.

Pick a rank tracker if you know exactly which searches matter to your business and you want to watch them week by week. They tell you where you rank in Google results for those searches and how your position moves. This matters if you already know which buyer searches send business to you and your rivals.

Pick a content planner if you're writing new pages and want to match what buyers search for. They show you what words and topics appear in the top-ranking pages so you can write pages that fit what the market wants to read. This matters if you're building content from scratch or rewriting pages that don't rank.

Pick an AI answer monitor if your buyers are asking questions that land them in ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews, and you're not being named there. They scan what AI systems say about your market and show you where the gaps are—where buyers ask questions that don't mention you or your rivals. This matters if you're losing visibility to AI before it reaches Google search. ReachKit scans AI answers and search results about your market, then publishes one page a day to fill the gaps AI found.

The best choice starts with knowing your market. What do your buyers search for? Where do they find your rivals instead of you? Do they land in AI answers, or do they start in Google search? Once you know where you're losing visibility, you can pick the tool that plugs that leak.
