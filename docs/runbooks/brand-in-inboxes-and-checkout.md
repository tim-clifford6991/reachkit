# Brand in inboxes and Stripe Checkout

Owner steps for the parts of #1568 that are not code: the sender logo in Gmail and other inboxes
(DMARC, BIMI and a mark certificate, or the free Google-account fallback) and the logo on Stripe
Checkout. Nothing here was changed by the pull request that wrote it. Every DNS answer below was read
with `dig @1.1.1.1` on 2026-10-19.

What the code already does (#1568, #1121):

- Every mail's From reads `ReachKit <hello@reachkit.app>`, and Reply-To is `hello@reachkit.app`
  (`src/lib/mail/vendor/resend.ts`, held by `tests/mail/vendor/vendor.test.ts`).
- The mail head shows the mark from `https://reachkit.app/brand/mark-96.png` at 28 px (3× pixels),
  with width, height and alt. The mark is a white shape on its own violet square, so it reads on a
  light or a dark inbox (`tests/mail/shell/brand-head.test.tsx`).
- `https://reachkit.app/brand/mark-bimi.svg` is SVG Tiny PS and is served as `image/svg+xml`
  (`tests/app/seo/metadata.test.tsx`). It is generated by `node scripts/brand/render.mjs`. Never edit
  it by hand.

DNS for `reachkit.app` is on Cloudflare (`connie.ns.cloudflare.com`, `hasslo.ns.cloudflare.com`).

## (a) DMARC: from `p=none` to `p=reject`

Gmail shows no BIMI logo while DMARC is `p=none`. You need `p=quarantine` with no `pct` below 100,
or `p=reject`.

### Alignment today

| Record | Answer on 2026-10-19 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `_dmarc.reachkit.app TXT` | `"v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:hello@reachkit.app; fo=1"` | monitoring only; `adkim`/`aspf` default to relaxed |
| `resend._domainkey.reachkit.app TXT` | `"p=MIGfMA0GCSqGSIb3DQEBAQUAA4GNADCBiQKBgQDKfio0…AQAB"` | Resend signs with `d=reachkit.app`, so DKIM aligns with the From exactly |
| `send.reachkit.app TXT` | `"v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all"` | the Resend return path; a subdomain of the From's domain, so SPF aligns under relaxed mode |
| `send.reachkit.app MX` | `10 feedback-smtp.eu-west-1.amazonses.com.` | bounces return to Resend |
| `reachkit.app TXT` (SPF) | `"v=spf1 include:_spf.mx.cloudflare.net ~all"` | the root's own SPF. Resend mail does not use it |
| `reachkit.app MX` | `route1/2/3.mx.cloudflare.net` | inbound is Cloudflare Email Routing; it forwards and sends nothing as `@reachkit.app` |

Resend mail passes DMARC on both DKIM and SPF. Raising the policy only hurts a sender that is not
Resend and still writes `@reachkit.app` in its From.

### Steps

1. **Confirm on a real mail.** Open any ReachKit mail you already have, such as a sign-in link to
   your own address, and choose Gmail → ⋮ → *Show original*. Expect `SPF: PASS`, `DKIM: PASS` with
   domain `reachkit.app`, and `DMARC: PASS`.
2. **Read the aggregate reports.** They arrive at `hello@reachkit.app` (`rua`). Over at least 7
   days, every source that sends as `reachkit.app` should be Amazon SES (Resend) and pass. If a
   source fails, such as a Google Workspace or a Stripe custom email domain added later, fix its
   DKIM before going on.
3. **Quarantine.** In Cloudflare → `reachkit.app` → DNS, edit the `_dmarc` TXT to:
   `v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; pct=100; rua=mailto:hello@reachkit.app; fo=1`
   Check it with `dig +short _dmarc.reachkit.app TXT @1.1.1.1`. Leave it for 14 days and keep
   reading the reports.
4. **Reject.** Edit the same record to:
   `v=DMARC1; p=reject; rua=mailto:hello@reachkit.app; fo=1`
5. **Rollback** at any step: put `p=none` back. The change takes effect within the record's TTL.

## (b) The BIMI record

**Current answer:** `default._bimi.reachkit.app` has no TXT record. It returns the wildcard
`*.reachkit.app` CNAME `489b9c49fcf4e2e9.vercel-dns-016.com.`, which is the hosted-pages edge and
not a BIMI record. When you add an explicit TXT record at `default._bimi`, that name stops matching
the wildcard. The wildcard keeps working for every other subdomain.

Add this only after step (a)3 is live. In Cloudflare, add TXT, name `default._bimi`:

- Without a certificate (Yahoo, AOL and Fastmail may show the logo; Gmail and Apple Mail will not):
  `v=BIMI1; l=https://reachkit.app/brand/mark-bimi.svg; a=;`
- With a VMC or CMC (Gmail and Apple Mail): commit the CA's PEM chain as `public/brand/bimi.pem`
  in a pull request, then use:
  `v=BIMI1; l=https://reachkit.app/brand/mark-bimi.svg; a=https://reachkit.app/brand/bimi.pem`

Check it with `dig +short default._bimi.reachkit.app TXT @1.1.1.1`. You can also paste the domain
into a public BIMI inspector such as the BIMI Group's at bimigroup.org.

## (c) VMC or CMC, or the free fallback

Gmail draws a BIMI logo only when the record's `a=` names a mark certificate. Either certificate
also gives the blue verified check in Gmail (VMC) or the logo alone (CMC).

| | VMC (Verified Mark Certificate) | CMC (Common Mark Certificate) |
|---|---|---|
| Prerequisite | the logo as a **registered trademark** at an accepted office (USPTO, EUIPO, UKIPO and others); a pending application is not enough | the logo **in public use for 12 months**, shown with archived evidence such as Wayback Machine captures |
| Gmail | logo and blue check | logo, no check |
| Cost | about US$1,000–1,600 a year (DigiCert, Sectigo, GlobalSign, SSL.com; get a quote), plus the trademark filing | about US$1,000–1,500 a year from the same CAs |
| Lead time | trademark registration takes months; the certificate then takes days to weeks with identity checks | earliest about **September 2027**: the current mark has been public since #982 (2026-09-22) |

Both are paid, yearly owner decisions. Before you order, ask the CA whether it needs a CAA `issuevmc`
record. The current CAA set lists `digicert.com`, `sectigo.com`, `comodoca.com`, `ssl.com`,
`letsencrypt.org` and `pki.goog` under `issue` only.

**Free fallback: a Google account for the sending mailbox.** Gmail shows the profile photo of a
Google account whose address matches the sender, in some views and with no guarantee. It costs
nothing and needs no DNS:

1. Go to accounts.google.com/signup and choose **Use your existing email** (not a new Gmail
   address). Enter `hello@reachkit.app`.
2. Google mails a code to `hello@reachkit.app`. Cloudflare Email Routing forwards it to the inbox
   that address already reaches. Enter the code.
3. Profile picture: upload `public/brand/mark-512.png`, and set the photo to visible to **Anyone**
   (myaccount.google.com → Personal info → Profile picture, and *About me* visibility).
4. Turn on 2-Step Verification and keep the recovery details with the owner's other credentials.

If BIMI is in place later, Gmail shows the BIMI logo in place of this photo.

## (d) Stripe Checkout branding

Checkout, the customer portal, invoices and Stripe's receipt mails all use the account's Branding
settings. Open **Dashboard → Settings → Business → Branding**
(`https://dashboard.stripe.com/settings/branding`) in live mode, then repeat in test mode. The two
modes keep separate branding.

| Field | Value | From |
|---|---|---|
| Icon | `public/brand/mark-512.png` (512×512, 13 kB; Stripe wants a square of at least 128 px, under 512 kB) | `src/lib/brand/mark.ts` |
| Logo | `public/brand/logo@2x.png` (442×112, wide). Stripe shows the logo in place of the icon and name where it fits | `scripts/brand/render.mjs` |
| Brand colour | `#5b4be0` | `--color-primary` in `src/ui/theme.css`, also the mark's chip `MARK_CHIP` |
| Accent colour | `#5b4be0` | `--color-primary`, so Checkout's button matches the product's primary button |

Stripe previews both colours before you save. If the violet panel looks heavy, use `#f3f4fb`
(`--bg`) as the brand colour and keep `#5b4be0` as the accent. Under **Settings → Business → Public
details**, check that the public business name reads `ReachKit`, because Checkout shows it beside
the icon. Product and price setup is in `docs/stripe-setup.md`.

**By API?** You cannot set branding by API for our own account.
`POST /v1/accounts/{id}` with `settings[branding][icon|logo|primary_color|secondary_color]`, using
file ids from `POST /v1/files` with `purpose=business_icon` or `business_logo`, applies only to
**connected** accounts under Connect. The pinned SDK (`stripe` 22.6.1) has no per-session Checkout
branding parameter. Use the Dashboard.
