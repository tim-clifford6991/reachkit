What a day card may hold

  1. Top: the date, and exactly one number — searches a month before the page is live, its Google position and change once it is.
  2. Middle: the page title, cut to two lines with an ellipsis. Never a sentence, a reason or a rival’s name.
  3. Bottom: one stage word with its dot — Live, Review, Scheduled, Planned or Needs you — and a mark when the page is an update.
  4. A day with no page gets at most two words. The full reason lives in the day panel.
  5. The card is a fixed 104px tall at every width. Nothing wraps past it and nothing scrolls inside it.
