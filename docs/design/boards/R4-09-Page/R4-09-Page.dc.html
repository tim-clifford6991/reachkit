How much does a bike service cost in 2026?

What a basic, standard and full service cost this year, what each one includes, and how to tell which one your bike needs.

Published September 20, 2026 by Harbour Bike Repair · 4 minute read
The short answer

A basic bike service costs $60 to $100, a standard service $100 to $160, and a full overhaul $180 to $280 before parts.

Answer

A bike service costs between $60 and $280 in 2026, and the price follows how much of the bike is taken apart. A basic tune-up runs $60 to $100, a standard service $100 to $160, and a full overhaul $180 to $280 before parts. At Harbour Bike Repair a basic service is $85 and takes about two hours.

What each level of service includes

Detail

Shops use different names, but almost every price list comes down to the same three levels.

What changes the price

Detail

The type of bike. E-bikes, and bikes with hydraulic disc brakes or internal cable routing, take longer to work on. Expect the labor on those to sit at the top of each range.

Parts. A service price covers labor. Chains, brake pads, cables and tires are extra, and a good shop tells you what they’ll cost before fitting any of them.

“We quote the full price before we pick up a tool. If we find something else once the bike is on the stand, we call you first.”

How long it has been. A bike that’s serviced every year is quick to work on. One that has sat in a garage for three usually needs seized parts freed before the real work starts, and that time is billed.

How to tell which service you need

Steps
  1. Squeeze both brakes and push the bike forward. If a lever touches the handlebar or the bike still rolls, book at least a standard service.
  2. Shift through every gear while pedaling. Hesitation, or skipping when you push hard, usually means a stretched chain or cable.
  3. Lift each wheel and spin it. A wobble means the wheel needs truing. A grinding sound means bearings, and that’s a full service.
  4. Think back. If you can’t remember the last service, start with a standard and let the mechanic tell you what they find.

Is a service worth the money?

Detail

A chain costs about $30 to replace. Left too long, a worn chain wears down the cassette and chainrings with it, and replacing all three costs $150 or more. A yearly service catches the $30 problem before it becomes the $150 one.

Common questions

FAQ

How long does a bike service take?

A basic service takes about two hours. A full service takes one to two days, because parts are soaked and bearings are repacked.

Do I need to book ahead?

In spring, yes. From March to June most shops are booked a week out. The rest of the year, a day or two is usually enough.

How often should I service my bike?

Once a year for most riders. Every six months if you ride to work daily or through the winter.
