Connect your blog’s address

One DNS record points guides.harbourbikes.example at your blog. Your domain is with Cloudflare, so these are Cloudflare’s own steps. Your first page is being written while you do this.

  1. 1Open your Cloudflare DNS page and choose harbourbikes.example.
  2. 2Press Add record and enter the three values below.
  3. 3Set Proxy status to DNS only — the grey cloud. With the orange cloud on, your blog can’t be reached.
  4. 4Save, then check the connection here.
