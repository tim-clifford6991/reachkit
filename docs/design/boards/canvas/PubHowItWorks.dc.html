From everything buyers ask, we pick the questions that fit your business, that people really search for, and that a site your size can win. Each one becomes a page, or an update to one you have.

Show the detail

Each Monday ReachKit plans up to 7 pages for the week. They are new pages, or updates to pages already live, and each goes live on its own day.

A question is chosen first for how well it fits your business, then for how many people search for it, how clearly they mean to buy, and whether a site your size can win it. A question you already answer becomes an update to that page, and at most 4 pages a week are new.

Step 3 · We write your pages

Every page is true to your business

Pages are written with AI, but only from facts on your own site and sources they link to. Before a page goes live, it’s put through up to 22 checks. If it invents anything, or says something your site doesn’t back up, it’s held back.

  • Every fact about you links to the page it came from.
  • No invented people, reviews or numbers.
  • A page that fails twice is held, never published.
Show the detail

What a page is written from. Facts about you come from your own site, word for word, and each one links to the page it came from. Facts about the market come from the first three results Google shows for the question and from the pages of up to 4 competitors. A page links to at most 2 studies, and uses no figure older than 3 years. A new page has to add at least 3 things the top results do not say, and at least 1 of them is a fact about your business.

Up to 22 checks. Up to 21 are plain checks a computer runs; one is a judgement made by AI.

  • Every fact and every figure matches a source · 4 checks
  • No invented people, reviews, tests or origins · 4 checks
  • Comparisons are fair, and nothing you told us to leave out · 4 checks
  • No hidden text, placeholder or leftover example · 4 checks
  • The page answers the question first, and its title and headings hold up · 5 checks
  • It does not repeat a page you already have · 1 check

The last check is the AI judgement: whether the page says you serve a case your site does not support. A page that fails gets one rewrite. If it fails again it is held, and never goes live.

Links. Each page carries 3 to 6 links to other pages on your site, chosen by what the page is about. The words of a link say what you find at the other end. Every link is tried once before a page goes out: a link to a page of yours that is gone is taken out, and a source that is gone takes its claim with it. When a new page goes live, up to 2 of your earlier pages get one sentence that links to it.

Questions

Questions owners ask

Is this AI spam?

No. Pages are written with Claude, an AI model, but only from facts on your own site and from sources each page links to. Before a page goes live it is read against up to 22 checks: no invented people, reviews or numbers, and nothing your site doesn’t back up. A page that fails twice never goes live. We plan a page only where your market has a real question to answer, so some weeks have fewer than 7. Google’s own guidance is that using AI is fine and using it mainly to game search results is not. We write to answer your buyers.

Do I have to approve each page?

No. Each page goes live on its own 24 hours after it is written. In your first week we email each page to you first. After that, the Monday mail lists the week’s pages. You can read, edit or stop any page, or do nothing at all. You can set that window anywhere from 1 to 7 days.

How long until I see results?

In week 1 you see your pages go live on your site. From the first Monday you see where they sit in Google and whether AI answers link to them. After that, search engines move at their own pace. Bing usually finds a new page within days. Google takes from a few days to a few weeks, and doesn’t list every page. Showing when people search takes weeks. Reaching Google’s first page takes months, and not every page gets there. No one can guarantee a top spot on Google, and we never promise one.

What do I need to start?

A website you can connect. You pay first, with no account form, then spend about 10 minutes on setup. Connecting is one setting where your web address is managed, for a blog address such as blog.yourdomain.com (we show you what to copy), or your WordPress login. Your first page is written while you do it.

What if I want to stop?

Cancel any time from Settings, and you’re not charged again. Section four of our Terms says when your access ends. Pages on your WordPress stay where they are. Pages on the blog address we run for you stay live until your access ends, and we email you a week before. Your export of every page stays available, with no end date.

Or use Cancel your contract at the foot of any page.[after launch gate]

Will it work for a local business?

Yes. ReachKit reads what kind of business you are and checks the searches where your buyers are, including the ones with your town in them. Pages answer what local buyers ask: prices, what’s included, how long a job takes. One honest limit: for searches that show a map, Google lists businesses from Google Maps first, and your Google Maps listing (Business Profile) and reviews count most there. ReachKit’s pages help your website, not your Maps listing. In a small local market some weeks have fewer than 7 good pages to write, and we never fill a week with filler.

Will it work for software or online businesses?

Yes. We check the searches and AI questions your buyers ask online, wherever they are, in your language. Your pages answer what they ask before they choose a tool.

Does it work in German?

Yes. Sites in Germany, Austria, and Switzerland are measured in German and get pages in German. Sites in United States, United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, and Canada are measured and written in English. A site anywhere else is measured in the nearest of these countries, in English, and its report says so. This website and your dashboard are in English.

Why is there no free trial?

The scan and your first page are free, so you can see what ReachKit would do before you pay. After that there’s one plan at €49 a month, and you can cancel any time.
