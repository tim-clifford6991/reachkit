Step 1 · We research you

We get to know your business

First we read your website: what you sell, who buys, and where. Every page starts from this, so it says only what’s true.

Who buyers choose instead of you

We look up what your buyers search and ask AI, and see who gets chosen instead of you.

Step 2 · We pick what buyers ask

We pick questions you can win

We pick questions that fit you, get searched, and that you can win. Each becomes a new page, or an update to one you have.

Step 3 · We write your pages

Your pages go live every week

Every Monday we plan up to 7 pages for the week, new or updated. If nothing is worth writing, we skip the day.

Pages true to your business

Pages are written with AI, only from facts on your site and linked sources. Each faces up to 22 checks, and anything invented is held back.

Step 4 · You see it working

See it working from week 1

Every Monday an email shows what went live and what moved. Google moves slowly, so it also shows what comes next.

Your part: about 10 minutes, once

Check what we read about your business, then connect your website. After that you’re done, though you can edit or stop any page.

Questions owners ask

What if I want to stop?

Cancel any time from Settings, and you’re not charged again. Section four of our Terms says when your access ends. Pages on the blog address we run for you stay live for 30 days after your access ends, and we email you a week before they stop. Your export of every page stays available, with no end date.

Or use Cancel your contract at the foot of any page.

Will it work for my business?

Yes, if it has a website: a local service, a shop, a restaurant, a firm, software or a creator. We check what your buyers search and ask AI, in your language, with your town if you serve one. Our pages help your website, not your Google Maps listing.

Does it work in German?

Yes. Sites in Germany, Austria, and Switzerland are checked and written in German. Sites in United States, United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, and Canada, and everywhere else, get English.
