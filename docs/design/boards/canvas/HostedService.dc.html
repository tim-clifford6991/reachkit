Questions buyers ask

Is the call-out price the same at night?

Yes. The call-out is $149 at any hour, as listed on our pricing page.

What should I do while I wait?

Shut off the water at the main valve, open the lowest tap to drain the pipes, and switch off the water heater. Our guide How to shut off water to a house in an emergency shows each step.

Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. After finding the problem, the plumber gives you a written price. No work starts until you agree to it.

Do you cover my area?

We cover the areas listed above. If your town isn’t listed, call and ask.
