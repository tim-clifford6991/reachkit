Questions

Before you pay

What exactly do I pay?

€49 a month, VAT included, billed in euros, in advance, through Stripe. No setup fee and no minimum term.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. There is a 14-day money-back guarantee: ask within 14 days of your first payment and it’s refunded in full. After that, payments already made aren’t refunded, except where the law requires it. You can cancel any time, and you won’t be charged again.

[after launch gate]

Why is there no free trial?

The scan and your first page are free, so you can see what ReachKit would do before you pay. After that there’s one plan at €49 a month, and you can cancel any time.

What do I need to start?

A website you can connect. You pay first, with no account form, then spend about 10 minutes on setup. Connecting is one setting where your web address is managed, for a blog address such as blog.yourdomain.com (we show you what to copy), or your WordPress login. Your first page is written while you do it.

Do I have to approve each page?

No. Each page goes live on its own 24 hours after it is written. We email it to you first, and you can read it, edit it or stop it, or do nothing at all. You can set that window anywhere from 1 to 7 days.

How long until I see results?

In week 1 you see your pages go live on your site. From the first Monday you see where they sit in Google and whether AI answers link to them. After that, search engines move at their own pace. Bing usually finds a new page within days. Google takes from a few days to a few weeks, and doesn’t list every page. Showing when people search takes weeks. Reaching Google’s first page takes months, and not every page gets there. No one can guarantee a top spot on Google, and we never promise one.

What if I want to stop?

Cancel any time from Settings. You keep access until the end of the month you’ve paid for, and you’re not charged again. Pages on your WordPress stay where they are. Pages on the blog address we run for you stay live until your access ends, and we email you a week before. Your export of every page stays available, with no end date.

Or use Cancel your contract at the foot of any page.[after launch gate]

Will it work for a local business?

Yes. ReachKit reads what kind of business you are and checks the searches where your buyers are, including the ones with your town in them. Pages answer what local buyers ask: prices, what’s included, how long a job takes. One honest limit: for searches that show a map, Google lists businesses from Google Maps first, and your Google Maps listing (Business Profile) and reviews count most there. ReachKit’s pages help your website, not your Maps listing. In a small local market some weeks have fewer than 7 good pages to write, and we never fill a week with filler.

Will it work for software or online businesses?

Yes. We check the searches and AI questions your buyers ask online, wherever they are, in your language. Your pages answer what they ask before they choose a tool.
