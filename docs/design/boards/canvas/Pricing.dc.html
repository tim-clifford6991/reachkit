Questions

Before you pay

What exactly do I pay?

€49 a month, billed in euros in advance. No setup fee and no minimum term.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. There is a 14-day money-back guarantee: ask within 14 days of your first payment and it’s refunded in full. After that, payments already made aren’t refunded, except where the law requires it. You can cancel any time, and you won’t be charged again.

[after launch gate]

Why is there no free trial?

The scan and your first page are free, so you see the work first. Then it’s one plan at €49 a month.

What do I need to start?

A website and about 10 minutes. After you pay, copy one line to connect it, or log in with WordPress.

Do I have to approve each page?

No. Each page goes live by itself 24 hours after it is written. You can edit or stop it first, or do nothing.

How long until I see results?

Your pages go live in week 1. Each Monday email shows where they sit on Google, and whether AI links to them.

What if I want to stop?

Cancel any time from Settings, and you’re not charged again. Section four of our Terms says when your access ends. Pages on your WordPress stay where they are. Pages on the blog address we run for you stay live until your access ends, and we email you a week before. Your export of every page stays available, with no end date.

Or use Cancel your contract at the foot of any page.[after launch gate]

Will it work for a local business?

Yes, we track the searches local buyers make, including ones with your town. Our pages help your website, not your Google Maps listing.

Will it work for software or online businesses?

Yes. We check what your buyers search and ask AI, in your language. Your pages answer them before they pick a tool.
