Honest by design

The rules we keep

These hold for every page, for every business, in every language.

We never

  • invent people, reviews, tests or numbers
  • hide text on a page
  • promise a top spot on Google
  • put filler live

We always

  • Every figure on a page matches a source.
  • A page that fails its checks twice never goes live.
  • Something we couldn’t measure is shown as not measured, never as zero.
