Emergency plumber in Austin: what to do in the first hour

If water is spreading, shut off the main valve first. In most Austin homes it sits where the water line enters the house, often in the garage or near the front hose bib. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

1. Stop the water

If you can’t find the house valve, the city meter box at the curb has one too. You need a meter key for it, which most hardware stores sell.

2. Turn off the water heater

With the supply off, a gas heater should go to “pilot” and an electric one should be switched off at the breaker, so it doesn’t heat an empty tank.

3. Call, and say what you see

Tell the plumber where the water is coming from and whether it has reached outlets or the ceiling. Harbor Plumbing answers emergency calls in Austin around the clock.

4. While you wait

Move rugs and boxes away from the water, and take photos for your insurer before anything is moved.
