Questions owners ask

Is this AI spam?

No. Claude, an AI model, writes each page from your site and linked sources. A page that fails its checks twice never goes live.

Do I have to approve each page?

No. Each page goes live by itself 24 hours after it is written. You can edit or stop it first.

How long until I see results?

Your pages go live in week 1. Each Monday email shows where they sit on Google and whether AI links to them.

What if I want to stop?

Cancel any time in Settings; you’re not charged again. Your export of every page stays available.

Or use Cancel your contract at the foot of any page.

Will it work for my business?

Yes, if it has a website: a local service, a shop, a restaurant, a firm, software or a creator. We check what your buyers search and ask AI, in your language, with your town if you serve one. Our pages help your website, not your Google Maps listing.

What do I need to start?

A website and about 10 minutes. After you pay, copy one line to connect it, or log in with WordPress.

Does it work in German?

Yes. Sites in Germany, Austria and Switzerland are checked and written in German. Everywhere else gets English.

Why is there no free trial?

The scan and your first page are free, so you see the work first.
