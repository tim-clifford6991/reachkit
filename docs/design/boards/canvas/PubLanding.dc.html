Questions

Questions owners ask

Is this AI spam?

No. Pages are written with Claude, an AI model, but only from facts on your own site and from sources each page links to. Before a page goes live it is read against up to 22 checks: no invented people, reviews or numbers, and nothing your site doesn’t back up. A page that fails twice never goes live. We plan a page only where your market has a real question to answer, so some weeks have fewer than 7. Google’s own guidance is that using AI is fine and using it mainly to game Google’s results is not. We write to answer your buyers.

Do I have to approve each page?

No. Each page goes live on its own 24 hours after it is written. In your first week we email each page to you first. After that, the Monday mail lists the week’s pages. You can read, edit or stop any page, or do nothing at all. You can set that window anywhere from 1 to 7 days.

How long until I see results?

Your pages go live in week 1. Each Monday email shows where they sit on Google, and whether AI links to them.

See it week by week →

What if I want to stop?

Cancel any time from Settings, and you’re not charged again. Section four of our Terms says when your access ends. Pages on your WordPress stay where they are. Pages on the blog address we run for you stay live until your access ends, and we email you a week before. Your export of every page stays available, with no end date.

Or use Cancel your contract at the foot of any page.[after launch gate]

Will it work for a local business?

Yes. ReachKit reads what kind of business you are and measures the searches where your buyers are, including the ones with your town in them. Pages answer what local buyers ask: prices, what’s included, how long a job takes. One honest limit: for searches that show a map, Google lists businesses from Google Maps first, and your Google Maps listing (Business Profile) and reviews count most there. ReachKit’s pages help your website, not your Maps listing. In a small local market some weeks have fewer than 7 good pages to write, and we never fill a week with filler.

What do I need to start?

A website you can connect. You pay first, with no account form, then spend about 10 minutes on setup. Connecting is one setting where your web address is managed, for a blog address such as blog.yourdomain.com (we show you what to copy), or your WordPress login. Your first page is written while you do it.

Does it work in German?

Yes. Sites in Germany, Austria, and Switzerland are checked and written in German. Sites in United States, United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, and Canada, and everywhere else, get English.

Why is there no free trial?

The scan and your first page are free, so you see the work first. Then it’s one plan at €49 a month.
