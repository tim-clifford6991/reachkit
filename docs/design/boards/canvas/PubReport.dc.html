The complete method, free

How to do it all yourself, step by step
Let AI in

Open the robots.txt file at the root of your site; your host usually has a setting for it. Find the rules that turn away the readers AI answers are drawn from, and add the lines shown, so each one is let in. A page can also keep itself out of an answer: remove nosnippet or max-snippet:0 from its robots tag. If your home page carries a noindex rule, remove that too. Nothing else needs to change.

Write the missing pages

Each missing page answers one question your buyers ask, where competitors show up and you don’t. Answer the question plainly in the first paragraph, then back it with facts from your own site: numbers, dates, names, each linked to where it lives. Put it at a plain address on your own website and link to it from a page that already does well. A few pages a week is a good pace; one a week still helps. ReachKit writes these for subscribers, but nothing here needs a subscription.

Make pages AI can answer from

AI answers mention and link pages that answer a question directly. For each page that reads like a brochure: turn its headings into the questions a buyer would ask, put a direct answer of two or three sentences under each, and add evidence: a figure, a date, a source you link to. Keep the page’s address, so it keeps what it already does well.
