2026-10-20  **Each page's own search is read 100 deep, and the broad AI questions are asked once on each engine** (owner rulings of 2026-10-19, "all as recommended": B2 · M3, *"page results are read 100 deep; unbranded AI questions get 1 run per engine (+0.96¢ per site per week)"*, and D2, *"read each page at weeks 2, 4 and 8, with adaptive depth, at 0¢ added"*; issue 1612). (1) **The slots.** The page questions' slots go first to the pages due a reading, newest first: a page is due on its first Monday and in weeks 2, 4 and 8 after it went live. Then to pages last seen at 11–30, nearest the first page first; then to pages an AI answer to their own question linked; then to the rest. The cap of 24 and the 8-week retirement of #908 are unchanged, so the number of reads a week is unchanged. This replaces #908's "those whose pages rank nearest the first page". (2) **The depth.** A page's own Google result is read 100 deep until the page is seen, then 10 places past where it was last seen, never under 20, so a page on the first page is read shallow and page two is always in view. Only the first twelve asked slots (24 less the twelve) read this way; where a thin market selects fewer than twelve searches, the slots past them read 10 deep, and a page absent from such a result past place 10 is not measured, never zero. The vendor bills each 10 results as one page; the AI Overview surcharge is one base price per read whatever the depth. A page absent from a result read at least 20 deep holds no place in the top 20; absent from a 10-deep result stored before this, it is still not measured. (3) **The prompts.** Each engine asks the 23 unbranded tracked prompts once and the 2 branded prompts twice: 54 asks a week instead of 100. (4) **The page's question.** The engines are asked the page's own question, never its search, and a local business's with its town where the question names none ("… in Austin?"). The ledger's weekly change is +0.96¢ a site whatever the market selects: +6.48¢ for the twelve deeper reads, −5.52¢ for the 46 asks no longer made. Each page slot also asks ChatGPT and AI Mode (0.24¢), so a week is 17.28¢ with twelve page slots and at most 21.6¢ with 24. `CAPS.WEEKLY_C` (40¢) is unchanged. The numbers 11–30, 10 places and 20 are the SEO review's drafted defaults the owner may correct.
