2026-10-22  **A live page's Stop is one press, and a hosted page can be put back for 7 days** (owner ruling R9, 22 October 2026, *"all as recommended"*, #1749; Q6 of `2026-10-20-1716.md`). This amends `2026-10-18-1532.md` (1)–(4) as follows. (1) *Stop this page.* A live page's control is *Stop this page* (E7: Stop is Stop everywhere), on Plan's *Published* list and on the page's own screen (board `PageDetail`). The first press stops the page. There is no in-page question: the line under the button says what the press does (*"Stopping takes this page off your site and drops the topic. Tomorrow's page is not affected. To pause every page, use Settings › Publishing"*). *Take it down* and *Keep it live* are removed. (2) *The path* is unchanged: `unpublish()` with the customer as actor and the reason `take_down`, for every live version at the address, and no row is deleted (ADR-080). (3) *The topic is dropped for good* (Q6), as every topic the customer stops is: the topic is dismissed with `stopped` set, from `open`, `queued` or `done`, so the topic is never proposed again. This replaces the 90-day rest, and `TAKE_DOWN.TOPIC_REST_DAYS` is gone. (4) *Put it back* is shown only after a stop, only on a hosted page, and only for `TAKE_DOWN.PUT_BACK_DAYS` (7): *"You can put it back until {date}."* beside *Put it back*. It republishes the same publication rows at the same address, then answers *"It's back on your site."* The topic stays dropped. A stopped WordPress page says *"Your WordPress post was returned to draft. To bring it back, publish it in WordPress."* and offers no Put it back (ADR-084 Decision 1). After a stop the screen's badge reads *Stopped*, its controls leave, and *"Tomorrow's page goes live as planned."* stays. Every sentence is drafted (#759) for the owner to correct. The page's screen is board `PageDetail` (canvas V22, `2026-10-19-1581.md`), so its ladder is the board's two rows with dates (On Google: Live · Found by Google · Showing in results · First page; In AI answers: Read by AI · Linked by AI · Visit from AI) in place of `2026-10-19-1553-search-ladder.md` (5)'s six one-word steps. A step nothing has read says *not measured*. On WordPress, the steps only server logs can see are not drawn.

2026-10-22  **A live page's screen departs from board `PageDetail` in three places** (owner ruling R19, 22 October 2026, approving the recommendation on #1749). (a) *Edit this page* is not drawn on a live page for now: a published draft has no edit path (`isEditableDraft` refuses it, and ADR-080 forbids editing a publication in place). Editing a live page means a new version at the same address through the refresh path and the state machine; a follow-up issue builds that and brings the button back. (b) The visits line reads *"{n} visits from Google in the last 28 days. Read {date}."*, because visits are read over the stored 28-day window, not a week. (c) *What happens next* says *"If it starts losing places, it gets an update…"*, the `lost_position` refresh cause, in place of *"If it stops gaining for 3 weeks"*, which no rule implements.
