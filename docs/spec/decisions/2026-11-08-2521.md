2026-11-08  **A new site's first week is prepared up to its first Monday** (owner, C2, #2521): "prepare the remainder of the first week i.e. up until the monday deep scan. Prepare means the topic and the shape but not write the content until the day before". Setup writes the first page. Each date after it, up to the day before the first Monday weekly pass, gets a committed topic and shape. Each of those days is written the evening before its date. That Monday's pass commits a fresh week.
