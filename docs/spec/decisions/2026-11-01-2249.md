2026-11-01  **The landing's proof card counts the sites scanned from 100 up** (owner, #2249: *"show the count from N ≥ 100, counting distinct scanned domains"*). A site is one domain listed in `free_scan_listing`, counted once however often it was scanned. ReachKit's own domain and the panel's sites (`docs/walk/panel/sites.json`) are not counted. Below 100 the row is not drawn; from 100 it reads the count rounded down to a ten, as "120+ sites scanned · with the free scan". It says sites scanned, never customers.
