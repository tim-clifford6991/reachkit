2026-10-19  **Hosted pages count visits from Google and Bing search** (owner, D6, rulings of 19 Oct; #1614; trust-compliance light check of 20 Oct on #1614). A request to a live hosted page whose `Referer` host is exactly one of Google's search hosts (`google.<tld>` or `www.google.<tld>`, from Google's published domain list) or `bing.com` / `www.bing.com` is counted as `kind = referral`, `engine = google | bing`, by page and by UTC day. Only the count, the engine and the day are kept: never the host, the path, the query, the address or the agent string, and no cookie, script or per-visit event. This amends the 2026-09-20 line *Nothing else about a visit is kept* for search visits. A classified read that is not written logs one `blog_read_skipped` line carrying its reason alone (`unverified_crawler`, `ranges_unreadable`, `page_lookup_deadline`).
