2026-10-19  **The weekly pass commits the next seven pages and three spares; the daily job takes its slot** (owner, #1559: *"on our weekly refresh, we pull in all new tracking information we require including keywords, ai searches and rivals. this context together with the users business context is used to find the next 7 content generations to support their seo and geo growth. this should be true for saas business and equally local businesses ... simplicity is always king."* The numbers are drafted defaults the owner may correct.) (1) Each Monday, after the week's verdicts and refreshes, one readiness check runs over the open rows and the one ranked list (§7, unchanged) is committed as the site's week: its first `WEEK_PLAN.days` (7) rows one a day from the first date with no page, then `WEEK_PLAN.spares` (3) spares. The commit is stored on the rows (`plan_place`, `planned_for`, `plan_write_as`) and the calendar shows it; a new commit replaces the last. (2) The evening run writes the row committed for its date, after a 0¢ check on the week's rows alone (a rest, the destination, a page that now covers the topic). Where that page cannot be written (the hard rules, a short pack, a step that failed, or the row no longer stands), it takes the spare that best fits the day by the same order and mix rules; D26's "at most once more" is unchanged. It no longer re-assesses the whole pool or ranks it again. Out of spares, the day has no page and says so, as before; a later day's page is never taken. (3) A date no commit covers (a new site, a site before its first Monday, or a deep pass that changed what the site holds) is committed on demand by that evening's run, once, from the rows the site holds then. (4) One depth number, `PLAN_DEPTH` = 7 + 3: *Pages left*, the low-supply notices and the 0¢ refill count writable days up to it. It replaces `SUPPLY_TARGET_DEPTH` (30, BP-041 d4) and the 14-day floor and evening refill of the 1549 decision of the same day. The refill from stored "People also ask" and related searches runs inside a pass only. (5) Removed: the deep pass's `pursueDepth` and the weekly `topUp` (one path now, `deriveForPass`), the readiness run inside each of them over the whole pool, the second `assessFixPages` after derivation, the Monday readiness run before the digest, and the evening's `assessFixPages` and `assessReadiness` over every open row. Unchanged: the hard rules, D52's one-listicle-a-week cap, the 28-day rests, the veto window and the customer's edit and stop controls. (6) A local service's and a shop's commercial searches still stop at the keyword gate, so those sites may not fill a week yet; that is a follow-up (the strategy review's step 6), not this ruling.
