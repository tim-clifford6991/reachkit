2026-11-09  **Paid means paid through today, with no outstanding invoice** (owner C4, 2026-11-08, #2513, recorded for #2525: *"correct, fully paid means no outstanding invoices"*). This supersedes ADR-050's "`paid_through` alone" rule, REQ-076 c8 ("whether or not its latest renewal was paid"), the `users.plan_status` column comment that says no gate reads it, and, on `past_due` only, the deferral named in `2026-11-06-2448.md` (its refund rules stand). A site is paid for when its owner's `paid_through` is after now and the recorded `plan_status` is not `past_due`; Stripe's `past_due`, `unpaid`, `incomplete` and `incomplete_expired` are all recorded as `past_due`. While it holds, the weekly scan, the daily draft and publishing stop for that site, and they resume on the next tick after `invoice.paid` records `active`. A cancelled customer owes nothing and keeps the month they paid for. Hosted pages keep serving under their own rule (`paid_through` plus 30 days). An unreadable account is not paid. No retry, dunning or refund is added: Stripe's own retries and mails recover the payment.
