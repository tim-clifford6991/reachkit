2026-10-23  **The Monday mail reads as board `MondayMail`, and it carries its own stop controls** (owner 2026-10-20, *"follow all recommendations"*, Phase C parity; #1741; drafted). It opens on a greeting, *"Hi {name}, here's week {n} for {business}."*, where the name is the account's own, or no name where none is stored, n is Plan's week of service, and the business is the site's address. Under that it gives the calm line, *"Nothing needs you. Each page goes live at {time} on its day unless you stop it."*, using the site's publish time. The calm line is left out when a page needs the customer or the address still waits to be connected. Overview's numbers follow under *Google*, *AI* and, on the month's first Monday, *This month*. Next comes *This week's pages*: a Monday-to-Sunday strip, then the first three pages, each with its day, its search, *Why:* in Plan's words for the stored `plan_reason`, and *Stop this page · Edit*. The rest are counted and link to Plan. Then the pace line and *Open your overview*. A page is still named by its search and never by its model-written title (REQ-093); where the board draws a title, this line wins. The footer reads *"You get this one mail each Monday. Pause all pages · Mail settings · Unsubscribe"*.

*Unsubscribe* is the kind-scoped token of §4.7 (`/unsubscribe/{token}`). It switches off the Monday mail's toggle on arrival, and only that one. The mail also carries `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` for the same token, at `/api/unsubscribe/{token}`. The sign-in mail is never reachable from it (ADR-042). *Pause all pages* is a link signed over the site alone (`/pause/{token}`). It switches publishing off once, as Settings does, and takes no page transition (ADR-080). This supersedes, for the weekly mail, the 2026-09-18 line's *"the three togglable kinds carry no stop control today"*. The page-ready and first-page-live mails are unchanged.
