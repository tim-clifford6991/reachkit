2026-11-08  **Checkout asks for the card details and the email, and nothing else is required** (owner, #2526): "Most important for checkout is payment details and email. Nothing else should be required from us at that stage". The billing address is asked only where Stripe needs it (`billing_address_collection: "auto"`), there is no tax ID field or "purchasing as a business" tick, and no phone number or custom field. The billing country is recorded where Stripe gives one and is null otherwise; an account opens either way. It replaces "Country for records; VAT number optional".
