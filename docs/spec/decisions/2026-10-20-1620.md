2026-10-19  **A page is as long as the questions it answers** (owner, ruling B7 on SEO C6: *"page length follows the questions a page answers; maximum lengths kept"*; amends *Length and the writer*, 2026-09-29, for new pages). A new page's target is its fixed sections plus the pack's buyer questions, kept within the template's fewest and most sections, each given an equal share of the type's maximum. The maximums are unchanged, and a short answer's voice caps the page at its own maximum. A new page has no word floor: its depth is held by its sections, blocks, FAQ and sources, and only a page over its maximum fails the length check. An update keeps its range around the live page (within 20%, or its grown share). The cap per page, `CAPS.DRAFT_C`, is unchanged.
