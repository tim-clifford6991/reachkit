2026-11-24  **An interrupted write is stopped and its date freed like any stopped page** (owner, #2722 rulings 98.2 and 99.1; #3430). A `generating` row that no run is still writing is moved to `needs_attention` at 0¢, and its date is released for the day's next tick; it is no longer rewritten automatically. The customer's restart is the one path that rewrites the same row. The move-back of earlier automatic rewrites to their planned date ends. `lines.areas.src/lib/generate` and `lines.areas.src/lib/publish` in `scripts/budgets.json` fall to what is left.
