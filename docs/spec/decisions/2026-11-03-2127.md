2026-11-03  **Settings has three tabs: Your business · Pages · Account** (owner R7, 2026-10-31, *"Settings goes to 3 tabs (Your business · Pages · Account)"*, `owner-rulings-and-actions-2026-10-24-expert.md`; #2127). This amends the 2026-09-20 line in SPEC §4 (#908): "Settings is tabs: Your business, Market and rivals, Publishing, Your voice, Mail, Billing, Account." The danger zone still lives in Account.

| Tab | Address | Takes in |
|---|---|---|
| Your business | `/app/settings` | Your business, Market and competitors (`#market`), Your voice (`#voice`) |
| Pages | `/app/settings/pages` | Publishing (`#publishing`), the link map, Mail (`#mail`), the content we already know about |
| Account | `/app/settings/account` | Billing first (`#billing`), This month, Account, the shortcuts, the danger zone |

1. **Boards.** SettingsIndex is Your business, SettingsPublishing is Pages, SettingsAccount is Account. SettingsMarket, SettingsVoice, SettingsMail and SettingsBilling are retired. Their cards move into those three boards unchanged, with their designer notes.
2. **Old addresses.** `/app/settings/market`, `/voice`, `/publishing`, `/mail` and `/billing` answer a permanent redirect (308) to their new tab and anchor, so links in mail already sent keep landing.
3. **Words.** "Settings › Mail" and "Settings › Publishing" now read "Settings › Pages". "Settings › Billing" now reads "Settings › Account".
4. **Later (#2127 PR B).** The seven voice controls become one card, "How your pages sound". "Facts we can quote" leaves Your business for the page detail, as #2127 names. Both stay where they are on these boards until PR B redraws them.
