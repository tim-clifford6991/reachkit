2026-11-06  **A sign-in or email-change link opened in the browser that asked for it goes straight in; anywhere else the one button stays** (owner, #2445, relayed by the orchestrator: the magic link landed on *Sign in to ReachKit / Press the button to finish*, and the owner wants the link to take them straight into the app). Asking for a link (`/signin`, or a change of email that Settings accepted) sets the `__Host-rk_signin_pending` cookie for the link's lifetime (`SIGNIN_LINK_TTL_H`, `EMAIL_CHANGE_TTL_H`): the kind of link and its expiry, signed, with no address and no account. `/auth/confirm` opened with a valid cookie for that kind sends its own form, so the customer presses nothing; opened without one (another browser or device, a mail scanner, a link issued after checkout) it shows the button as before. Opening the page still spends nothing; only the post to `/auth/confirm/redeem`, from our own origin, spends the link, and a successful redeem clears the cookie. The dead-link answer is unchanged.
