2026-11-09  **A composed comparison names each rival in full, or is not composed** (owner, #2587; corrects reading (4) of #1314, 2026-10-07). A "{rival} alternatives" or "A vs B" search carries the rival's whole name as stored, else a whole name it is stored as searched under, at most four words and not a web address. A name is never cut to its first words or to the label of its address ("boot" for Boot.dev, "youcanbook" for YouCanBook.me); a rival with no whole name is left out of the comparisons, and the others stand. An open topic that already says such a cut label is a rival called by its domain (reading (3)) and is dismissed by the next pass, unless something was drafted from it.
