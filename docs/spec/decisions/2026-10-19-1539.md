2026-10-19  **An AI answer that misstates the business is shown on Overview, and the next page on that topic states the fact plainly** (issue 1539, from the SEO review of 2026-10-19, N6; each rule and every sentence are drafted defaults the owner may correct). Found: the stored answers to the tracked prompts were read for whether they mention or link the site, and nothing compared what they said about the business with what its own site says. (1) *What an answer states.* When a tracked-prompt answer is read, the price, count or country it gives the business is kept on that answer (`states`): only in a line or clause that mentions the business and names none of its rivals, as the answer wrote it, with at most 160 characters of the words around it. A figure that is one end of a range, has a bound before it ("<$100"), is a share or a year, and a count with no noun after it, are not kept. The `scan/rescore` job reads it again from the answers' stored text, so stored reports carry it at 0¢. (2) *What contradicts the site.* It is held against the knowledge base as it stands: a price in a currency the site prices in that none of its statements gives; a count of a thing the site counts once, higher than the site's (a lower figure may be an answer's "over 500" of 1,200); a country, where the site's own places name countries and never that one. A price in another currency is not compared. (3) *Overview.* Under the main-site link, while the newest pass's answers misstate anything, *What AI answers get wrong* (`overview.misstated.title`) says up to three: *An AI answer says {ai}. Your site says: {site}.* (`overview.misstated.row`), the answer's own words beside it, *Where your site says it* (`overview.misstated.source`) linking the page, and *Your next page on each of these topics states what your site says, plainly.* (`overview.misstated.next`). It is a finding, never a needs-you item. (4) *The next page.* A page whose target asks what something costs, or shares a word with the site's statement, carries the finding in its pack (`corrections`), and the one write call is told to state the site's fact in a sentence of its own and never to repeat what the answer said. No model call is added; everything here is a code read of stored rows.
