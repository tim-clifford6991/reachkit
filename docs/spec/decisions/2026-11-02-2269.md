2026-11-02  **The scan in progress and Free scans are built to canvas version 36, boards PubScanning and PubScans** (owner, #2269: *"approved, build it prioritising efficiency and speed of delivery. i need this live asap to start selling"*). Both boards are copied from the canvas into `docs/design/boards/canvas/` unchanged, except that PubScanning's one "Designer note:" box carries the `note` class, so parity reads it as the board's own note. PubScanning's specimen is Fernwood Tea (`fernwoodtea.example`, a shop), the business its scan becomes on PubReport; its strings are excused in `PubScanning.allow` as sample data. The real companies on Free scans are unchanged.

What the scan in progress now draws, as the board does:

1. **The site's own mark.** A 48px `SiteMark` sits left of "Scanning {domain}". It is the domain's letter tile from the first frame, and becomes the logo the scan kept as soon as the logo route answers: it is asked on the first frame and again as each stage finishes, so the screen never waits on it.
2. **The logo route also serves a scan that is running.** This extends the 2026-11-01 amendment of SPEC §2 2026-09-24 (#1058, #2267): `/scans/logo/{domain}` serves the stored logo of a domain whose report page is served or whose scan is running. A removed domain, or one with only a profile row, still gets 404.
3. **"What we read" leads with kind · industry** (REVIEW4 §2), e.g. "Shop · loose leaf tea", worded by the same helper as the report's first read-as chip. The market stage's summary carries the kind, and for a site with no town how far it sells and in which country, so the scan's where chip reads as the report's does ("Across United States").
4. **Under the waiting line, the report's lasting address in mono and an outline "Copy link" button** (REQ-001 c7), as the board draws them. The public header's own copy link is unchanged. A finished stage carries its time in mono beside "Done", and "What we read" draws its pages as a bar against the most a scan reads (`SITE_PROFILE.MAX_PAGES`).
5. **On a phone each stage row puts its status under its words**, the stages not reached yet included (the board keeps their "Next" at the right): a stage that finishes then keeps its row's height, so the list never jumps as the scan runs.

What Free scans now draws, as the board does:

1. **Every card has a 44px mark on a tint**: the stored logo in a padded frame, else the domain's letter. A stored logo that fails to load paints the same letter tile, drawn by the image's own `::after`, so it needs no script and never shows a broken-image glyph. `SiteIcon` folds into `SiteMark`. The gallery no longer reads a site's touch icon or favicon from the site itself, so `/` and `/scans` no longer open `img-src` to any `https:` origin; the "no logo found" badge goes with it.
2. **Each card's line opens with its kind's icon** (Tag where no kind was read) and drops an industry that only repeats the kind ("Software · Software" reads "Software").
3. **The six filter chips read the six `gallery.kind.*` words** (REVIEW4 §2) and are 44px, and "View report" is a 44px primary link on every card at every width.
4. **The strip's icon sits in a tile, and its filled and ringed dots are drawn the same size.**
5. **The close** keeps its compare rows, then one tinted box: "Your part: about 10 minutes, once." and "€49 a month. One plan. Cancel any time. See how it works →", then the field.

The designer's cuts, built as drawn: on the scan, the "What you'll get" card, which repeated the report's placeholders, and the sample caption, now a designer note; on Free scans, the chart caption shortened to "Visibility Score: how easy a business is to find, out of 100. {domain} is not scored.", the remove-report line (the public footer still names the removal address on every page, REQ-002 c1), the "Inside a report" and "After the report" eyebrows, the second lead sentence, the example caption, the 4-step figure, the close's lead line, its link to How it works and its report-at-a-glance card.

Where the build differs from the board:

- **At 390, the cards come first and the sticky toolbar keeps the six chips on one row that scrolls sideways** (SPEC §2 2026-10-10, issue 1377: on a phone a card is on screen on load and while scrolling). The board puts the scan form and the chart before the reports, and draws the filters as a wrapping, labelled block with a Sort select. Asked of the owner on #2269 (2026-11-02); built as 1377 until answered.
- **The strip's dots keep the context series colour** (BUILD §2.4: two chart colours only, and status colours are never a series). The board draws findable green and hard to find orange.
- **"Sells online, across the US" ships as "Across United States"**, the report's own where chip (SPEC §2, issue 1454), as on PubReport.
- **Titles of more than six words keep their shorter form** (SPEC 2026-10-15, #1444): "The 12 newest, by Visibility Score" (the board's "The 12 newest reports, by Visibility Score") and "Reports look once. ReachKit keeps going.", as before.
- **The competitors card stays grey until the report arrives** (owner R27, 2026-10-23), and the twelve searches' card waits on its stage, as before.
