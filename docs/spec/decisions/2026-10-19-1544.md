2026-10-19  **A question another page of the site targets is that page's to answer** (issue 1544, from the SEO review of 2026-10-19, N2; the readings are drafted defaults the owner may correct). Since #1519 a "People also ask" question or a related search can be a page's own target. A new page's buyer questions (D61, #1390) leave out every question that another page of the site targets: a live page, a page written or being written, or a topic the ranking plans next (§7's *every page the site has published or planned*, D36), the page itself aside. A target is reserved as it is worded and as the question it asks (`templateQuestion`, in the site's language), and a related search is also matched by its search, all compared in lower case with punctuation set aside. The question is dropped, not kept with a link: the page that targets it answers it in full, and a short repeat is the near-duplicate #1509 forbids. A Q&A left with fewer than five questions is short as before (D26). Packs already stored are not rewritten: a pack is what a page was written from. 0¢. It adds no sentence to any screen.
