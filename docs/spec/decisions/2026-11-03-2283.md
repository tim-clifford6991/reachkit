2026-11-03  **The hosted pages carry no sample pills and fit the phone** (owner, 2026-11-01, canvas v36: *"approved, build it prioritising efficiency and speed of delivery. i need this live asap to start selling"*; #2283). Boards HostedIndex, HostedArticle, HostedService and HostedGone are copied from the canvas into `docs/design/boards/canvas/`. Every Designer-note box also carries `note`, so the parity reading skips it.

The designer's decisions in `PAGES-PUBLIC-CHANGES.md` are built as drawn:

- **Notes.** The long design notes became short designer notes: three on HostedArticle, six on HostedService, three on HostedIndex and three on HostedGone. HostedService's seven E10 "only with its facts" notes are one ordered rule. The full rule text is kept word for word in `docs/design/hosted-rules.md`, and each board's first note points there.
- **Pills.** Every "Sample …" pill is gone. The product never drew one. The article and service bodies are unchanged.
- **Phone.**
  - The logo no longer squashes. It is `SiteMark` at 36px in the header and 48px in the business block, and it never shrinks.
  - The step cards are rows. The hosted steps already draw the number beside the text at every width, so the stored body's classes do not change.
  - On a phone the header gives the name its own row, with Call and Visit wrapping under it, as drawn. From `sm` it is one row, with each button capped at 16rem.
  - The header, Visit, breadcrumb, contents, business-block, footer and 410 links are 44px targets, and so is each guide title on the index.

After the ux-designer's 390 review (`ux-review-2322.md`, 2026-11-03), built:

- **One solid button.** The primary action (Call, else the book or quote page) is the only solid button, in the page's own ink (`base-content`), never the product's colour. Visit, the search button, the pager and the 410's "Browse all guides" are outlined. Book a visit and Ask for a quote keep the link underline.
- **Visit names the main site's host**, e.g. "Visit harborplumbing.com", as the board draws it. The compact block carries Call and Visit (E8).
- **Footer.** Only the domain is mono and underlined. The name and RSS are plain text.
- **Index.** "Search the guides" is a visible label. Each card states its date above its title. Guides outside a topic sit in an "Other guides" card.
- **Service page.** What is included, the price and where are each a card, the stored body split around them and never edited. There is no contents list and no compact block: the full block under the answer and at the foot is the service page's ask (R1).

Exceptions, and why each holds:

1. **The pointer names `docs/design/hosted-rules.md`**, not the board itself. The v36 notes point at the repo's board file, but that file is now the v36 board, which no longer holds the rule text.
2. **The letter tile takes no product colour.** On a customer's domain `SiteMark` picks between the theme's success and warning tints only, never primary. SPEC §7 (2026-09-29) says nothing on a hosted page names ReachKit, and the hosted suite holds that no class resolves to the accent. The board's specimen tile is the specimen's own blue.
3. **The letter is the name's, and the logo comes from the customer's host.** SPEC §7 (2026-10-04) says the logo is served by `logo.img` on the customer's own host, else the name's first letter. So `SiteMark` takes `src="/logo.img"` and the name's letter here, never the report's logo address or the domain's letter.
4. **An article's contents stay where SPEC §7 puts them.** The review asked for an open list at the end. SPEC §7 (2026-09-29) rules a table of contents "sticky beside the article from `lg` up and a collapsible block below it", and the board's own R5 note says the same. So it stays a collapsible block under the short answer below `lg`.
5. **The price is not made larger.** The board's large figure is a sentence of the stored body, and its words and markup are the page's own.
