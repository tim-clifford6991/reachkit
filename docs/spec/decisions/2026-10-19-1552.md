2026-10-19  **A comparison page names the business in its title, and a rival-only "vs" needs demand ReachKit did not compose** (owner, #1552: *"some of the scheduled pages are competitor vs competitor and do not, at least in the title, talk about reachkit"*; from `overview-supply-rivals-2026-10-19.md` §3; it amends D50 and the 2026-10-18 decision of issue 1520 (2); each reading here is a drafted default the owner may correct). (1) D50's "the page's title **may** add the business as the third name" becomes **does**: a page whose search sets products against each other or asks for one's alternatives — a "vs" or "alternatives" search, in any supported language, that names a rival or a product the pack holds — names the business in its title, by its name or its domain's label, whatever the page's type. The title check (§7, D51) fails it as `business`, the picker prefers a title that holds it, and the writer and the one fix call are told so. A search that names the business already passes as it is; a comparison of category terms ("aeo vs geo") names no product and is not held to it. (2) A "vs" between products, neither of them the business, is not observed by a tracked prompt or its measured answer: every tracked prompt is ReachKit's own wording, and its comparison prompts set the top rivals against each other by template, so the evidence is circular. Such a "vs" is ready only on its own measured volume above zero, on a "People also ask" question or related search on a stored SERP (issue 1520's other arms), or on the AI answer the row was found in. A "vs" the business is a side of still counts a prompt whose measured answer names every side. Found on reachkit.app: "profound vs peec ai", "profound vs otterly" and "peec ai vs otterly" each measure 0 searches and were ready only through prompts such as "Peec AI or Otterly: which is the better choice…". Nothing is bought: 0¢. Stored readiness is corrected by the next evening's reassessment, and live pages by issue 1506's hourly recheck. Condition 3 of #1552 — own-offering and buyer-question pages before any rival comparison on a new site — amends D50 further and waits for the owner.
