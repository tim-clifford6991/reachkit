2026-11-02  **Setup is three short steps with the action always on screen, built to canvas v36** (owner, #2270: *"approved, build it prioritising efficiency and speed of delivery. i need this live asap to start selling"*). Boards Setup1, Setup2, Setup and SetupZone in `docs/design/boards/canvas/` are the v36 files. Each one is unchanged except that its designer-note box also carries `note`, so the parity check skips it. The specimen is Tallyroom, the software business, and its city is left empty because it sells online. These designer decisions are built as drawn:

1. **Every step** has a white header band with the brand, the three steps drawn as bars over their one-word labels, and the pass's status line where one is running. The question is one reading column, with no side panel: "What happens next" and the blog preview are gone. The action sits in a bar pinned to the foot of the screen, Back on the left from step 2 on, with no line under it. "Step n of 3" is gone, because the bar shows it.
2. **Site** keeps "About 10 minutes, once" on step 1 only. It cuts "What happens next", the cancel line, the place preview and the "The site your free report checked…" line. Its labels read "Where your buyers are" and "City or area you serve". The site's own mark (`SiteMark`, 36) stands beside its domain. It is the logo the scan kept, read with the profile setup already loads, or else the domain's first letter.
3. **Details** has one line, "{domain} · {n} pages read", in place of the diagram, again with the site's mark. It cuts the step line's first sentence, the "read" and "optional" pills, "What happens next" and the cancel line. The four sections and "We never invent prices, people or reviews…" stay.
4. **Connect is one screen, the third step** (orchestrator, 2026-11-02, on the ux review of #2301: the issue promises three short steps, and the board draws the picker and the record together). It holds the status line with its start ("Writing your first page · started 10:42 AM"), "Where should pages live?" as daisyUI radios, then for the hosted address the record to paste, "Do this later" and the week after. The record's Name row is the subdomain label field, so the founder still chooses it here (SPEC §5, 2026-09-12). Choosing WordPress replaces the record with what connecting it will ask for. The bar holds Back and the step's one primary. For the hosted address that is "I've added it, check now", which saves the step and then checks; "Do this later" saves it and goes into the app. For WordPress it is Continue. "Finish" is gone. This supersedes the 2026-09-20 ruling (#924) that the record is its own step after setup. `/setup/connect` stays as the way back to the record from Overview and Settings until the address is live. It also cuts the eyebrow ("One last thing · about 5 minutes"), the 7-day strip and the separate cancel line, which is now the fourth tick: "Cancel or export in Settings at any time." "Do this later" reads "We'll email you a reminder. Your first page waits for it." The WordPress option's line is "You create an app password for us." The DNS rows, the four "Show the steps" instructions and the application-password help are unchanged.
5. **SetupZone** is the heading and one status card with the spinner: "So every date shows in your time. A few seconds, once. Nothing to press." The "Why it matters" figure is cut. The 10-second line is unchanged. The spinner is drawn as the board draws it (orchestrator, 2026-11-02). DESIGN › Busy and done (owner, #1196, E42) now names this card as the one place a spinner stands outside a pressed button, because the screen itself is the wait and has nothing to press. The owner may still reverse this.

Where the build differs from the board:

1. **`/setup/connect`, the way back later, draws no picker, no Back and no status line.** Setup is finished by then, so there is no destination to change there (Settings › Publishing is where it changes), Back would land on `/app`, and the page reads no pass.
2. **The site's mark is 36px**, as the issue states. The board draws 28px on Site and 24px on Details.
