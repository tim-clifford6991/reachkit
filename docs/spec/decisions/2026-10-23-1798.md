2026-10-23  **The Google bar counts what the Google card counts** (owner R10, 2026-10-22, *"all as recommended"*, Arm A, recorded in the owner's rulings file of 2026-10-19; #1798). SearchPresence is `100 × first-page count / measured searches`, over the same SERPs the presence card counts, with a place in a search's local pack counted as first page in both (D67, #1441). Where no search was measured, or there is no card, SearchPresence is not measured and is never 0; #1439's mid-scale rule and range then apply unchanged. The Labs reach, `min(100, 25 × log10(ranked + 1)) × (0.55 + 0.45 × min(1, 4 × top10share))`, no longer enters the score; the ranked count and rows are still bought, stored and used for the rival bands and winnability. Stored scans are re-scored at 0¢ from their stored card and SERPs through `scan/rescore`; a report with no stored card or measured search keeps its stored value until its next scan. Arm B (an unbiased top-ten sample) is not built. It defines §0's Google half (`SearchPresence`); the `Score`, `Presence`, AIPresence, band and range formulas are unchanged.
