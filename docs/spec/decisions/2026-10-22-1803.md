2026-10-22  **A DataForSEO refusal for credit is named, is never billed, and stops the day's page; the owner is told at 14 days of credit** (operations finding F4 of 2026-10-22; owner ruling R16 of 2026-10-22, which keeps this alert as the backstop to auto-recharge; #1803). (1) `VENDOR_LOW.runwayDays` is 14; `floorC` stays 1,000¢. (2) HTTP 402, and a 40200 or 40210 on the task or at the top level with no task, are one funds refusal (`isFundsRefusal`). Each is ledgered at 0¢ and marked `funds` on its row, and each cost context that meets one logs `vendor_funds_refused` once. `50001` stays an ordinary vendor failure. (3) A day whose research the vendor refused writes no page: the day ends `generate:vendor_funds`, which `draft_days` keeps and the missing-page mail carries. (4) The weekly trace, with every DataForSEO call refused: `weekly/refresh` → `startWeeklyScan` → `runWeekly` → `runScan` (tier `weekly`) → each stage's vendor call → `ledgered` → `callEndpoint`. Each refused call is `unmeasured / undeterminable`, never 0. Before this change, a top-level refusal was `unparseable` and ledgered at the reserve (4.8¢ a call in the Monday journey). The pass ends `degraded` and names `market`, `rankings`, `ai_answers` and `rivals` unmeasured. It does not fail hard. `runWeekly` keeps the week's row, so the week counts as measured. `tellWeek` then judges it and plans its pages, and the job ends `degraded` with that list. Two things stop the week from staying due: `dueSites` selects a site only in its Monday due hour, and `releaseWeek` cannot delete a row that `fetches` rows reference. So "stays due, ends `vendor_funds`, chooses no page" is #1824, pinned by a skipped case in `tests/journeys/06-monday.test.ts`.
