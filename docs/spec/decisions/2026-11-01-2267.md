What the report now draws, as the board does:

1. **The site's own mark.** A 56px mark sits left of the domain h1. It is the logo the scan kept (SPEC §2 2026-09-24, #1069), else the domain's first letter on one of the theme's three tints (primary, success, warning), picked from the domain; the other daisyUI slots are not declared in `theme.css` and drew grey. It is drawn by `src/ui/SiteMark.tsx`, the one mark the landing, the gallery, the app shell and setup share.
2. **The logo route serves every public report.** This amends SPEC §2 2026-09-24 (#1058): `/scans/logo/{domain}` serves the stored logo of any domain whose report page is served, whether the gallery lists it or not. A removed domain, or one never scanned, still gets 404. A served logo is kept for a day by browsers and the CDN, so a busy report reads the stored bytes once a day, not once per view. After a takedown, a copy already cached can stay visible for up to that day.
3. **"We read you as" leads with the kind of business, then its industry** (REVIEW4 §2), e.g. "Shop · loose leaf tea". The kind is written in the singular the board draws: Local service, Shop, Restaurant or venue, Agency or firm, Software, Creator or course. These are the singular forms of the Free scans filter's words. The plural group labels ("Shops") name a list, not one business.
4. **"Not your market? Correct it"** is one 44px link button that opens the same correction.
5. **Where it sells is a chip, with MapPin** as the board draws it, a town or a country alike (DESIGN E29: the board's choice wins). A reach nothing stored decides draws no chip and no line; the "Not found yet. Searched in …" line went with *What we read*.
