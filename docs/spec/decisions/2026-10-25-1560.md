2026-10-25  **The weekly pass keeps the knowledge base current** (owner strategy of 2026-10-19: *"the weekly refresh pulls in all new tracking information … and the business context"*; #1560's condition 1). Each weekly pass reads the knowledge base again from the pages its own crawl just read, by the rules of *How the knowledge base is read* (2026-10-03). A page whose text is unchanged since the last read keeps its stored reading and costs nothing. Only a changed or new page is asked about, one small-tier call each. The read runs after the topic typing and the monthly competitor re-check, so it spends what they leave of the weekly 40¢ cap and never the reserve held for the typing. It stops when the cap or its own 15¢ is spent, and a changed page it did not reach is left out of the base until a later week reads it. This replaces the line "The weekly pass does not re-read" of 2026-10-03.
