Wording and drawing exceptions, and why each holds:

1. **"One page didn’t pass our checks…"** stays where it applies. The v36 specimen does not draw a set-aside week. The line is owner O7 (#2164), and the cut list does not name it.
2. **Sizes** come from the mail's token ladder (`src/lib/mail/shell/tokens.ts`): 12px eyebrows, 13px small lines, 15px body. The board's 14px and 16px have no rung there.
3. **Light only.** The mail has no dark scheme: `tokens.ts` rules a half-switched dark mail worse than none. The buttons are text and a border with no background image, so an inbox that forces dark inverts them whole.
