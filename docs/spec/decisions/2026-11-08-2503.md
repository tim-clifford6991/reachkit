2026-11-08  **A day whose own page and spare end without one takes the writer's update of the site's oldest live page, committed or not** (owner, #2503: "I need to be absolutely sure that daily content is planned, generated, and goes live for each day"; the spend is the owner's "yes" under the 2026-11-05 "no exceptions" ruling; amends the writer half of the 2026-11-06 ruling from #2388). Decision: after the committed week's 0¢ mend, a day that ended without a page for any `generate:*` reason (not `no_site` or `no_scan`), at select or after a page was written and stopped, takes a writer-made refresh from stored research only (no DataForSEO) of the site's own live page that is longest past `REFRESH_MIN_AGE_DAYS`, whether or not the week committed it. It uses that page's open refresh row, else plans one and assesses its readiness as any refresh is; a page the customer edited, took down or stopped a refresh of is passed over, as is a row that rests or is not ready. It runs in the day's `CAP_DRAFT`, opened at what the date has already spent, and only where what is left pays for a write and its one fix: about 15–17¢ on a day that would otherwise publish nothing, inside DRAFT_C 45. A row the rules stopped gives up its date to the update, as to a spare. The update enters review only where today's whole battery passes it, and there is still one draft a date. With no such page the day ends `no_page` with its own reason and `;no_update_candidate` after it. The 30-day floor stands.
