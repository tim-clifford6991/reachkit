2026-10-23  **A home page that was not read, on an address that names no country, is measured in D66's default and marked as guessed** (owner via product manager, #1797). The report says it guessed and offers the one "Not your market?" correction. A guess is never stored as a site's market. Where the site's own Google listing, the ranked rows the pass already bought, names one supported country three to one over any other in at least three rows, that country is the reading: the report names it, and a paying site stores it for its next pass, at 0¢. D66's US/en for a read page that says nothing is unchanged.
