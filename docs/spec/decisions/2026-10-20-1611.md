2026-10-20  **Every Monday a page's own results decide what happens to it next** (owner rulings of 2026-10-19, "all as recommended": B3 · M6 and M-a, the metrics model's Monday table; issue 1611). (1) The `close` refresh covers a page's own place 4–30 for its own search (was 4–20) and waits until its last 3 weekly readings hold no place better than the first of them (`GAP_REFRESH`), with §6's 30-day floor unchanged. 2026-10-19-1521's gaps and growth still apply only at 11–20. (2) A climbing page, a gain of 10 places since first seen with its best place inside the 8-week window (the verdict's own `climbing`), gets no `close`, `lost_position` or `unmet` refresh. (3) A page not moving (D60's `unmet`) is refreshed for it once. If a later version is still `unmet`, its question is retired: its results plan no further `close`, `lost_position` or `unmet` refresh. The page stays live and linked, and is never deleted or noindexed (ADR-080). A refresh for a fact, a price, the site's own figures, a link or its structure still applies. (4) 2026-10-20-1610's `linked_by_ai` part also goes to one new page in each cluster that holds a page a tracked AI answer linked in the last four weeks: the cluster's best open Write row by points. (5) A committed row these results placed stores that action as `plan_reason.rule` in place of the tier: `stalled near page one: gap refresh`, `not moving: refreshed once`, `linked by AI: one more in its cluster`. The numbers are drafted defaults the owner may correct.
