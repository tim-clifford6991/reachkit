2026-11-08  **Every SPEC section is rewritten** (owner C7, #2527, #2513: "All SPEC section to be rewritten"). This supersedes the 2026-10-18 rule (#1525) that the dated lines already in `docs/SPEC.md` stay where they are. A section's rewrite folds its dated lines and its decision files into the section, and the decision file that records it lists the files it supersedes on a `replaces:` line. Each section's rules sit in one `name | block/warn | outcome protected | ruling` table. Only the owner makes a rule `block`, and `blockRules` in `scripts/budgets.json` holds each section's count.
