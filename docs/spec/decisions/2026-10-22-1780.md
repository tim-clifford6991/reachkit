2026-10-22  **A monthly competitor re-check the cap refused stays due until one concludes** (owner ruling B5 of 2026-10-19, *"re-inferred monthly"*; architect decision on #1719 of 2026-10-22; #1780). Amends point (2) of 2026-10-20 (#1700). (1) A weekly pass re-checks the competitors when no re-check has concluded yet in the site-local month its week starts in, not only on the month's first Monday. Concluded means the "unchanged" skip, a founder's own competitors re-weighed, or a settled conclusion. A re-check the cap refused, one whose answer did not parse, and one that failed leave it due for the next weekly pass. The day it concluded is `rivalsCheckedAt` in `sites.market`, and a deep pass that concludes one sets it too. A weekly pass concludes at most one a month. (2) The competitors' model call is asked once, not twice (`INFERENCE_ATTEMPTS`): it runs at temperature 0, so a second ask gives the same answer. This halves what it reserves, so in a usual full week it fits in what the typing leaves. The weekly cap, `TYPING_RESERVE_C` and the order (typing first) are unchanged.
