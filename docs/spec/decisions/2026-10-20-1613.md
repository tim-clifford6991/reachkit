2026-10-20  **Each page is labelled from its own reading: Working, Climbing, Too early or Not moving** (owner rulings of 2026-10-19, B1 · M4 and M-a; issue 1613). The label is a pure function of the page's stored reading (`publications.search_reading`) and the week's date. *Working*: this week's place is 20 or better, or its best place is 20 or better and under 8 weeks old (a week its search went unread), or the answer to its own question links it; an old best alone never keeps a page working (orchestrator ruling 2026-10-20, per M-a). *Climbing*: its best place is 10 or more places better than its first, and that best is under 8 weeks old. *Not moving*: its best place is 8 weeks old or older, and no answer links it. *Too early*: anything else — under 8 weeks, a gain short of 10 places, or a page no result has ever held (#1538's unseen fix runs instead). Working and Climbing are stored as `working`, Too early as `too_early`, Not moving as `not_working`, so a cluster is suppressed and an Improve retired only by a page that is not moving. A tracked prompt's answers citing the page also make it working (D60). A gate or site-check page is working when its check passes, and is otherwise labelled from its reading. A page with no reading writes no row that week. The window is 8 weeks (`TOO_EARLY_WEEKS`), up from 3.
