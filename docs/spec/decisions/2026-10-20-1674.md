2026-10-20  **The Monday mail's stop link per page** (orchestrator, #1674, split from #1593 Condition 1: *"the link shows the planned page and stops it; written pages keep their current links"*, *"the stop must use the existing veto/skip seam"*). Each of the week's committed pages is a row with its day, its search and a signed *Stop this page* link to `/veto/{token}`. A page not yet written has no draft, so its link is signed over the site, the page and its day and stores nothing; it runs out after that day. Arriving shows the planned page and writes nothing. Pressing stops it the way stopping its draft does (the topic is dismissed), and that day then takes the week's next spare. No draft moves, so the publishing state machine is not reached. A page written since the mail is stopped from Plan or its own page-ready link, and the link says so. Dates in the committed week with no page planned are counted in one line. The rows are named by their search, not a title (REQ-093).
