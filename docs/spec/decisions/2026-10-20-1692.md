2026-10-20  **Settings changes the country and language, and the crawl keeps the measured market's section** (owner, "follow all recommendations", on the seo-master multi-market ruling on #1689, conditions C4 and C5; amends D66's "setup asks once"). (1) Settings › Market and competitors offers setup's question with setup's list, the pair measured and the markets the site's home page declares (`alsoServes`) first. It is optional and never asked. A pair off the list is refused and writes nothing. A changed pair is stored on the site and scanned again at once, under the same daily bound and refusals as a category change; the same pair starts nothing. Every series breaks at the first pass measured in the new pair, with its own account line, as a domain or category change does; published pages are not touched. The "also serves" line in the app says "Change it in Settings". (2) On a site whose home page shows it splits its markets by path (a same-host hreflang alternate in a locale path; links alone never split a site) or by subdomain (a host its hreflang gives only to other markets), the crawl reads the measured market's section and the unprefixed pages, and leaves out only the other markets' paths and hosts its hreflang declares; a site that shows no split loses no page. Cost is unchanged: no new call. A place (D67) stored under the old country is cleared when the country changes, and the next pass decides it again (2026-10-23-1767).
