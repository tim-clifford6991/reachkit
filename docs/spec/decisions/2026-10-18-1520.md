2026-10-18  **A comparison, alternatives or composed search is a topic only on observed demand** (owner on issue 1520: *"follow seo master"*, from the SEO master's gap review of 2026-10-18, B3; it amends D50 and the reading of issue 1506 (1); every other reading here is a drafted default the owner may correct). Found on the customer panel: four or five "{rival} alternatives" rows per local or hospitality site, every one unmeasured and ready — a rival's name there is what buyers type to reach that rival — and on wesbos.com four of thirteen ready topics that were ReachKit's own composed words ("javascript tutorial pricing"). A tracked prompt let every "vs" with a top-three rival through, because the prompt templates write those rivals in. Observed demand is any one of: its own measured volume above zero; a "People also ask" question holding every word of it, or a related search, on a SERP the site stored in the last 30 days (D62's store; its own SERP does not count); or a measured answer, as follows. (1) *"{rival} alternatives"* (German "{rival} alternative") is ready only on observed demand, the measured answer being one to a tracked prompt, in the newest report, that named or cited the rival and cited an address holding "alternative". The answer's addresses are recorded on each stored answer from now on (`alternatives`); titles are not stored, so the address alone is read, and an answer stored before holds none. (2) *A "vs" between products* (issue 1506 (1)) counts a tracked prompt only where the prompt names one of its rivals and a measured answer to that prompt names every side — the business by name or link, each rival by name or citation. A prompt that names one rival no longer suffices. A side that is not a rival, a product a pass only read a brand volume for, is one no answer can attest. The AI answer the row was found in still counts as before. (3) *A search ReachKit composed* — the category or a synonym for its buyer or with its price, the category against what it is not, the job as a how (`composedSearches`, in English and German) — with no measured volume needs the SERP evidence; otherwise it is `no_intent`. A row is composed where its evidence carries `composed` (from now on), or where its words, a plural read as its singular, are one of the market in force's composed searches in any supported language. Only a Write row is judged so. (4) *A business whose market serves one place* is never proposed "{rival} alternatives" (D50's "for each searched rival") unless one of these is on hand when the pass derives: the volume the pass read for that search, the SERPs, or the report's measured answers. A business that sells everywhere is still proposed it, and (1) judges it. (5) A topic buyers were seen asking (issue 1519) is observed, so none of this holds it. Nothing is bought: stored SERPs and answers only, 0¢. A held topic leaves its day to the next ready one (D26). Live pages are read by issue 1506's hourly recheck against these rules as they stand. The panel check *a ready alternatives or composed search was seen asked* runs in `npm run panel`. It adds no sentence to any screen.
