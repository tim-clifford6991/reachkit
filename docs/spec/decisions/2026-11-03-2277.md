2026-11-03  **Publishing, Mail, Billing and Account each read in one short card, built to canvas v36** (owner, #2277: *"approved, build it prioritising efficiency and speed of delivery. i need this live asap to start selling"*). Boards SettingsPublishing, SettingsMail, SettingsBilling and SettingsAccount in `docs/design/boards/canvas/` are the v36 files, unchanged except that each designer-note box also carries `note`, so the parity check skips it. Settings keeps its seven tabs, the phone's tab bar wraps and sticks under the top bar, as #2276 built it. These designer decisions are built as drawn:

1. **Publishing.** The timeline is three steps: *Written and emailed*, *{window} to edit or stop it* ("From the mail or This week"), *Goes live*. Each step's moment is the site's own clock, "Sun 18 Oct · 4:00 PM". Where pages go live is the address with its Live badge and "Your website stays as it is." The five explainers are cut: "How each page goes out" with today's page, "Checked against your facts…", "Then in your Monday mail", "Set the publish time… below" and "How long you have to stop a page…". The publish time is a choice of clock times that saves when chosen. The stepper's buttons are "One day less" and "One day more", and every row label is in sentence case.
2. **Mail.** The sample-mail wells, the 7-day strip and the second card are cut. The card says "Sent to {email}". The rows are *Monday mail* and *A page is ready*, each with its one line. With both off, the card says only sign-in and account mail still come. The climbing count on the cut well goes with it, so Settings › Mail no longer reads the published pages.
3. **Billing.** "What you got this month" is now *This month*, "{from} to {to}. All included in {price}." The four tiles are a figure and what it counts, 2×2 on a phone and 4 across from `md`. The tile icons and sub-lines, "See every page →", the period bar and its legend, and "Show the detail" are cut. *Subscription* is renamed *Plan*, and the plan reads "€49 a month · tax included". The note under the button is "Update your card, download invoices, or cancel."
4. **Account.** The three-step sign-in strip and the Danger zone comparison grid are cut. The Email row is the address, *Change email*, which opens the field, and "You sign in with a link sent here. No password." Export is one row: *Your pages*, "{n} pages, with images", **Export everything**. A pending change reads "Open the link we sent before {at}, or nothing changes."

Where the build differs from the board:

1. **The Monday mail line names no send time** (owner R29, 2026-10-23, #1911). The board, and condition 3 of #2277, write "Mondays, 8:00 AM". The Monday mail goes when the site's weekly pass finishes, and the hourly tick picks the hour (`WEEKLY_TICK_CRON`), so a typed hour would be untrue. The line is "Mondays. What went live, what moved, and the next {days} pages."
2. **The billing card keeps its cancel route line** (owner E13, 2026-10-19, #1581; R24, 2026-10-23, #1853): "Reply to any ReachKit email or use Manage billing in Stripe." The board cuts it as a repeat of the button. Its first half is the route the button does not name.
3. **The refund line renders only behind the launch gate, as today** (owner 2026-10-20 Q13, #1598; D4).
4. **The time zone row keeps its Change** (SPEC §4: the customer may set the publish time, the time to stop a page and the time zone). The board draws only the zone and "From your browser." The zone reads as the place, then the identifier (#925).
5. **Publishing keeps "How your pages link your site"** below its two cards (SPEC §7, 2026-10-10, issue 1391). The board does not draw it.
6. **Account keeps the invoices note** under the Email row (REQ-077 c1: invoices and receipts go to the address in the billing portal). It also keeps *Open your pages* and *Help* between the card and the Danger zone (owner, 2026-10-08, issue 1339).
7. **Each danger action still takes the export first** (REQ-079 c3), before its typed confirmation. The board draws only the confirmation. The two consequence sentences and the confirm words "unpublish all" and "delete account" are unchanged.
8. **A pending address change sits under the Email row, not in it.** The row states only the address the account signs in with (REQ-077 c2).
9. **The plan reads "€49 a month · tax included"**, the shared `price.interval` that the Pricing card ships ("a month · tax included", `2026-11-01-2266`, ux-designer). ux-designer's review of this PR (2026-11-03) keeps "· tax included" as the trust-compliance price wording. `2026-11-01-2267` records that the free report still ships "VAT included" until the owner rules on the tax wording; whatever that ruling says, this line follows it through the one key.
10. **The paused lead is two sentences**: "Paused: no page goes live until you turn publishing on. Your pages are kept." The board's note writes three ("Paused. No page…"). Owner O6 (2026-10-31, #2145) has a key file's plain-words count only go down, and a third sentence would add a finding.
