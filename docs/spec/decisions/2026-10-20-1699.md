2026-10-20  **A cancel in the first 14 days refunds the first payment by itself, once per web address** (owner, rulings D4, A6 and E13, 2026-10-19, recorded for #1699; supersedes REQ-076's "refunds deferred" non-goal for this one case). When `customer.subscription.updated` reports `cancel_at_period_end` or `customer.subscription.deleted` arrives, and the cancel falls within 14 days of the customer's first paid invoice, ReachKit refunds that invoice in full through Stripe (idempotency key `refund:{invoice}`) and then ends access at once through `end-now.ts`. "Once per web address" is read from the `refunds` table: the canonical domains of the account's sites, against every earlier refunded account, never the email. A refund Stripe refuses fails neither the webhook nor access; it is logged with the invoice id and retried on the next delivery. Published pages follow the existing 30-day path. Settings › Billing states the E13 route ("Reply to any ReachKit email or use Cancel in Settings") and, inside the window and only where the address has had no refund, that the refund is automatic. Chargebacks, `past_due` and any later refund stay deferred.
