2026-10-31  **Only a cause critical for the customer is told** (owner O7, #2163). Critical is one of three things: a legal or safety risk only the customer can resolve, an explicit customer hold or their own words, or a destination they must fix.

1. Every reason written on a move into `needs_attention` is a closed type (`StopReason`, `src/lib/publish/record/needs-you.ts`), and `critical()` classifies it.
2. Critical: `reason_needs_customer` (an expired or invalid credential, no destination, DNS not pointed, a destination that refused), the customer's own words (`live_edit`, and a `rules:*` or `step_failed:*` page with `meta.first_edited_at`, which no re-check touches), and a reason this build cannot read. These are told once, as before: the Overview alert, the badge and the Monday mail's one needs-you line.
3. Not critical: `rules:*` and `step_failed:*` on a page the customer has not edited. The daily re-check rescues or sets them aside (#2160, #2162). They get no alert, no badge, no Monday line and no cause sentence.
4. `retries_exhausted` is re-attempted by `{system, publish/retry}` once a day, 23 h after its last claim (so 7 days hold 7 re-attempts), through `needs_attention → publishing` with every guard on that edge. After 7 days from its first rest (`DELIVERY_REATTEMPT_DAYS`) it is no longer re-attempted and is critical.
5. The keys `publish.needs-you.{rules,step,adds,elsewhere,pack}` are deleted with the draft screen's short-pack alert. `publish.needs-you.live-edit` is drafted; the owner corrects it.
