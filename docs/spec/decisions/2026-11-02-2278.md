2026-11-02  **The Google, AI and Competitors tabs (`/app/search`, `/app/ai-answers`, `/app/rivals`) are built to canvas version 36, boards AppGoogle, AppAI and AppCompetitors** (owner, #2278: *"approved, build it prioritising efficiency and speed of delivery. i need this live asap to start selling"*). The boards are copied from the canvas into `docs/design/boards/canvas/` unchanged. None has a Designer-note box, so nothing gains the `note` class. The designer's decisions in `PAGES-APP-CHANGES.md` are built as drawn:

- **Every tab:** the head (eyebrow, title and the day it was checked) sits above the tabs, as Overview's does. Searches are mono (`docs/DESIGN.md` › type). A list or table longer than its first rows ends on one "Show all N pages" or "Show all N searches" control, 44px tall, and N is the stored row count. The "6 of 12…" and "9 of 12…" counters are cut.
- **AppGoogle:** the "Where your pages sit" dot strip and its legend are cut. Each page's line drops the search it repeated ("Place 8, up 6 since Oct 12."; "Place 18, flat for 3 weeks. Update goes live Tue 20."), and the second "not in results yet" is cut. So is the market table's lead line. The pages and market tiles draw a bar under their figure. At 390 the searches table is the board's three lines (the search and your place; its demand and who shows; what we're doing) under the Search and You heads. Each "what we're doing" badge wears the board's icon.
- **AppAI:** the 4-icon legend, "6 of 12 pages asked", the summary that repeated the tiles, the two body lines that repeated the tile explainers, "How many of those 184…" and "Opens as a grid" are cut. Each engine's answer is a flat 44px row at 390 with a round mark tile, and from `sm` it is one of three tinted boxes. The 3%–10% honesty range stays. Its bounds are the stored month reading's own (`percent(rate.low)`, `percent(rate.high)`), and "Mentioned by AI: n of m answers" heads the card.
- **AppCompetitors:** "See every answer →", the first-page card's sub-line and the long table intro are cut. The table's lead is shown, not folded. The competitor filter is a labelled `select`, as drawn. At 390 the table is stacked rows: the search, "N a month", what we're doing, then "Won by …". The far-larger-sites line is the board's shorter one, and Settings › Market reads the same key.

**Kept where a ruled requirement asks for it:**

- **A page's question keeps the search it came from** on AppAI ("… from {search}"). REQ-093 c3 says a question's wording is shown only beside the search it was phrased from (as ruled for issue 2267). The board draws the wording alone.
- **The collapse arrow** on each tile is daisyUI's CSS arrow at the tile's corner, which stands for the board's chevron after "What this means" (DESIGN 2026-10-07). The label is primary, as drawn.
- **Not measured is never 0** (REQ-004): a tile or card nothing measured says so, and its bar is not drawn.

**Known differences, left for a board or a component change:**

- AppGoogle draws a small place line per page. A page row stores only its first, previous and current place, and the line beside it already says them in words. No row chart is added, and the board's per-row `chart` components stay a gap in `AppGoogle.baseline.json`.
- The pages tile's bar is one `progress` (showing of read), not the board's two-tone bar with a swatch legend. The split under it stays one line of words.
- The Competitors bars are `PresenceBars` (Recharts, one per row), which the component check does not count as the board's `.track`. From `lg` the board sets "Who your buyers see first" in three columns, and the shared component keeps one.
