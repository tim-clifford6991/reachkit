2026-10-18  **A refresh closes the gap to the pages above it** (owner on issue 1521: *"follow seo master"*, from the SEO master's gap review of 2026-10-18, B4; amends D42 #7; the owner's are the 2 results, the 3 sections, the 40% and D59's 30 days, every other reading is a drafted default the owner may correct). (1) *Close.* A live page of ours whose own address ranks 4–20 for its search in the site's stored ranked rows (`report.ownRankedRows`, the rows the pass already buys) and whose version is 30 days old or more is due a `close` refresh, ahead of a newer fact and of age (after `own_figures`, `unmet` and `lost_position`). It ranks as a close update (D15), and it is the page's one open row: the page's open `answerable_page` is dismissed when it is planned. (2) *Heading gaps.* The research step records the H2 and H3 headings of the top results it already reads, inside their `<main>` or `<article>` and never in their navigation, header, footer or sidebar, at 0¢. A subtopic is a heading's content words in any supported language; two headings are one subtopic where half the words of the shorter are the other's. The pack of a `close`, `unmet` or `lost_position` refresh carries `gaps`: each subtopic two or more of those results head and no H2 or H3 of the live page does, most covered first, at most three — never the FAQ, a summary, the contents or the comments, and never only the target's own words. A gap is carried only with the claims of the pack whose passage holds half its words beside the target's, rounded down and never fewer than one; one no claim speaks to is left out. (3) *New sections.* Such a refresh may add up to three sections, each headed on one gap and linking one of the claims it names, and where its pack carries a gap its sections may grow to 40% over the live page's (the floor stays 20% under). `required_blocks` stops one that adds a fourth, or a section on no gap or linking none of its claims (`new_sections`), and the fix call is told so. Every other update still keeps its live sections within 20%. (4) *Answer box.* The writer returns the live short answer word for word unless a fact in the pack changes the answer to the target; where no section changed, code keeps the live short answer, takeaways and closing line. (5) *Prices.* A live listicle or comparison of ours whose oldest recorded rival price was read more than 30 days ago (D59) is due a `stale_prices` refresh, after a newer fact and before age; the hourly re-check plans it as it plans `structure`, ahead of it, and the customer's stop and §6's floor stand. Its research reads the rivals' pages again, and the writer is told to state each rival's price as the pack now gives it and drop one the pack no longer holds. (6) *Dates.* A written refresh whose every section reads as the live page has it is dated as the version it refreshes (`drafts.meta.datedAs`, as #1507); only a change of what the page says moves its dates. No call is added: the reads are pages already fetched, and the write and fix calls are the day's own. It adds no sentence to any screen.
