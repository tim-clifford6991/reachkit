2026-11-23  **A short evidence pack writes the planned page** (owner, #2722 ruling 100.2; #3390). A pack short of options, buyer questions, sources, recent results, facts or what it adds is written with what it holds, and its `draft_generated` line and `meta.standard` name the shortfall (`pack_short`). Only two things stop a page before writing: a comparison with no first-hand evidence for a product it names (`rival_evidence`), and an update whose live page is missing (`live_page`). The fallbacks that replaced the planned page go: the `adds` stop and its update-instead, the last step's page written anyway, and its waivers. `lines.areas.src/lib/generate` in `scripts/budgets.json` falls to what is left.
