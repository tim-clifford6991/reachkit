2026-10-20  **Competitors are re-checked once a month, and a change buys no second deep pass** (owner, ruling B5 of 2026-10-19: *"two-list competitor model, re-inferred monthly (#1560)"*; #1560's amended condition 3, *"The 150¢ competitor deep pass (P8) is removed"*; built in #1700). Amends the sentence on a rival change in *How a pass keeps them current* (2026-10-02, #1185 point 4, D28). (1) A rival added or dropped no longer starts a #837 re-measure, after a deep pass or a weekly pass; the next weekly pass measures the new set. A founder's own change still re-measures (#837). (2) A weekly pass re-checks the rivals only when its site-local week starts on day 1–7 of the month: the first Monday of the month. A deep pass still re-checks them. (3) When the rivals the report derived at 0¢ (§6.6) are the rivals in force, the rival model call is skipped. The weekly cap and `TIER_PARAMETERS.weekly` are unchanged.
