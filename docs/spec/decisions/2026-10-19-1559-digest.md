2026-10-19  **The Monday mail states the week it committed** (owner, #1559; the wording is drafted and the owner may correct it). "The next three things" becomes the week this Monday committed: its pages in date order under *this week’s pages* (`mail.weekly.next`), and its spares under *spares, if a day’s page cannot be written* (`mail.weekly.spares`), each named by its search. A week with no page left keeps its one line (`mail.weekly.next.none`); a week with no spare has no spare list. The missing-day and stall mails are unchanged.
