The wording and drawing exceptions, and why each holds:

1. **"How far your 12 pages have got"** ships as "How far your {n} pages got". A title is six words at most (SPEC §0; owner R12, B12).
2. **The mover's line and band** keep `--chart-you` and `--chart-goal`, not the board's green. BUILD §2.4 allows two series colours only, and status colours are for state. The board's date under each reading is drawn.
3. **The ladder's bars** are daisyUI `progress` at every width. From `lg` the board draws 56px blocks that fill upward; no library draws that, and the app carries no inline style or custom SVG chart.
4. **One denominator per ladder** (ux review of #2309, round 2): every rung's "of N" counts the live pages the step can be read on, which is the head's N wherever the site is hosted. A page no reading has reached yet is not on a rung above Live. "{k} not checked yet" is said once, under the Live rung's bar, so a page not measured is never left unsaid or drawn as a 0. The mail keeps its own "of N pages checked".
5. **Dates** are the product's en-US forms ("Friday, September 4" in the head). The board's day-month order is drawn where it is a short value: "Mon 19 – Sun 25 Oct", "26 Oct", "Mon 16 Nov".
6. **The "2 climbing" pill** keeps the shared Climbing label's primary tint, not the board's green, so Overview, This week and the page screen draw one label one way.
7. **The waiting count beside Overview's menu item stays.** REQ-040 c2, as `_shell/model.ts` states it, counts "the calendar items waiting on the customer — §9's `in_review` and `needs_attention` drafts"; SPEC §4 (2026-09-20) moves it to Overview; SPEC §4 (2026-10-17, #1477) names it: "On Overview and in the count beside it, *needs you* holds a page only while it waits on the customer."
