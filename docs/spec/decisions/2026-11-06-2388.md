2026-11-06  **A day whose new page does not pass takes a ready update from the committed week, in the same run** (owner, 2026-11-04 and 2026-11-05: "each day must have new or updated content, no exceptions"; #2388). Decision: when the day's run ends without a page for a reason of its own (`generate:*`, but not `no_site` or `no_scan`), the same run reads the committed week only, never a re-ranking. It takes first a ready update spare, then a ready update committed for a later date of the week, latest date first. Within each, ReachKit's own refresh comes before an `improve` row. A candidate enters review only where it passes today's whole battery. Tier A0 is a code-mended `structure` refresh at 0¢, on every such day. Tier B, the writer-made refresh from stored research, is the 2026-11-08 ruling of #2503; a day never spends above its cap. A row the rules stopped gives up its date to a mend only where the mend passes. With no passing candidate, the day keeps its own reason, and nothing is padded or invented. No later-hour retry (R-1, 2026-11-04). Until the owner rules R-F3, the 30-day refresh floor stands, so a customer's first 30 days are filled by new pages or stay an honest empty day. The day's record reads `drafted` with reason `refresh:<original reason>`.
