2026-10-31  **A sign-in link asked for again within 15 minutes is the same link; after that, a new link replaces the old** (owner default O2, 2026-10-31, #2098; security expert judgement, the owner has not objected). This amends REQ-024 c4 and §3's "a new one spends older ones". Anyone who knows a customer's address could ask for links until theirs was dead. Now a request inside 15 minutes mails the link already live, so a flood mails the customer a link that still works. The link is stored sealed under its own key and cleared when it is used or the address changes. Once spent, or after 15 minutes, a request mints a new link and the old one stops working. Email-change links still mint on every request. Single use and the 24-hour life are unchanged. The customer sees no new screen and no new sentence.
