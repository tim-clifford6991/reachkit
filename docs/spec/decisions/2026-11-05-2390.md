2026-11-05  **Readiness is assessed in three places** (architect ruling on #2390, 2026-11-05; owner principle 2026-11-04: new or updated content every day). Amends 2026-10-24-1624. Readiness is assessed in three places: the week's commit (`planWeek`), the evening check of the committed rows (`pageForDay`), and, between Mondays, the rows a refresh planner has just created (structure, stale prices, link-backs, moved links), with `only` = exactly those ids. Context: such rows stayed `not_assessed` until the next Monday, invisible to their own week (owner principle 2026-11-04: new or updated content every day). It reads stored rows only and calls no model or vendor, so it is 0¢. Which rows are ready is unchanged, and nothing ranks or commits. Consequence: an assessed row still fills a day only if it is committed (L2, #2388).
