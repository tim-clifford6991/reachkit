2026-10-31  **No demo text reads the site's demo phrases only** (owner R-B, #2133; amends the #1249 check narrowly). The site's `nav`, `header` and `footer` lines are no longer phrases a page may not repeat: a menu label such as "How it works", a hosted page's table of contents and its breadcrumb are also honest headings and link labels, and stopping on them stopped pages that said nothing false. The site's demo and example phrases stay, and a sentence that is one of them, whole and three words or more, still stops the page. seo-master's reasoning is in the 2026-10-31 content-rules report linked from the issue.
