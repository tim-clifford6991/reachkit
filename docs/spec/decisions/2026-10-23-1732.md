2026-10-23  **The free-scans gallery follows board `PubScans`, and its filter is the kind of business** (owner, *"follow all recommendations"*, 2026-10-20, `docs/spec/decisions/2026-10-20-1716.md`; design-parity audit §4.16; #1732). `/scans` shows the count of listed reports in its lead, the 12 newest reports' Visibility Scores on one strip, *Free reports* with a *Kind of business* filter, *What every free report shows*, then the shared close. The filter is *All* and the six kinds of `src/lib/market/kinds.ts`, read from each report's stored category by the profile's own reading (`kindOfProfile`); a category that decides no kind is listed under *All* only, never guessed. This replaces 2026-10-10's category chips, their *More* menu and the `?cat=` filter; `?kind=` takes their place, and an unknown kind is the whole gallery. The rest of 2026-10-10 stands: the toolbar is two rows on every width, a placeholder is no category, and on a phone the first card is on screen on load, so below the laptop width the scan field and the score strip come after the reports. A card reads *"{score} of 100 {band}"* or *"Not scored"*, never 0, then its kind, its stored category and its day.
