2026-11-06  **Nothing is refunded by itself; the plan is month to month** (owner, #2299 comment 6689949822: *"Money-back guarantee: no … cancel any time, with no refund of the current month … no copy may promise a refund"*, recorded for #2448). This supersedes `2026-10-20-1699.md` (D4, the first-14-days refund) and `2026-10-24-1592.md` (D10, the first-renewal refund with no page live, and its E1, E2 and E4 lines). A cancel at any time, the first 14 days included, ends nothing early: access runs to `paid_through` and no refund is made. A paid first renewal is never refunded, whether or not a page went live. The webhook calls `refunds.create` on no event. Settings › Billing states no refund date, and no mail says a refund was made or promised; the `refund-refused` incident is gone with the path that raised it. The rows already in `refunds` and `renewal_refunds` stay as history: none is deleted and no migration drops either table. A refund the law requires, such as a consumer's statutory withdrawal, is made by hand under E13 (`docs/walk/refund.md`). The Terms' own words are #1641's.
