2026-10-31  **Setup, Settings and sign-in say each thing once, in plain words** (owner O6 plain words, with the Say It Once answers R1–R18, #2171). Setup's three steps are **Site · Details · Connect**. "Check" became "Details" because the Monday check already owns that word. Each step's time reads "about 10 minutes of your time", and the waiting strip's last phase is "First page live". The Connect step and its screen no longer draw "How it connects" (the website, the one setting and the pages as a diagram) or "After you add it" (reserved, waiting, live as a timeline). The record, its steps, the check's own answer, *Do this later* and the week that needs nothing more stay. Every DNS line says "where you bought your web address", and the record name keeps "CNAME" (rule 4). Boards Setup, Setup1, Setup2 and SetupZone are amended to the shipped text, and the milestone icon leaves `docs/design/parity/icons.json` with the diagram.

Settings › Your voice offers two voices under **Write as**: "Your business, as “we”" and "A neutral guide, naming your business". "Let ReachKit choose for each page" and "Short answers, 600 to 900 words" are gone, because ReachKit picks the voice and the length for each page (rewrite D5). An account that chose neither has nothing checked, and its pages are written as before. A stored choice is still honoured. The two choices use the `pair` layout. Billing says "subscription", not "plan". The Monday tile is "Monday mails". The danger zone's table reads "Account, pages and settings". The Market tab's button and its lines say "Check again now". The publishing timeline says a page is "Emailed in your first week" and then appears in the Monday mail, because page mails stop at the first Monday mail. Boards SettingsIndex, SettingsMarket, SettingsPublishing, SettingsVoice, SettingsMail, SettingsBilling and SettingsAccount are amended to the shipped text. The tab names and the Settings head are not changed here; they wait for R7's three tabs.

Sign in (§3) no longer draws the "Waiting inside" example dashboard or the "nothing to install" chip. The request arm is the form alone, with how signing in works below it. The answered arm and the dead-link arm keep their split, with the inbox or why links stop. Boards AuthSignIn, AuthCheckEmail and AuthExpired are amended, and the sign-in specimen leaves `_landing/specimen.ts`.

Held, not shipped: the broader-market chooser (`setup.remeasure.suggested`, `.own`, `.refused.empty`) and `setup.release.market-too-small`. R17's "the product picks the broader market" is not built, and the chooser is still the only way a thin market reaches a page (journey 09), so its copy and UI stay until that behaviour lands.
