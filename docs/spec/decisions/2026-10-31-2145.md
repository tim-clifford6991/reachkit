2026-10-31  **Customer copy uses plain words, and the count of long ones only goes down** (owner O6, plain words for the first 90 days, #2145). Every customer-facing string follows these rules: the landing, How it works, pricing, the free scan and report, setup, the /app screens and every mail.

1. One idea per sentence: aim for 8 to 12 words, at most 15. A heading is at most 6 words (#1463 stays in force and meets O6's 7).
2. At most 2 sentences per paragraph.
3. Lead with what the reader gets or must do. The mechanism comes last, or never.
4. Plain, everyday words for a busy local business owner. Never: SEO, index, crawl, CNAME (outside setup's DNS steps), derivation, pass, re-score, supply, spares, veto, draft (as a customer word), opportunity, keyword, prompt, fetched.
5. Second person: "you" and "your site"; "we", or "ReachKit" where a name is needed.
6. No asides: no em-dash insertions, no "not X but Y", no colon reveal, no scare quotes, no filler ("simply", "just", "actually", "worth noting").
7. Numbers come from the facts: €49, up to 7 pages a week, about 5 minutes to connect, 10 minutes of your time in total.
8. Say each thing once per screen: one caveat and one telling of the steps per page.
9. Honest claims only: "names you" or "mentions you", never "recommends you"; the free report never says ChatGPT; never promise a ranking.
10. One name per concept (the Say It Once vocabulary).
11. An error says what happened and what to do next, in one or two short sentences.
12. Exempt, never counted or rewritten: text on a customer's own pages (`hosted.*`, `body.*`, `fact.*`), the AI engine names (`ai-answers.engine.*`), the legal wording (`legal.*.body`, the withdraw and order-step keys of #1863 and #2110), the owner-only `review.ts` and `ops.ts`, `keys/de/`, and the Hosted, Review, AppCosts and Legal boards.

How it is checked. `scripts/lib/plain-words.mjs` is the one parser: a `{slot}` or `[[owner: …]]` is one word, a line is a paragraph, a sentence ends at `.`, `!`, `?` or `…` before a capital, digit, quote, bracket or slot, and an `.aria` key skips the paragraph rule. `tests/ui/design/strategy.test.ts` counts long sentences, long paragraphs, long board headings and the rule-4 words that still have hits, per key file against `plainWords` in `scripts/budgets.json` and per board against `plainWords` in its `docs/design/parity/baseline.json` row. A count may fall and never rise; `audit` already refuses a raised budget. The rule-4 words with no hit left (prompt, keyword, veto, derivation) are retired names, zero tolerance, and the retired names are now read on the boards too. A rule-4 word becomes a retired name in the change that removes its last hit.
