2026-11-01  **Sign-in is one centred card per state, built to canvas v36** (owner, #2268: *"approved, build it prioritising efficiency and speed of delivery. i need this live asap to start selling"*). Boards AuthSignIn, AuthCheckEmail, AuthExpired and AuthConfirm in `docs/design/boards/canvas/` are the v36 files, unchanged except that each designer-note box also carries `note`, so the parity check skips it. These designer decisions are built as drawn. AuthSignIn drops the "Sign in" eyebrow, the "link works 24 hours" chip, "New to ReachKit? Start a free scan →", "How signing in works" and the busy-state sentence, and its column is centred. AuthCheckEmail drops the inbox phone, its captions, the 24-hour bar and "A newer link replaces an older one". Its facts are one card of three rows: Sent to, Subject *Your sign-in link*, and Works until. AuthExpired drops the "Why links stop working" figure, "Ask for a new one below" and "Start a free scan →". Its field label is "The email you paid with", and its lead ends "Nothing in your account changed." AuthConfirm keeps its words and is centred. All four use the landing footer already shipped.

Wording exceptions and their rulings:
- REQ-098 c2's last string, "New to ReachKit? Start a free scan →", is no longer on the screen: the v36 approval cuts it, because the header already carries Free scan. The other four strings of c2 stay byte for byte.
- REQ-098 c7's dead-link line becomes "Links work for 24 hours, and only the newest one works. Nothing in your account changed." It still says that the link can no longer be used, and the field under it is the way to ask for another. The line is the same whatever the reason.
- "If that address has an account, a sign-in link is on its way." stays exact (SPEC §3, no account enumeration).
- "Works until" is the send time plus `SIGNIN_LINK_TTL_H`, written on the reader's clock, never typed. The board's "Tomorrow" holds while the link lasts 24 h, and `tests/account/identity/otp-expiry.test.ts` pins that lifetime.
