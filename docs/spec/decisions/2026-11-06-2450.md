2026-11-06  **At launch a buyer connects by DNS only** (owner, #2450). The CNAME is the only connect path a site without a WordPress destination is offered. WordPress waits behind `WORDPRESS_CONNECT` in `src/lib/config/constants/publish.ts`, off, until it is proven on a real site (#324). A site that already has a WordPress destination keeps it unchanged.

## Boards changed by this file

The `.dc.html` files were edited by hand, in their WordPress wording only:

- `Setup` draws the hosted destination alone (its *On my WordPress site* option and its `Layers` mark go). The *WordPress chosen* state note stays as the drawing of the switched-on arm.
- `PubHowItWorks` loses the *WordPress* pill under *Connect your website*. Its line reads *Copy one line we give you into your domain’s settings.* The research detail drops *On WordPress, ReachKit rewrites missing titles and descriptions for you.*, and the cancel answer drops *Pages on your WordPress stay where they are.*
- `PubLanding`, `PubLandingPhone`, `PubHowItWorks` and `Pricing` answer *What do I need to start?* with *A website and about 10 minutes. After you pay, copy one line into your domain’s settings to connect it.*
- `PubReport`'s *Pages AI can’t quote* badge reads *ReachKit writes*.
- `PubToolMeta` and `PubToolOnPage` state *Writes the pages your buyers are looking for* in place of the WordPress row.
- `OverviewDNS`'s *Other ways to connect* drops the WordPress line and link.

Switching WordPress back on is an owner decision that restores these drawings.
