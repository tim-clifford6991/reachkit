2026-10-24  **A competitor's page that AI links becomes a topic, from either competitor list** (owner, ruling B5 of 2026-10-19: *"two-list competitor model"*; the SEO review's C4, *"AI answers pick topics through the pages they cite, not the prompt's words"*; #1560's amended condition 2; built in #1560). The two lists are the ones a site already keeps. The *search competitors* are the rivals a report derives at 0¢ from its results pages (§6.6). The *business competitors* are the rivals in force, read from the profile and the AI answers, and re-checked monthly as the 2026-10-20 (#1700) entry sets out. (1) Each tracked-prompt answer stores the pages it cited on other sites, without tracking parameters and without a site's home. A stored scan gains them at 0¢ from the answers `fetches` kept, through the re-score. (2) A cited page on a domain in either list proposes a topic. The topic is the last segment of the page's address, two to six words with no number. A search whose results page was bought and holds every word of it stands in for it, the one with the fewest words first, so the topic is shaped by that results page (an offering page where the page asks for one, 2026-10-20 #1618). A topic that names the business or a competitor is not proposed. (3) It then passes the gates every proposal passes: the business-fit judgement, the "would a buyer search this?" check and readiness, #1552's demand rule among them. Its rival's address is the cited page.
