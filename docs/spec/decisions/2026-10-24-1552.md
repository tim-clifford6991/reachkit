2026-10-24  **A new site writes its own offering and what buyers ask before comparisons, fills one cluster before a third, and writes a rival-only "vs" only from first-hand evidence** (owner ruling R31, 2026-10-24, *"approved as recommended, build condition 3"*, on #1552's three questions; Condition 3 of #1552, the SEO review's R4 of 2026-10-19). It amends 2026-10-20-1610 (the points and the week's rules) and D12 (what a new page adds). (1) **A new site.** A site whose current report counts fewer than 30 searches it ranks for is new. A count not measured is the cold-start 0. A site with no readable report is ordered as an established one. (2) **Own offering and asked questions first: a points part, not a rule.** On a new site, a Write or Earn row (a new page of the site's) gains one point, part `new_site`, when its page is on the business's own offering (page type `product`) or on a question buyers were seen asking on a stored SERP (`askedBeside`). A row whose page compares products (a listicle, comparison or alternatives page) never gains it. A tracked prompt does not count as asked, because ReachKit wrote it (2026-10-19-1552). So, at equal fit and demand, such a row ranks ahead of a comparison, and the one points ranking of 1610 still holds. (3) **One cluster filled before a third: an ordering rule.** On a new site, a new page in a cluster with no live or drafted page waits while the site already has two clusters with a page and none of them holds five. A row with no cluster is not held. Like the topic's rotation, it orders and never empties a day. It gives way after the rotation and D52's Q&A, and before the offering count (place rule `best, a third cluster`). 1610's "four rules and no others" becomes five. (4) **First-hand evidence for a rival-only "vs".** A page whose search sets two or more rivals against each other and does not name the business is written only where its pack holds, for every rival it names, a claim read off that rival's own site, or off a study or primary source about it, and never off a top search result. Otherwise the pack is short (`rival_evidence`), nothing is written, and the topic rests `TOPIC_REST_DAYS` (28) as any short pack does (issue 1536). An update of a live page is not held to it. (5) **A page that adds nothing (D12).** The founder is asked first, as today: the draft waits in *Needs you* for a fact. The topic rests 28 days from that day, so nothing else is written on it unless the founder answers, and an answer that passes queues it as any written draft does. D12's bar (three added claims, one of them the business's) is unchanged. It already holds R4's "at least one grounded claim that none of the top results states". The numbers 30, 5 and 2 are the SEO review's drafted defaults, which the owner may correct. 0¢: nothing is bought.
