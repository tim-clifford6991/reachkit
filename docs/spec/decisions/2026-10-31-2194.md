2026-10-31  **Government sites and job seekers' searches are sources, not rivals, and a job seeker's search proposes no page** (D24 extension; seo-master and architect, 2026-10-31, from scan d149c119; examples of #1365 (2) under #1454 and of §6 *Topics by business fit*, #1506, under #1127). No owner ruling was needed.

- **A public host is a source.** A government's, an army's or a public service's host, read by its suffix (`.gov`, `.mil`, `.gov.xx`, `.govt.xx`, `.gouv.xx`, `.gob.xx`, `gc.ca`, `nhs.uk`, `police.uk`, `bund.de`, `gv.at`, `admin.ch`, `europa.eu`), and a university's or a school's (`.edu`, `.ac.xx`, `.edu.xx`), is never a rival, wherever it ranks.
- **A job page** is read by its host (`jobs.`, `career(s).`, `karriere.`, `stellen.`), its address (a path segment such as jobs, vacancies, careers or stellenangebote) or its title: job(s), career(s), vacancy, salary, apprenticeship, traineeship, "how to become", Stellenangebote, Gehalt, Ausbildung, emploi, empleo, vacatures.
- **A job seeker's search** is one whose titled top ten is at least half job pages. It gives no rival, beside the place, namesake and explainer searches already skipped, and it proposes no Write page on the free report or the paid plan. A search with fewer job pages than that is kept. A skipped search counts as no measurement for any rival, never as a zero.
- **The exemption.** A business whose own category is about jobs, or says it recruits, staffs or trains (recruitment, staffing, headhunting, employment agency, training, courses, bootcamp, Personalvermittlung, Zeitarbeit, Weiterbildung, recrutement, intérim, reclutamiento, formación, werving, uitzend), keeps those searches, their rivals and their pages: job seekers are its buyers. There is no new kind of business.
- **A source row** (`isSourceRow`, `src/lib/market/rivals/domains.ts`) is one reading of a top-ten row for every holder: a domain the rivals' partition sets aside or the report stored as a source, a job page, or an article about others or an explainer. A report rival's row never is one. The Google table's "Who's first" names no source.
- **Known limit.** National agencies on a plain country domain (for example a federal employment agency on `.de` or `.fr`) are missed by the suffix rule. On a jobs search the jobs skip catches them. No list of hosts is added.
