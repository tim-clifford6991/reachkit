2026-11-23  **No re-check loop for a relaxed rule** (owner, #2722 rulings 100.1 and 99.1; #3393). A waiting draft is no longer read again after a standard deploy, so `drafts_restandarded` and the set-aside for a newer standard end; a draft already set aside that way stays as it is. The morning re-check of a stopped page keeps its planned title and never re-picks one. The stopped-page rescue stays until its 30-day count is read. `lines.areas.src/lib/generate` and `lines.areas.src/lib/publish` in `scripts/budgets.json` fall to what is left.
