2026-10-19  **The mail diet: one Monday mail, the page-ready mail for the first week only, and page 1's published mail** (owner, D3, *"page-ready mail is on until the first Monday mail, then off"*, #1581; plan item 8 and the customer-success review of the same day, *"remove veto-reminder, inactivity, and the per-page published mail after page 1"*; #1593). This amends the Retention row and the page-ready and published lines of this section. (1) *Page-ready.* A customer who never set the switch gets the page-ready mail until their first Monday mail is sent (`scans.digest_sent_at` on any of their sites), then not. A customer who set it keeps their choice. The switch never holds a page. (2) *Removed.* The inactivity nudge (idle 7 days) and the veto reminder (under 6 hours) are no longer kinds, and the tick no longer asks for them. Retention is payment-failed, cancellation, hosting-end and the win-back once at +30 days. (3) *Published.* The published mail is sent for the site's first live publication only, including where that page was taken down later. Every later page is told in the Monday mail. Settings › Mail names that switch *When your first page goes live* (drafted). The Monday mail's stop link per page is issue 1674.
