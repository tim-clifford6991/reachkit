2026-10-24  **Readiness answers one of six reasons, assessed in two places** (owner ruling C6, 2026-10-19: *"merge the readiness rules from 11 to about 6"*; architect S19; #1624; the merge below is a drafted reading the owner may correct). One check, `eligible()`, says why a row may not fill a day, in this order: `resting` (a stopped or skipped draft's rest, a cluster judged not working, a retired URL), `destination_cannot_address` (an update the destination cannot deliver, or a topic one of the site's pages already covers), `low_fit`, `no_grounding_fact`, `no_intent` (no demand of the kind its page needs: a comparison nobody was seen asking, a keyword page below its gate, a format page with no format word, or no volume and no tracked prompt near it) and `outsized`. `cluster_suppressed` and `url_retired` merge into `resting`, `already_covered` into `destination_cannot_address`, and `keyword_gate` and `format_not_allowed` into `no_intent`. Which rows are ready is unchanged. Readiness is assessed only when a week is committed (`planWeek`: Monday, setup's first week, or a date no commit covers) and by the evening check of the committed rows. A pass no longer assesses the rows it derives, so it no longer reports writable days. 0¢.
