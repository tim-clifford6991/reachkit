2026-10-18  **A customer can take down one live page, and put it back within 7 days** (issue 1532, from the owner's ruling on issue 1337: *"they can solely edit or stop the content"*; the 7 days, the 90 days and every sentence are drafted defaults the owner may correct). Found: before a page publishes it can be stopped; once it was live, the only way to take it down was Settings → unpublish everything (#259). (1) *Take down.* Every live page carries *Take down* (`draft.action.take-down`) on Plan's *Published* list and in the rail of its own screen. The first press asks in the page itself (`draft.take-down.question`), never in a browser dialog; *Take it down* or *Keep it live*. (2) *The path.* It is `unpublish()` with the customer as actor and the reason `take_down`, for every live version at the page's address (a refresh leaves older versions live beside the newest). No row is deleted (ADR-080). A hosted address answers 410, its cache is cleared, the sitemap and the feed drop it, it is announced once (issue 1511) and the site's other pages show a link to it as its words (issue 1506), all because `unpublished_at` is set. A WordPress post is returned to draft by its adapter; where the site could not be written, the destination's health is checked again so the fault shows there. (3) *Afterwards.* The system never puts the page back: §9 gains one edge, `unpublished → published`, open only to the customer and only while the page's last move is their own take-down (`put_back_window`). The topic the page's versions were written for rests for 90 days from the take-down (`open`, `resting`, so the unique index holds its place and it is not proposed again). A refresh of the address that has not gone out is stopped (its draft skipped, an unwritten row dismissed). A page its customer took down and put back is theirs, as an edited page is: the hourly topic re-check (issue 1506), the consistency re-check (issue 1507) and every refresh cause leave it. A page taken down by the system or by *unpublish everything* has no way back. (4) *Put back.* For 7 days a hosted page's screen says *You took this page down. You can put it back until {date}.* (`draft.put-back.until`) beside *Put back* (`draft.action.put-back`), which publishes the same versions at the same address: the destination first (it clears its cache), then each row's `unpublished_at` and outcome are cleared and each draft takes `unpublished → published` with the reason `put_back`; the topic is queued again and a hosted address announced. The draft's transitions keep both moves. A WordPress post returned to draft is not put back: that is a call that sets a post to `publish`, which ADR-084 Decision 1 keeps out of the WordPress client, and the owner is asked on issue 1532 whether to open it for this case. 0¢. App screens stay English (owner, issue 1443); the German drafts are in the pull request for the owner.
