2026-10-19  **A needs-you technical issue opens a fix the customer can act on** (owner report on issue 1550: Overview's "Needs you" task "simply links me to my free report"; the readings and sentences are drafted defaults the owner may correct). (1) *Where it lands.* A technical issue in Overview's needs-you opens *Technical issues* at `/app/issues`, inside the account, at that check — never the free report at `/scan/{domain}`, which carries the offer. The view holds one card per check waiting on the customer: its title, its count over its set, its severity, one sentence saying what to do, and the addresses it applies to (or that it applies to the whole site). It is not in the navigation; Overview's needs-you and its count of the rest open it. (2) *Something to act on.* A check is a needs-you item only where it has a fix sentence and, for a count of pages or links, the addresses it was counted from. Broken internal links now keep the addresses that answered with an error. A report written before the addresses or the second read were kept states the count on the report and raises no item. (3) *Slow page, two reads.* A page whose crawl read took 3 s or more is read once more in the same crawl, under the same bounds; it is slow only where both reads took 3 s or more, and the quicker of the two is kept on the report and shown beside the page. A second read that does not come back leaves the page not slow. 0¢: the second read is the crawl's own fetch.
