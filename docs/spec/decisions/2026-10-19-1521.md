2026-10-19  **The gap refresh is only for pages near page one** (owner, ruling B8 on issue 1521: *"the +3.7¢ gap refresh, only for pages near page one"*; narrows 2026-10-18-1521 (2)–(3)). A `close`, `unmet` or `lost_position` refresh carries `gaps`, may add sections and may grow to 40% only where the page's own place for its search was 11–20 when the refresh was planned. That place is the one the page's stored search reading holds (`publications.search_reading`), else, before a reading is stored, its own address in the stored ranked rows; the refresh row records it (`shortfall.place`). Any other page, including one with no place read, keeps today's refresh: no gaps, no new section, within 20%. The `close` cause itself still covers places 4–20.
