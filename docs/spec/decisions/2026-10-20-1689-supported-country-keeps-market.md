2026-10-20  **A supported country keeps its market** (owner, #1689, on the seo-master ruling; amends D66's *"anything else → nearest supported country in English"*). A reading whose country is on the list is measured in that country, in that country's own language, whatever language its page reads as: a `.de` site that reads as English, or reads as nothing, is measured in Germany, in German, not in the United Kingdom in English. The report states only what was measured and what the site read as (*"Measured as searched in Germany, in German. Your site reads as English; …"*). It never says the site's market is that country, because a site may serve many markets; a ruling on multi-market sites is to follow. A supported language read in a supported country that does not use it (for example, German in the United Kingdom) is still measured in the country's own language. Cost is unchanged: the same calls with a different `location_name` and `language_code`.
