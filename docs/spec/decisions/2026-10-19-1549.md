2026-10-19  **Pages left counts the days a week can write, and a dismissed topic stays dismissed** (owner, #1549; the numbers are drafted defaults the owner may correct; its depth, floor and refill are as amended by the 1559 decision of the same day, which commits a week of seven and three spares). Found on reachkit.app: "Pages left: 6" was six listicles and comparisons, and D52 lets one of those out a week; no page went out on 10-18, 10-19 or 10-20. (1) *Pages left* and the low-supply notice count writable days: the days, up to a week and its spares, that the week's commit fills with a page of the topic's own type, by the same rows and rules as the ranking. A listicle or comparison over D52's cap waits for its window and is not counted as the Q&A it may fall back to. A resting or unready row is not counted, and a topic another row on the same topic would be written as is counted once. (2) A pass that leaves fewer writable days than a week and its spares writes down answer, how-to and documentation topics from the "People also ask" questions and related searches already stored beside the site's searches, those beside the market's own searched terms first, the ones the pass judged at fit 2 before the ones no pass judged. A question no pass judged is written only where §6.7's relevance guard holds (the business's vocabulary supports every word of it) and it names no rival. Nothing is bought and no model is asked; it runs inside a pass only. A question buyers were seen asking passes the keyword gate as a top-fit search does (issue 1519: it is observed demand). Where the questions run out first, the site is left short and the count says so; nothing is composed to fill a day. (3) Of a weekly pass's twelve searches, at least `SELECTION.minFresh` (4) are ones the site has not measured in the last `SELECTION.freshWeeks` (4) weeks, and at least `SELECTION.minAnswering` (4) are how-to or informational, where the market holds them, inside the rival and how-to caps. Neither floor pads: a market without them selects as before. (4) A dismissed topic is not written down again unless a measured volume above the one it was dismissed at is read for its search. (5) A search a held row already absorbed joins that row's topic; a member whose business fit is below the gate never wins a topic over one at or above it; a page `content/recheck` withdraws leaves its topic resting for `TOPIC_REST_DAYS` (rival claims) or dismissed (off the site's topic), never `queued`.
