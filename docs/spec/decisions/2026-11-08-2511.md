2026-11-08  **Only a harm check stops a page; the week takes the highest-value topics in value order** (owner, #2511, verbatim: "The following week rules are clearly too constraining and worry this might be where we end up failing 3 times this week. I would expand or remove these significantly i.e. the highest value pages should be published with priority even if these are of the same type or adjacent or other. Publish - Nine checks first also seems like a huge area where things might fail. I can clearly see from the conditions within the week rules, writing, review and publishing process how many content pieces can easily fall between the cracks and fail. we need much less conditions and constraints in the app. The high value topics are choosen, research is performed and content is written. Everything ele are areas for clear failure."; and "Accept the defaults for #2511"). Decision: rank by value, take the top N, research, write, publish. A page is stopped only by a harm check: the kill switch, paid access and the customer's switch, a broken or missing destination, the do-not-claim list, an invented or unsourced fact or a defamation of a rival, spam or a broken page, a near-duplicate, and the same intent as an existing page. Every other rule (`title`, `plain_prose`, `distinct_headings`, `required_blocks`, `first_block_answers`, `cites_market`) is a warning: recorded on the draft and shown in its Checks list, never a stop, and never the reason for a fix call. A title fault that states a figure (`description_figures`, `figure_owner`) is an unsourced figure and stays a stop. `fit_claim`, `rival_source` and `grounding` are repaired by deleting the claim, and until that repair is built they stop. The defaults accepted for the seven open questions: (1) the fit floor stays fit >= 2, with no multiplier; (2) a page with the same intent as an existing page becomes an update of that page; (3) the value score stays `rank/points.ts`; (4) publishing is blocked only on a noindex or a canonical pointing elsewhere in the page ReachKit writes, and a sitemap or status miss after publishing is a warning with a retry; (5) the `NEW_SITE` cluster fill is deleted; (6) title and description length and banned phrases are warnings, code clamps the length, and D51 is superseded; (7) the veto window is 24 h from when the draft appears on the dashboard, not from when the mail is sent. Supersedes D26, D36, D51, D52 and #1127, and §6 lines 241, 245 and 341 and §7 lines 368, 415, 429 and 506 of `docs/SPEC.md`. Kept: `ceiling_day`, `claim_recheck`, `ceiling_unreadable` as a retry, `PIPELINE.fixBeforeMs` and `DAY_TOPICS_MAX`. Publishing waits for no mail, no week ceiling and no time zone: the draft-ready mail is best-effort, retried by the hourly tick while the window runs, and an unsent one is logged; one page a day caps a week, so the week ceiling is gone; setup stores the browser's zone, else the site's country's where all its zones keep one clock, else UTC, and a site with no zone publishes in UTC.
