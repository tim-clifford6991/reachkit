# Where can I find reviews of the best SEO software?

## Where can you find reviews of the best SEO software?

SEO software comes in distinct categories, each built to solve a different problem.

## What matters most when you're choosing SEO tools

**What problem are you solving?** The best tool depends on whether you need to track rankings, analyze rivals, understand where buyers are sent by AI and search, or publish pages that close the gaps. A ranking tracker tells you if your position moved. A competitor analyzer shows you what keywords they target. A content publisher helps you fill the market with pages. But tools that focus only on tracking or analyzing won't tell you why buyers find your rivals instead of you—or how to change that.

**Are you measuring what matters now?** Search engines and AI systems now answer questions directly, often without sending a click to your site. A tool that only tracks search rankings won't show you whether AI answers name you when buyers research your market. You need to see where AI sends buyers, what your competitors rank for in AI answers, and where the gaps are—the questions your market asks that no one is answering yet.

**Can the tool act on what it finds?** Some software tells you what's wrong. The best software helps you fix it. If a tool identifies a visibility gap but leaves you to write the page yourself, you'll spend weeks catching up. If it closes the gap for you—by writing and publishing one page a day—you'll move faster than competitors still planning their next piece.

**What fits your team's size?** Small teams need tools that do the work for them. Large teams can manage more moving parts. The right choice depends on whether you want a dashboard to check daily or a system that runs on its own and reports what changed each week.
