# Trying ReachKit — what we ask you to do

Thank you for doing this. We want to see whether someone who has never seen ReachKit can use it on
their own. Nobody will help you while you do it. That is on purpose: if you get stuck, that is what
we need to learn. Write it down and carry on as far as you can.

You will pay with your own card. **We refund the full amount afterwards.**

This takes two or three days. Most of that time you are waiting. The steps where you do something
take about an hour altogether.

## What to do

1. Go to **reachkit.app** in your usual browser.
2. Get a free check of your own website.
3. Read what it tells you about your site. Take as long as you like.
4. Sign up and pay.
5. Finish setting up your account.
6. Get ReachKit's first page live on **your own website's address**. Wait for it if you have to,
   and check that it is there.
7. When ReachKit emails you about the **next** page, stop that page from the email.

That is everything. Stop there.

## While you do it

- Use the findings form, one section per step. For each step, write what you expected, what
  happened, and roughly how long it took.
- If something surprises you, confuses you or annoys you, take a screenshot and write one line.
  Small things count.
- If you cannot finish a step, write where you stopped and why. Then stop. Do not ask us how.
- Write your notes during the walk, not from memory the next day.

When you are done, fill in the last part of the form (would you pay for this?) and send it back
with your screenshots.
