## What does the discoverability score measure?

The discoverability score is a number out of 100 that ReachKit assigns to a site after scanning it. It stands in for one question: if a buyer asks AI or Google what to use in your market, does your business show up?

A low score means rivals are named instead of you. One scan result put it plainly:

> 47 /100 Hard to find — and we'll show you the fixes that move it. [ReachKit sign in](https://reachkit.app/signin)

That 47 isn't a grade on your website's design or its traffic. It's a read on whether AI answers and search results point buyers toward you or away from you.

## How does ReachKit calculate the score?

ReachKit runs a scan that reads your site, reads your access rules, and finds your market and rivals. From there it checks the 12 biggest searches buyers make and asks AI the 12 questions those buyers ask, then scores what it finds.

The scan analyzes three things: answerability, presence, and foundations. Answerability asks whether your pages answer buyer questions. Presence asks whether AI names you in its answers. Foundations checks things like whether AI readers are blocked from your site. Together these roll up into the single score.

The score isn't static. One account showed a discoverability score of 62, marked up 8 points in a week, alongside AI answers at 2 out of 12 and 17 pages published. That's the loop: scan, score, publish, rescan.

## What does a real score look like over time?

One tracked account showed searches-you-appear-in climbing week by week: 12, then 31, then 44, then 61, then 96, then 128. Over that same stretch the discoverability score sat at 62, marked up 8 points, with AI answers at 2 out of 12 and 17 pages published. That pairing is the point: the score moves alongside pages going live, not on its own.

A separate example showed a score of 47 out of 100, flagged as hard to find, with fixes shown next to it rather than left as a bare number.

## What else should you read alongside the discoverability score?

The discoverability score sits next to a few other things ReachKit measures and does. AI answers counts how many of the 12 tracked questions name you. Pages published counts how many pages ReachKit has written and put live for your site, at a rate of one a day. Weekly re-measurement keeps the score current as your market and rivals shift.
