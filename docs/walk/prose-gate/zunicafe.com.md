# Does Zuni Café take reservations?

:::answer
Yes. We take reservations at Zuni Café, either by calling 415-552-2522 during our listed hours or by booking online. Reservations open up to 60 days in advance, and parties of seven or more should call us directly rather than booking online.
:::

:::takeaways
- Zuni Café takes reservations by phone or online.
- Reservations can be made up to 60 days in advance.
- Parties of seven or more need to call rather than book online.
- Phone hours run Tuesday through Sunday, with different windows on different days.
- Zuni Café is at 1658 Market Street in San Francisco, near Civic Center BART.
:::

## Does Zuni Café take reservations?

Yes, we do. The clearest statement of how comes straight from our own booking page.

> To make a reservation, please call us at 415-552-2522 Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday 2:00 pm – 9:30 pm and Friday, Saturday, Sunday 10:30 am – 9:30 pm or you can make a reservation online here. [Zuni Café](https://zunicafe.com/)

That means Monday is the one day phone reservations are not an option, since our listed phone windows run Tuesday through Sunday. If you prefer not to call, the online booking link covers the same calendar. Either way, the reservation lands in the same system our host stand uses, so there is no advantage to choosing one method over the other beyond your own convenience.

## How far ahead can you book a table?

We open our book 60 days before the date you want, and not further out than that. This is stated plainly on our site: "We take reservations up to 60-days in advance." [Zuni Café](https://zunicafe.com/)

If you are planning around a specific event, such as a show at Davies Symphony Hall or the Opera House nearby, mark your calendar for 60 days out and call or book online as close to that morning as you can, since popular evening slots move fast. There is no advance-deposit system mentioned on our booking page, so a reservation is simply held under your name until your arrival.

## What if you have a large group?

Groups of seven or more cannot book through the standard online form or the regular phone line process. As we state directly, "For parties of seven or more please call us for information." [Zuni Café](https://zunicafe.com/)

This is because larger tables often need adjustments to seating or timing that an automated booking flow cannot handle well. Calling lets our staff check what configuration of tables and what time windows work on the night you want, rather than guessing from a form. If your group is six or under, the standard phone hours and online booking link apply without any extra step.

## How Zuni Café itself handles reservations?

We built our reservation process around two paths that both end up in the same book: phone and online. Phone hours run Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday from 2:00 pm to 9:30 pm, and Friday, Saturday, Sunday from 10:30 am to 9:30 pm, matching the service hours of the restaurant itself. The 60-day advance window applies to both the phone line and the online form equally, so neither method gives you earlier access than the other.

Our own view is that calling ahead, rather than waiting for a walk-in table, is the more reliable way to get the night and time you want, since Zuni Café has been operating in the same Market Street location since it opened on February 15, 1979, and demand for evening tables near the Civic Center area is steady. If you are ready to check availability, visit [Zuni Café](https://zunicafe.com/) to call or book your table online.
