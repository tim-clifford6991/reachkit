Patterns in these titles:
- the search phrase in the first 3–5 words;
- a number on listicles and alternatives pages;
- the year in about 90% of commercial titles, in parentheses or after a colon;
- a qualifier after the colon: a proof word ("Tested"), the criteria ("Pricing, Features"), or the audience ("for Small Businesses").

No ranking title uses wording like "questions, answered". Small new domains rank for "Profound alternatives".

## Research findings

**Titles in Google**
- Google rewrites 61.6% of titles; 51–60 characters gets the lowest rewrite rate, and titles over 70 characters are rewritten about 99.9% of the time (Zyppy, 80,959 titles) [DATA].
- Square brackets were rewritten 77.6% of the time [DATA].
- 40–60-character titles get 8.9% higher CTR; power words cut CTR (Backlinko, 4M results) [DATA].
- Google rewrites boilerplate, out-of-date years and inaccurate titles, and uses the H1 to build its title link (developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link).

**AI citation**
- Pages ChatGPT cites have titles closer to the prompt: similarity 0.602 vs 0.484 for uncited pages, 0.656 against fan-out queries. Natural slugs are cited 89.8% vs 81.1% (Ahrefs, 1.4M prompts) [DATA].
- Only about 12% of URLs ChatGPT, Gemini and Copilot cite rank in Google's top 10 (Ahrefs) [DATA]. Specific long-tail pages can be cited without ranking.
- Listicles get about 22% of all citations and about 40% for commercial queries (Wix, 1M+ citations) [DATA].
- "X vs Y" titles lead in ChatGPT, "how to" in AI Mode and Perplexity, "what is" in AI Overviews (HubSpot State of AEO 2026) [VENDOR].
- 44.2% of ChatGPT citations come from the first 30% of a page (Growth Memo, 18k citations) [DATA].
- Statistics +31% and quotations +41% (Princeton GEO, KDD 2024) [DATA, lab].
- Cited pages are fresher (Ahrefs, 17M citations) [DATA].

**Off-page**
- Branded web mentions correlate 0.664 with AI Overview visibility; backlinks 0.218 (Ahrefs, 75k brands) [DATA, correlational].
- Only 5.7% of new pages reach Google's top 10 within a year [DATA].

**Risk**
- After Google's December 2025 core update, SaaS sites with templated self-ranking "best X", alternatives and vs pages lost 29–49% of visibility (Lily Ray) [DATA, observational].
- 69% of self-promotional listicles that AI Overviews cited did not get the author recommended [DATA].
- Google's guidance (updated 2026-10-01) asks that AI-written titles and descriptions be fact-checked.

**Vocabulary**
- Small business owners describe the outcome ("get recommended by ChatGPT", "show up in AI Overviews"). Vendors say "AEO" and "AI visibility".
- Avoid "LLMO", "share of voice" and "LLM visibility" in titles. Spell out GEO or AEO, or pair the acronym with "AI search" or "ChatGPT".
- Use "optimisation" or "optimization" to match the customer's locale, never mixed.
