Issue 1454, step 1. It cost 0¢. It used only stored data:

- **Production, read-only, through the Supabase connector.** The latest stored report of every scanned domain (96 domains: 95 free scans and reachkit.app's weekly pass), read from `scans.report` → `market.value.profile` (category, job, offering type, audience terms, town, language), `locale`, `marketFrom` and the report's rival candidates. Also `site_profiles` (name, products) and `sites.market` (the one paying site, reachkit.app). Nothing was written.
- **The repository.** The free scans of local businesses run by issues 1438 and 1441 (`docs/walk/free/2026-10-15.json`, `2026-10-15-1441-after.json`), and the paid first passes of the panel in `docs/walk/panel/*.json` (latest run per site).

reachkit.app is one row, with no special weight.

## How each cell is scored

| Mark | Means |
|---|---|
| ✓ | right |
| ~ | partial: true but incomplete, or right only after a display-time repair |
| ✗ | wrong |
| – | missing: nothing stored |

| Dimension | Read from | ✓ when |
|---|---|---|
| **Sells** | profile `category` (paid: market `category`) | the category names what the business sells, and **every** line it sells. A business with several lines reduced to one category is ~. |
| **Buyers** | profile `audienceTerms` (paid: market `buyer`) | the terms name who really buys |
| **Where** | profile `town`, report `locale`, (paid: market `serves`, `area`) | local: the town is stored. National or online: the country is right **and** the reach is stated. A free scan never states a reach, so a right country alone is ~. |
| **Kind** | (paid: market `kind`) | the kind is stored and right. The free scan stores no kind. Its free-text `offeringType` ("SaaS platform") is not a kind any stage can read. |
| **Name** | `site_profiles.site_name` | the stored name is the brand. ~ where the stored value is a page title and the display rule of issues 1380/1384 cuts it to the brand on screen. ✗ where the stored value is not the business at all. |
| **Rivals** | the report's top-ten rival candidates (paid: concluded rivals) | at least 3 of the 5 really compete for the same buyer. ~ where 1–2 do; ✗ where none do (publishers, dictionaries, a namesake). |
| **Language** | report `locale`, profile `language` | the language its buyers search in |

## Failure patterns, most sites first

Counted over the 96 production reports and the 10 local free scans: 106 free-scan readings. The 11 paid passes are counted only where they disagree with a free scan.

| # | Pattern | Sites | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No kind and no reach.** The free scan stores no business kind and does not say whether it sells locally, nationally or online. Its only signal is a town, and a town is asked only of businesses served in person. The deep pass knows both (`kind`, `serves`). The free scan, the report and the gallery do not. | **106 of 106** | every free scan |
| 2 | **The rivals are not competitors.** Of the 5 top-ten candidates, at most 2 compete for the same buyer: publishers, review sites, booking platforms, dictionaries or a namesake. | **51** (~ 41, ✗ 10) | drift.com, notion.so, webflow.com, zunicafe.com, laynescafe.co.uk |
| 3 | **Several lines reduced to one category.** A business that sells several lines gets one category, or one string that joins two or three lines with "and" or commas. Its searches and its rivals then cover one slice. | **31** | hubspot.com, zoho.com, atlassian.com, deel.com, alexander.co.uk, baeckerei-hinkel.de |
| 4 | **The stored name is not the brand.** A page title, a call to action, a section or a protection's page. 20 are repaired on screen by the issue 1380/1384 rule. 4 are not the business at all, and 5 have no name. | **29** | hotjar.com "Sign up for free", deel.com "HRIS", freshworks.com "Just a moment..." |
| 5 | **No market at all.** The profile was never read, so there is no category, no buyers and no rivals. | **9** | hiverhq.com, trello.com, personio.de, manchester-plumber.com |
| 6 | **Where is wrong.** The wrong country, or an online seller measured as local. | **3** | stripe.com (Australia), roselinecoffee.com (online roaster, "near me"), baeckerei-hinkel.de (no town) |

| 8 | **The free scan and the paid pass disagree** on the same business: what it sells, who buys, where, and the rivals. ### What the counts say

- **The paid pass already understands a business well.** On the panel's 11 paid passes, every dimension is ✓ except 2 cells. It reads a kind (`src/lib/market/kinds.ts`), where the business serves, a buyer, and rivals filtered by kind and place. **The free scan reads less, and reads it separately.** That gap explains patterns 1 and 8 directly, and most of 2. The free scan has no kind or reach to filter rivals by, so a café's top ten counts OpenTable and a city guide as rivals.
- **Pattern 3 is the largest one the model could answer from the page it already reads.** The free profile call is asked for one `category`, so a multi-line business can only answer with one line or a joined string.
- Patterns 4, 5 and 7 have their own issues and fixes on `main` (1379, 1380, 1384, 1387, 1427, 1450). Those fixes run when a report is read, or on the site's next scan. This issue does not repeat them.
- Pattern 2's local half is issue 1449 (local competitors) and is not repeated here. What this issue adds is what that filter needs on every free scan: a kind and a reach.

## What is fixed first (issue 1454, step 2)

1. **Kind and reach on every profile** (pattern 1, 106 sites). The one profile call answers them. `kind` uses the closed list the paid pass already uses (`BUSINESS_KINDS`): local service, shop, hospitality, professional services and agencies, software and online services, creators. `reach` is local, national or international. A town is kept only for a local reach.
2. **One to four offerings, each with its own category and weight** (pattern 3, 31 sites), read in the same call. Selection spreads the searches across them in proportion to their weight. Every offering's category is also a seed and a relevance term.
3. **One reading shared by every surface** (pattern 8): `src/lib/business/understanding.ts`. For a paying site, the customer's own edits come first, then the paid pass's market, then the free profile. A free report reads the profile alone. The report and the app read this one function. The writer already reads the same rows in the same order: the market in force (`writingMarket`), then the customer's confirmed profile. A customer's edits now also reach selection (`profileWithEdits`).
4. **Shown plainly.** The free report and the app overview show one block, *What we read*: name, what you sell, who buys, and where.

Where the stored data allows it, the reading is re-derived when a report is read, at 0¢. A stored town is read as a local reach. A paid market's `kind` and `serves` are read as its kind and reach. Offerings, and a kind or reach on a free scan without a town, need the profile call again, so they wait for each site's next scan. Nothing is bought for them.
