# ReachKit — MVP Specification

The nine features below are the product. Anything not needed by one of them is out of scope (§11).
UI is `docs/DESIGN.md` — this file does not describe pixels. What is on each screen, in what order and with what wording, is that screen's board named there: structure and wording, never a pixel baseline.
Every customer-visible sentence is a key in `src/lib/presentation/copy/keys/`. A missing sentence is drafted and shipped, never `TODO(copy)`; the owner corrects the wording (owner, 2026-09-16, #759). A mail carrying the marker still sends nothing.
Pinned numbers live in `src/lib/config/constants.ts`. Each section states the rule that is current; a line a later ruling replaced is deleted, and git keeps the history.
A new dated ruling is its own file under `docs/spec/decisions/`, headed by the section it belongs to, and reads as part of that section; `node scripts/spec-decisions.mjs` lists them per section (owner, 2026-10-18, #1525). The lines already in a section stay there.

## §7 Calendar and daily publish

**User gets** One calendar of planned / in review / live / waiting. At most one page a day. Whole draft to read, 24 h to stop it from the mail without signing in. Markdown edit with live preview. Published assets link into their own site.

**Rules**
- Autopilot only: generate → veto → publish. No Copilot, no mode picker, on no screen and in no mail (2026-09-11).
- Veto default 24 h, range 1–7 days, never zero. Same window for updates as for new pages (2026-09-11).
- At most one publish a day, and the plan's week (`WEEK_PLAN.days`, seven) a week. Kinds: new post, new page, update of an existing page.
- Fail closed (2026-09-16, #792): when the day's or week's publishes cannot be counted, the page is held, not published, the log says why, and the next tick asks again.
- Every asset links to real inventory pages — three to six, the ones nearest its topic (2026-10-10, *Links*, below) — and to earlier assets in its cluster. No link known to go nowhere. Drafts follow the stored voice. A fact about the business comes only from their site, the profile or the knowledge base; a fact about the market comes only from a public page cited beside it (2026-09-29).
- A closed Markdown subset (with the 2026-09-20 blocks below); one serialiser for screen, copy-as-HTML and copy-as-Markdown. One fix call before review (D41, below); never after. Edits save with no save button.
- A page is written from its context pack and its type's template, and from nothing else (D41, D42, below). A pack short of what the template asks writes no page.
- Publish is one idempotent call to the destination on the customer’s domain. At +24 h: reachable, indexable, in a sitemap, AI-readable.
- Public veto link redeems on GET once. MVP paid service ends at a page on the customer’s own domain (2026-09-11).
- Opportunity status (2026-09-15): a written draft queues its opportunity; a vetoed draft dismisses it.
- 2026-09-17  Target choice (owner walk, #858): a Write or Earn target's competition band reads the search's own keyword difficulty where the vendor gave one — the measurement of that very top ten — beside the top ten's ranked counts, taking the better of the two; above the difficulty ceiling (§6) the target is outsized for every type. So a cold-start site's long-tail question whose small domains nobody sized is still a winnable or reach target, never an unmeasured one.
- 2026-09-16  Draft edits (owner, #789): the editor saves the title, the body and the meta description. Every save re-runs the stage-4 checks (D41) on the edited text. An edit that breaks a rule is still saved, the page says so, and the page is held from publishing on every route until a later save passes. The state and the veto window do not change.
- Stopped drafts (2026-09-16, #788): a date keeps one draft row across every attempt. A draft the checks still stop after its one fix call moves to needs_attention with the checks that stopped it. Regenerate writes that date's draft again on the next hourly draft tick.
- 2026-10-02  **A stopped draft is repaired from its failures, not re-rolled blind** (#1178, part of #1127; the fix call is D41's). A page a check stops is first repaired in code and checked again, at no model cost: an unlinked number is linked to the pack source that states it beside the same word, or its sentence is dropped; a case-study sentence is dropped; a table's placeholder cell takes its column or its product out (#1135, #1250); an opening list is joined into a paragraph and an over-long opening is split after the sentences that fit. The repair is kept if the page then passes, or (since #1252) if it then fails fewer checks and no new one; the one fix call is told each check the page still fails and the sentences that failed it. Every repair is recorded on the row (`drafts.meta.repairs`), per check, cleared or not. Nothing a repair does writes a word.
- 2026-09-16  (#813) A stage that could not run after the date's row exists ends the same way: it is tried once more, then the row moves to needs_attention naming the stage. No second row; Regenerate restarts it.
- 2026-09-18  **Who decides what, once** (owner ruling, #884: *"right-sizing should only occur once, during the deep scan. The calendar content generation should occur based on the information that is passed to it"*). Three layers, and no rule lives in two:
  - **The pass** measures and stores. The ceilings of §6 are applied while it selects its questions, and what it measured — volume, keyword difficulty, the site's own footprint, each rival's size — is written into the report. Nothing downstream re-measures.
  - **Derivation** decides and stores. It bands every target against the site's footprint (`fit_band`) and records whether the target may fill a publishing day at all (`ready`, `unready_reason`) — including §6's right-sizing law, whose reason is `outsized`. A row banded under an older policy is brought up to date from its own stored evidence on the next pass; no vendor call is made to do it.
  - **The calendar, the ranking and the daily job** only read. They order and render what the row already states; none of them re-applies a bar, re-bands a target or holds a filter of its own. A row that reaches the ranked list is a row the stored answer called ready, and a disagreement is a defect to be asserted against, not repaired at read time.
- 2026-09-18  A `not-yet` target never fills a day (owner's dogfood, #881; the enforcement half of §6's right-sizing law). It is excluded from the ranked list rather than weighed 0 in it — a zero weight orders and does not exclude, and where every candidate scored 0 the tie-break handed the day to the biggest keyword: ReachKit wrote "best seo software" (1 000/mo) for a site ranking for 3. An outsized row stays on file, because the site may grow into it, but it is not a day of supply and is not counted towards pages planned; a site whose every target is outsized has zero days of pages, and the calendar and Overview say that is why rather than calling the market used up or unmeasured. A re-measure adds rather than replaces, so a pass's right-sized candidate now takes the place of an outsized row already held on the same topic — a queued row still survives, since its page is already being written.
- 2026-10-02  **A stopped draft frees its date** (owner, D26, #1186; rewrites #833 of 2026-09-17). When a draft still fails after its fix call, the same run tries the next-ranked opportunity for the same date, at most once more, inside `CAPS.DRAFT_C`. The founder still resolves a stopped draft from the draft: Regenerate rewrites the one row; Skip rests its topic as above.
- 2026-10-03  How D26 is carried out (#1179). *Stopped* is either cause that rests a row: the checks after the fix call, or a stage that could not run twice (#813). A fix page, which takes one attempt, counts the same. The next topic is tried only where the stopped page wrote a row and the day's `CAPS.DRAFT_C` is not spent; both pages are paid from that one ceiling, and each row records its own cost. The stopped row gives up its date (`scheduled_for` is empty) only when the run goes on to the next topic, so a stopped page nothing replaced still shows on its day, and a second stop holds the date — a re-delivered tick writes no third. A resting topic stays on file, open and not ready (`resting`, `opportunities.rest_until`), and is not counted as a day of supply; the rest is counted from the stop, and again from a Skip. A rewrite or an edit that passes queues the topic as any written draft does. A page skipped after it entered review still dismisses its topic (#712).
- 2026-10-02  **What the hard rules are for** (owner, D27, #1186): *"If a draft has failed twice, i would be highly concerned to why. the pass criteria should mainly be there to guard the content for obvious violations, and sticking to a structure."* The hard rules exist to stop obvious violations — invented facts, people or reviews; false claims about the business; adverts posing as answers; unsafe markup — and to enforce the page structure. They are not stylistic filters, and a rule that stops good pages is a defect in the rule. A draft that fails twice is an alert to investigate, never a normal outcome: its causes are reported per week (#1184), and the rules are reviewed against this purpose (#1178 and the issue that review files).
- 2026-10-03  **The rules, reviewed against that purpose** (#1187; D27 above, and D34: stored drafts and stored evaluation pages only, nothing spent). Each rule states what it guards and was re-run on the 12 stored drafts and the 10 kept evaluation drafts; the table and every reading are `docs/walk/rule-review.md`, rewritten by `npm run eval:content -- --rules`. - 2026-10-02  Fixing a stopped page (owner, #1180; D26 above): the draft screen of a page that rests in needs_attention without ever entering review offers *Edit*, *Write it again* and *Skip* — the exception to #882's "every other stage opens as a read". - 2026-09-16  Daily decision (owner, #777): each day chooses between a *new post* (Write/Earn) and an *update of an existing page* (Improve/Fix), whichever ranks higher for this site. Update candidates are the site’s own pages — its ranked URLs and crawled inventory, not only the home page — matched to the market’s questions; both sides are right-sized per §6. - 2026-09-16  (#781) An update is offered only where the site's destination can deliver it: otherwise it is not ready (`destination_cannot_address`) and never fills a day. - 2026-09-17  (#855) A `fix_page` the site's destination cannot deliver — every one on a hosted-only site — is never the day's work and never supply: it is stored not ready (`destination_cannot_address`), no draft is written from it, and supply depth does not count it.
