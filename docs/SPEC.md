# ReachKit — MVP Specification

The nine features below are the product. Anything not needed by one of them is out of scope (§11).
UI is `docs/DESIGN.md` — this file does not describe pixels. What is on each screen, in what order and with what wording, is that screen's board named there: structure and wording, never a pixel baseline.
Every customer-visible sentence is a key in `src/lib/presentation/copy/keys/`. A missing sentence is drafted and shipped, never `TODO(copy)`; the owner corrects the wording (owner, 2026-09-16, #759). A mail whose subject or body is empty sends nothing.
Pinned numbers live in `src/lib/config/constants.ts`. Each section states the rule that is current; a line a later ruling replaced is deleted, and git keeps the history.
A new dated ruling is its own file under `docs/spec/decisions/`, headed by the section it belongs to, and reads as part of that section; `node scripts/spec-decisions.mjs` lists them per section (owner, 2026-10-18, #1525). The lines already in a section stay there.

## §0 Terms

**Glossary.** The words every section uses, and the goal they serve.

- **The goal.** Every change is judged by one result: each customer gets content specific to their business, published every day, on its own topics, on what is rising in its market, or on what its competitors win. No customer day passes without new content or an update of a live page; customer copy still promises only "up to 7 pages a week, new or updated" (A1) until the owner digest shows 7 of 7 days kept for two weeks running (owner, 2026-11-17, 4(c)). (owner, 2026-09-16; owner, 2026-11-17, #2786; owner, 2026-11-17, #2722)
- **Current focus.** The work in hand is the measurement: a site's scan returns the correct competitors, market, keywords to work towards and AI answers to optimise for (§6). That context and the site's own information build the daily page (§7). (owner D35, 2026-10-03, #1210)
- **Every kind of customer.** A rule in market understanding, the knowledge base, competitors, terms, measured searches or topics is general: it holds for a plumber, a restaurant, a shop and a German bakery as it holds for reachkit.app. A change to these stages passes the customer panel (`npm run panel`) before the owner is asked to measure. (owner, 2026-10-07, #1304)
- **SEO / GEO.** Google organic findability of the customer's domain / whether ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews name them. (SPEC, 2026-09-16)
- **AI.** The customer-facing name of the AI measure: the tab, the report section, the mail section and the verdict bar are all "AI". "GEO" is a gloss, once on the landing and once in the report's method, never a label. A sentence about what the engines do says "AI answers". Routes, keys and types keep `ai-answers`. A line elsewhere that says "AI answers tab" or "AI Visibility" means this tab. (owner, 2026-10-15, #1444)
- **Visibility Score.** One 0–100 number: how easily buyers find you on Google and in AI. `Score = round(∛(Foundations × Answerability × Presence))`, `Presence = max(1, 0.5 × SearchPresence + 0.5 × AIPresence)`, over the halves that were read (§2). Each factor 0–100. Bands: invisible 0 · hard-to-find 25 · findable 50 · dominant 75. A score always carries its range. The factors are **Readiness** (code `foundations`) and **Content** (code `answerability`); Presence shows as its halves **AI** (`AIPresence`) and **Google** (`SearchPresence`), so the verdict draws four bars, AI before Google, an order the score does not read; the formula is unchanged by the names. Readiness or Content unmeasured means no score. Shown on the free report only; the app does not show it (§4). (owner, 2026-10-15, #1444, #1457; range E9, 2026-10-24; AI first, owner, 2026-11-16, #2739; free report only, owner, 2026-11-16, #2737)
- **AI bar counts what the AI card counts.** An AI answer counts for the site where it mentioned or linked it. That one predicate, `namesOrLinks` in `src/lib/measure/drivers.ts`, decides the AI bar, the AI card's x of N and its row marks. `AIPresence = max(1, 100 × answers that mentioned or linked the site ÷ answered AI Overviews read)`. On the free report the AI bar is `round(10 × x ÷ N)` of the card beside it; on a paid scan the page questions' AI Overview cells stay in AIPresence. An unread AI is never 0, and AI answering none of at least 4 searches leaves Presence to search presence. The range is the Wilson interval of the one rate. A mention is §2 *What a mention is*; a link to the site counts only to a page it serves (its stored crawl or a live ReachKit page; none stored keeps every link). (ux-designer and seo-master, owner-delegated E5, 2026-10-24, #1622; owner ruling 73, 2026-11-22, #3222)
- **Market.** What the business is judged in; customer text says "market", never "category". It fixes the twelve searches, the competitors and the volumes. For a paying site ReachKit identifies and maintains it from evidence, re-checked each scan; the founder may override it but never has to. The free report's visitor may correct it once (§2). (owner D28, 2026-10-02, #1186; words #1444)
- **Site profile.** Page inventory (URL, title, h1, purpose), site name, products and claims, brand-voice summary. Up to 100 pages from the sitemap and internal links. The free scan stores inventory and name; voice is paid-only, shown at setup. (SPEC, 2026-09-12)
- **Competitor (code: rival).** Customer text says "competitors", never "rivals", and "market leaders" where it means the same. One of the 3–5 real competitors ReachKit identifies from evidence and re-checks once a month; the founder may override them but never has to (§5). A publisher, aggregator or platform in a top ten is a source, not a competitor. (owner D24, D28, 2026-10-02, #1161, #1186; monthly #1700; words #1444)
- **Sources are not competitors.** A government's, army's, public service's, university's or school's host, read by its suffix, is never a competitor wherever it ranks. A job page is read by its host, address or title. A job seeker's search, one whose titled top ten is at least half job pages, gives no competitor and proposes no Write page on the free report or the paid plan; a skipped search counts as no measurement, never a zero. A business whose own category is about jobs, recruiting, staffing or training keeps those searches. A source row (`isSourceRow`, `src/lib/market/rivals/domains.ts`) is one reading for every holder, and the Google table's "Who's first" names the first business on the first page, never a source: the holder the missing-page cards name (`firstBusinessRow`). No list of hosts is added. (seo-master and architect, 2026-10-31, #2194 (D24 extension; no owner ruling needed))
- **Search (code: question).** One of the twelve buyer searches the scan measures, called "searches" in customer text, never "questions": searches this business could answer, chosen by business fit first and demand second, right-sized per §6. They stay the Google searches; the AI measure asks tracked buyer prompts instead. (SPEC, 2026-09-16, 2026-09-29; owner D19, 2026-10-03; words #1444)
- **Tracked buyer prompt.** One of the site's 24 AI panel items, drawn by rule from the Google searches it is measured on (§6 *Tracked buyer prompts*) and asked every week; in the app, "questions we ask AI". The AI measure is the share of their answers that mention or link the site, a 4-week rolling mention rate with its margin, never an AI position (§6). (owner D19, 2026-10-03; words #1444; owner ruling 73, 2026-11-22, #3220)
- **Business fit.** 0–3, how much the business's own offering is the answer to a search: 3 it is the answer, 2 it plainly helps, 1 it can only be mentioned, 0 no relation. Judged against the knowledge base (§6). (SPEC, 2026-09-29)
- **Knowledge base.** What the business sells and how: each offering with its features, prices and uses; who it serves; the problems it solves; how it differs from each competitor; its proof; its buyers' common questions; integrations and places. Every item carries the page it was read from, or says the founder gave it (§5). (SPEC, 2026-09-29, #1133)
- **Footprint.** How many keywords the site itself ranks for, and each competitor's count: the presence right-sizing scales with (§6), not the score's Presence factor. An internal input only, never shown as a score or a lead. (SPEC, 2026-09-16; owner D22, D24, 2026-10-02)
- **Opportunity.** Write (missing page) · Improve (owned URL) · Fix (technical) · Earn (first-party citable asset on their domain, never outreach). (SPEC)
- **Asset.** What one day publishes: a new post, a new page, or an update to an existing page. At most one a day. (SPEC)
- **Veto.** A finished page waits, visible and stoppable, before it publishes. Default 24 h, range 1–7 days, never zero for a customer; the owner's own sites take 0 h. (SPEC; owner, 2026-11-17, #2722)

### How the seven guidelines are met today

What the writer and its code do now under each guideline above. It folds into the code that does it once milestone #3313's proof writes are read.

- *About this business*:
  - **A new page adds what the web does not say** (§7). Before the write call, code counts the pack's facts whose words are on none of the top results read. A new page needs three (`ADDS`); none need be a business fact. Short of that it is written with what it holds and logs `adds` in its `pack_short`; it is never stopped or turned into an update. The counted facts are listed under *what this page adds*. An update is not held to it. (owner D12, 2026-09-29, #1143; owner, 2026-10-10, #1393; owner, 2026-11-08, #2511; owner ruling 100.2, 2026-11-23, #2722)
  - **A page is written for the current market** (§7). The pack reads the market from `sites.market`, the founder's own category and rivals in their place; the report's category only where no market is stored. The draft records it (`drafts.meta.market`). (2026-10-04, #1248)
- *Answers deeply*:
  - **Five stages** (§7). (1) Decide, in code: new or update, and the type. (2) Context pack: at most two research model calls, Sonnet 5.5 thinking between tools, a call that times out tried once more as two halves, stored whole with the draft (`drafts.meta.pack`). (3) Write: one call, Opus 5.5 at low effort, from the type's template and the pack only. (4) Check: code, plus at most one fix call. (5) Publish: the window, then live. Beside the four calls, a listicle or comparison makes one `fit_claim` call and current insights one news judgement, all inside `CAPS.DRAFT_C`. The target's SERP is bought once, before research reads anything: its organic rows are the top results where the scan stored none, and its "People also ask" and related searches are the buyer questions. It and the study or guidance search are bought on the normal standard queue, whose links are the pages (a live or high-priority SERP links only Google's `goto` redirects): the daily job posts them in a step of its own before the write and collects them in later steps, about 45 minutes in all, and the write reads them at 0¢. A read not ready by then is no research, never a live SERP; a `google.com` row is never read. The brief, outline, per-section calls, critic and every model-judged rule are retired.
