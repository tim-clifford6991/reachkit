Unblock the AI readers
Open the robots.txt file at the root of your site — your host or CMS usually has a setting for it. Find the rules that block the AI readers named on the card and add the lines shown there, so each reader is allowed. Save and publish; the readers pick up the change on their next visit. If your home page carries a noindex rule in its head, remove that too — it tells every reader, Google included, to ignore the page.
