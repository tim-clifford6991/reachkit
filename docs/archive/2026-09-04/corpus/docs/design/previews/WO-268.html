0b · Band system — ADR-093 (status: proposed)

Cited as the stated band law, not accepted — ADR-093 carries status: proposed. Decision 1: three bands, compact / medium / wide, two boundaries, closed set. Decision 2: the floor of the supported range is 320px. Decision 3 (LANDMINE): text is never shrunk to fit, and no type step below --t-floor (11px) may be minted. Decision 6: containment and no-clipping are checked at five widths, including each boundary.

Which boundary tokens this sheet reads the two bands off. ADR-093 names the set and its constraints but leaves “the token names and the two pixel values” to design/. tokens.md §2b already derives four breakpoints under rule 1.1 for a different obligation (the three narrow-viewport surfaces), and two of them satisfy ADR-093's own arithmetic exactly: --breakpoint-lg (1024px) is where “the sidebar returns” — ADR-093's compact/medium boundary, since Decision 1's table keys the sidebar's visibility to exactly that transition — and --breakpoint-xl (1280px) is where “the day panel sits beside the grid,” ADR-093's own description of where wide begins. No new token is minted for this sheet; this is a reading of two existing ones, recorded here rather than silently assumed, and it is a drawing choice, not a ruling.

Which of the fifteen actually has a registered band arm. None of BP-018's five edited-by-ADR-093 obligations (vocabulary, the suite, Surface, the floor) gives an individual primitive a per-band layout rule — Decision 5's card-grid collapse and the calendar/day-panel arms bind the four custom surfaces and the surface blueprints, not the fifteen atomic controls here. Of the fifteen, exactly one has a band-relevant behaviour that is already part of its registered contract: Table's overflow-x-auto wrap, which is what keeps it inside its own box at every width without a second markup path (drawn in §7 below, at all three bands). The remaining fourteen are single-instance atomic controls with no registered band arm; each section below states this in one line rather than inventing a reflow no artifact specifies, and confirms its own text stays at or above --t-floor at every band (§16).
