# REQ-022 — €49 a month, VAT included, one price, and what checkout records

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder deciding whether to buy, I want one flat monthly price, tax
included, with nothing metered behind it and my business details recorded as I
gave them, so that I know exactly what ReachKit costs me before I pay and every
month after, and my invoices are right for my accounts from the first payment
onward.

## Rationale
The founder's decision rests on a number they can hold in their head: €49 a
month, VAT included, flat, with nothing metered behind it, so the product is
never something they have to ration — which is the opposite of what it is for.
The €49 is the whole of what the customer owes, so the bill they signed up to
never rises when the tax treatment behind it changes. Most buyers are businesses
whose bookkeeping needs a VAT identity on the invoice, and a record that was
never captured cannot be reconstructed later, so the country of the billing
address they gave and the VAT number they entered sit on their billing record and
their invoices from the first payment — with no country refused and no registry
check standing between them and buying. This requirement is the single home for
the price and for what checkout records; every surface that states the price
cites here, and where the customer later corrects the VAT number is REQ-076
criterion 9's (`BUILD.md` §13, §4.1). Placement: JN-002 step 1.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a founder on any surface that states the price, when they read it, then
   it reads €49 per month and says that VAT is included, and €49 is the amount
   checkout charges.
2. Given an active customer, when their month elapses, then €49 is charged
   again, with no per-page, per-scan, per-competitor or any other usage-based
   amount added to it, whatever they used that month.
3. Given a founder at checkout, when they look for an alternative plan, then
   there is exactly one — no tiers, no seats, no annual variant, no add-ons.
4. Given a buyer in any country, when they reach the amount payable and every
   time they are charged after, then it is €49 in euro — the same amount and the
   same currency wherever they are, never converted to a local one — with no tax
   amount added to it or taken from it, any VAT due being contained within the
   €49, so no customer's bill rises because tax registration was set up.
5. Given a founder at checkout, when they pay, then the country of the billing
   address they gave at checkout is recorded against their billing record, and
   no country they give refuses them the purchase.
6. Given a business buyer at checkout, when they have a VAT number, then a field
   exists to enter it, and what they enter appears on their billing record and
   their invoices.
7. Given a buyer with no VAT number, when they leave that field empty, then the
   purchase completes unimpeded; and given a buyer who enters one, when they
   submit it, then it is recorded and the purchase completes whatever any
   registry would say about that number — no check against VIES or any other
   registry stands between them and their purchase.

## Non-goals
- Annual or multi-month billing, per-seat pricing, or a second plan — criterion
  3 carries the customer's promise.
- Trials, coupons, discount codes, or promotional pricing.
- Charging in any currency other than EUR — criterion 4 carries it.
- Overage charges, credits, or metered usage of any kind — criterion 2 carries
  what may never be added to the €49.
- Calculating, charging, displaying or remitting VAT, or showing a tax line
  beside the €49 — criteria 1 and 4 carry what the customer reads and pays, and
  criteria 5 to 7 what is recorded.
- Validating a VAT number against VIES or any registry — criterion 7 carries
  the customer's promise that no such check can block them.
- Reverse-charge or tax-status wording on invoices.
- Country-based pricing (criterion 4), or blocking purchases from any country —
  criterion 5 carries the second.
- Where the customer corrects the VAT number afterwards, and every other billing
  control — plan, price, next invoice, card, invoices and cancellation — all
  REQ-076's.
- Refunds, chargebacks and failed renewals: no requirement states what those
  customers experience, because REQ-076 defers all three to post-launch by owner
  ruling.
- How what the customer gives reaches Stripe, and which of Stripe's fields
  holds it — the blueprint that owns billing decides both.
