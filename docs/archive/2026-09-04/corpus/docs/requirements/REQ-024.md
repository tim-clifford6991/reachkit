# REQ-024 — A completed payment, and nothing else, opens the account

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder who has just paid, I want my way in to arrive by itself, so that I
am never left having paid with nothing to show for it.

## Rationale
Because nothing exists before the money moves (REQ-020), the payment is the only
event that can create the customer — so it must always create them, exactly
once, and nothing that is not a real payment may. A founder who has paid is
never left holding a charge and no way in: not when the link is late, not when
the account fails to open at all, and not when the same person pays twice
(`BUILD.md` §13, §3).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a payment that completes, when it is confirmed, then a sign-in link to
   setup is sent to the address that paid, within 60 seconds, with no further
   action by the founder.
2. Given a payment that did not complete — abandoned, declined, or a claimed
   payment we cannot verify as ours — when anything acts on it, then no account,
   no site and no sign-in email results.
3. Given the address that paid already has an open account, when a further
   completed payment from it is processed — the same payment reported to us
   again, or a second purchase — then once that processing ends the address
   still has exactly one account, one site and one running subscription, no
   further sign-in email and no further deep pass result from it, and a founder
   whose second purchase was charged is told that it bought no second
   subscription in an `account` mail to the address that paid (REQ-064 criterion
   6) naming one way to reach a person.
4. Given a founder who closed the tab or lost the email, when they ask for a new
   sign-in link using the address they paid with, then they are returned to
   where they left off — setup if it is unfinished, otherwise onward into the
   product (REQ-029 criterion 6) — never to a dead end or a second checkout.
5. Given a payment that completed, when 15 minutes have passed since the charge
   and no one has signed in at the address that paid, then that address is
   written to again with an `account` mail (REQ-064 criterion 6): it says the
   payment succeeded,
   carries a working sign-in link where the account is open or says it is not
   open yet where it is not, names one way to reach a person, and never asks for
   payment again.
6. Given a payment that completed against which no account opened, when 24 hours
   have passed since the charge, then an account is open against that payment
   and a sign-in link to it has been sent to the address that paid, with no
   second charge and nothing required of the founder beyond opening that link.

## Non-goals
- Access that depends on the founder staying on, or returning to, the
  post-payment confirmation page — criteria 1 and 4 carry the customer's
  promise that closing the tab costs them nothing.
- Manual or support-assisted provisioning as the normal path — criteria 1 and 6
  bound how long the founder waits either way.
- Team accounts, invitations, or more than one site per account.
- Password or social-login credentials as an alternative to the emailed link.
- Refunds, chargebacks and failed renewals — everything that can go wrong with
  money after the first payment is deferred past launch by owner ruling, an
  accepted debt on the same footing as the deferred tax handling in `BUILD.md`
  §13 and carrying the same trigger: revisit before meaningful EU B2C volume.
