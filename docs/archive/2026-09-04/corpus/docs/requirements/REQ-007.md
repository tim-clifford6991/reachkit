# REQ-007 — Every one of the 12 questions shows the search it came from

> Superseded by REQ-006, which absorbed this requirement's acceptance criteria
> verbatim as its criteria 8–13 (2026-08-30; see
> `requirements/history/REQ-006-2026-08-30-requirement-cleanup.md`). Retired in
> place; cite REQ-006, not this file — criterion 2, cited across the corpus for
> the provenance line, is now REQ-006 criterion 9.

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder being told these are my market's biggest searches, I want to see
the real search and its monthly volume behind each question, so that I can judge
whether this is actually my market before I believe the verdict.

## Rationale
The 12 questions are the foundation the whole report rests on, so a founder must
be able to check each one against the real search and volume it came from, and
must be shown 12 genuinely different searches rather than one search rephrased —
without that a founder cannot tell a measured market from a guess, and the
correction path (REQ-014) has nothing to act on. The selection scoring, intent
weights and composition constraints are mechanism for the blueprint. Source:
`BUILD.md` §4.1 (the 12 questions list and its provenance line) and §6.7 steps
3–4.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a completed report, when the questions module renders, then all 12
   questions are reachable from the page, with the first four visible and the
   remainder one action away.
2. Given any question, when it renders, then it shows the search it was derived
   from, that search's monthly search volume, and the brands named in its
   answer.
3. Given a question on which no AI answer appeared at all, when its row renders,
   then it states that no AI answer appeared, and it is never presented as the
   customer being absent from an answer.
4. Given a question whose AI answer named no brand, when its provenance line
   renders, then it states that the answer named no brand rather than showing an
   empty list.
5. Given the set of 12, when it renders, then no two of them derive from
   searches that differ only in plural form, word order, punctuation or
   stop-words — the 12 stand for 12 different searches.
6. Given any of the 12 questions, when it renders, then the search shown beside
   it (criterion 2) is the search that question was selected from, and how the
   question is worded never changes which search it stands for: a differently
   worded question is still the same measured search, and no question stands for
   a search the report did not measure.

## Non-goals
- No editing, reordering, or removing of individual questions on the free report
  — the only correction available is the market itself (REQ-014).
- No display of the full ~50-search market set; the report shows the 12.
- No generated commentary or interpretation attached to any question; whether
  the product's own voice may be model-written is REQ-093's rule, cited here and
  not restated.
- No phrasing of a question that changes which search it was selected from
  (`BUILD.md` §6.7) — stated as criterion 6, not left to this list.
