# REQ-009 — The report names three problems and gives the whole method away free

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder who has just been told AI recommends my rivals, I want to see
exactly which three things are wrong and how to fix them myself, so that the
report is worth something to me whether or not I ever pay.

## Rationale
A founder who has just been told AI recommends their rivals must leave knowing
exactly which three things are wrong, how serious each is, and how to fix them
without us — the fix for blocked AI readers copyable exactly as it must be
pasted, because a wrong robots line is worse than none. Giving the method away
is the trust position the free funnel is built on, and a severity a founder
cannot rely on is worse than none: a card that reads the same however large the
problem behind it teaches them to ignore all three. Source: `BUILD.md` §4.1
(three problem cards, "Left border color = severity", DIY collapses), §2.5 ("Red
appears only for *the customer's problem being shown to them*") and §7
(`unblock` — instruction only, never generated, never automated). The source
fixes no severity vocabulary, so criterion 8 fixes the shape of one — three
closed levels, monotone in the count — and leaves the three words themselves to
the owner's copy.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a completed report, when it renders, then it shows three problems — AI
   readers blocked, missing pages, unquotable pages — each with its own count and
   its severity, stated in words and never by colour alone; where a problem could
   not be measured, its count and its severity both read "—" (REQ-004). What
   those words may be is criterion 8's.
2. Given AI readers are blocked on the customer's site, when that card renders,
   then the exact lines the founder must add are shown verbatim and copyable,
   labelled as a free fix the founder does themselves.
3. Given a site with no access gate, when the blocked-readers card renders, then
   it shows a count of 0 and states that nothing is blocking AI readers, and no
   fix lines are offered — the card is never absent and never shows lines a
   founder should not paste.
4. Given a site whose access rules the scan did not read — it tried and could
   not determine them, or it stopped early before attempting them — when the
   blocked-readers card renders, then it shows "—" in place of the count, states
   in writing which of those two happened in the words REQ-004 gives that case
   (criterion 6 for a read that could not be determined, criterion 9 for work the
   scan never got to), and offers no fix lines — it never shows 0, never states
   that nothing is blocking AI readers, and never tells the founder we could not
   read rules we never tried to read.
5. Given the missing-pages and unquotable-pages problems, when they render, then
   each shows a count and names who does the work, and neither lists or previews
   the pages themselves.
6. Given the report, when the visitor reads past the problem cards, then three
   instructional sections — one for each of the three problems — are readable on
   the same page, each stating the steps the founder performs themselves, with
   no payment and no email address asked for.
7. Given any of the three fixes the report names, when the founder acts on it,
   then the report has only instructed them: ReachKit makes no change to their
   site, its files or its access rules, and offers no control that would. Every
   fix on the free report is one the founder performs themselves.
8. Given the severity of any of the three problems, when it renders, then it is
   one of exactly three written levels drawn from one closed set — the same three
   words on every report the product renders, never wording chosen per report or
   per problem — and it follows that problem's own count and nothing else: a
   problem measured at 0 always carries the lowest of the three and states in
   writing that there is nothing to fix, and of any two reports the one with the
   larger count for the same problem never carries the lower severity.

## Non-goals
- No automated or one-click fix of an access gate; fixes are instructions only
  — stated as criterion 7, not left to this list.
- No per-page list, audit table, or export of the missing or unquotable pages on
  the free report.
- Whether any of this instructional text may be model-written rather than
  written by us: REQ-093 is the single home for that rule.
