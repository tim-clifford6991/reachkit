# REQ-065 — Every active site is re-measured once a week, and a week that could not be measured says so

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer, I want my market measured again every week without my
asking, and to be told plainly on a week it could not be measured, so that the
numbers I judge this subscription by are always this week's and never last
week's wearing today's date.

## Rationale
Everything the customer reads weekly — the movement on Overview, the distance to
each rival, the verdict on each published page, the Monday mail — is derived from
one recurring event, so what must be true is that the event happens once a week
for every site with active access, that the values the customer reads are the
ones it produced and carry its date, and that a week it could not fully measure
is said out loud rather than filled in from the week before. BUILD.md §11 fixes
the cadence — "`weekly/refresh` | Mon 06:00 UTC | Weekly scan per active site →
re-derive → verdicts → movement email" — and BUILD.md §10 makes the partial
outcome a first-class one rather than an internal error, the `scans` table
carrying "status(running/done/degraded)", which is why a half-measured week is a
promise this requirement owns and not a fault the customer never hears about. Research on
the DataForSEO endpoints, competing products' measurement practices, and AI-answer stability is in
`registry/evidence/RESEARCH-dataforseo-endpoints.md`,
`registry/evidence/RESEARCH-competitor-visibility-measurement.md`, and
`registry/evidence/RESEARCH-ai-answer-stability.md`.
No implementation exists to touch: this repo carries no `src/`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a site with active access, when a week beginning on Monday in the time
   zone the customer set arrives, then its market is re-measured exactly once
   within that week and never before it begins, without the customer asking; it
   is due on that week's Monday, and it carries the date it was taken in that
   same time zone.
2. Given that week's re-measurement completed, when the customer reads any value
   the product states weekly, then it is the value measured in that week and
   carries that measurement date, and no value from an earlier week is presented
   as this week's.
3. Given a site with active access whose week's re-measurement did not run on its
   due Monday, when the customer opens the product or reads that week's mail,
   then one written line states that this week has not been measured and names
   the day the next measurement is due, and no movement figure or verdict is
   produced for that week; the line stands until a measurement for that week
   completes, and is never replaced by an earlier week's figures.
4. Given that week's re-measurement ran but could not complete every
   measurement, when the customer reads that week, then what was measured is
   shown with its date, what was not measured is stated as not measured, and the
   week is never presented as a complete measurement.
5. Given a site whose access has ended, when the week begins, then it is not
   re-measured, no week is stated to that customer as unmeasured and no next
   measurement is announced to them, and every measurement already taken stays
   readable with the date it was taken.

## Non-goals
- The page-by-page verdicts derived from the re-measurement, and the mail that
  carries them — REQ-063's.
- What Overview shows before any weekly measurement has ever completed —
  REQ-041 criterion 3.
- Re-measuring on demand, on save, or more than once in a week.
- Which searches, questions and competitors the re-measurement runs against —
  REQ-071.
- Which subscription states count as active access — REQ-076's.
- A day the daily generation and publishing loop did not do its work — REQ-092's.
- Any cost, cap or vendor detail of the re-measurement shown to the customer.
- The clock the job runs on: what must hold is one measurement inside each
  customer's own Monday-to-Sunday week, whatever schedule delivers it.
