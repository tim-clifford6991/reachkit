# REQ-078 — The customer can export everything ReachKit wrote for them, always

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a customer — paying, cancelling or already gone — I want to download every
page ReachKit wrote for me in a form I can use elsewhere, so that the content I
paid for is genuinely mine and leaving costs me nothing but the subscription.

## Rationale
The product's claim is that the customer is the publisher of record and the
pages are theirs, so every page ReachKit wrote must be downloadable in a form
that works without ReachKit, at any time and whatever the state of the
subscription — an export that stops working when the card does turns that claim
into lock-in, and it is the one promise a departing customer will test
(BUILD.md §4.7, "**Your content** (pages count, Export everything — always
available)"; §9, "Export = Markdown + assets zip, always available"; §13,
"export always").

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a customer on Settings, when they open "Your content", then they see
   how many pages ReachKit has written for them — published, in review, vetoed
   and failed alike — and a single action that exports all of them.
2. Given a customer whose subscription is active, cancelled, or past its
   paid-through date, when they sign in and request an export, then it
   downloads to them directly and is never withheld on account of subscription
   state; a lapsed customer reaches it by the sign-in link REQ-076 criterion 5
   keeps working.
3. Given an export is produced, when the customer opens it, then it contains
   every page ReachKit wrote for them — published, in review, vetoed and failed
   alike — as Markdown files with their assets, readable without ReachKit.
4. Given an export containing a page that was published, when the customer
   reads that page's file, then it carries the page's title, its publish date
   and the URL it was published at; and where the page is no longer published,
   the file says so and carries the date it was taken down.
5. Given an export cannot be produced, when the customer requests one, then
   they are told so in one written line and are not given a partial archive
   presented as complete.

## Non-goals
- Exporting measurements, scores, opportunities or scan history — the promise
  is the customer's content (BUILD §4.7, §9).
- Scheduled, automatic or API-driven export.
- Importing content into ReachKit.
- Migrating a customer's pages to another platform on their behalf.
- Copying a single page as Markdown or HTML from the draft view — REQ-045
  criterion 12 (BUILD §4.6, §9).
- Exporting another format: PDF, DOCX or a site archive.
- Mailing the archive or holding it behind a link the customer must come back
  for — the export is a download taken while signed in.
