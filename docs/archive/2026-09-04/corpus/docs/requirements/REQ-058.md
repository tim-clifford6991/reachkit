# REQ-058 — Publishing has hard limits whatever the settings, and stops instantly

> Superseded by REQ-056, which absorbed this requirement's acceptance criteria
> verbatim (2026-08-30; see `requirements/history/REQ-056-2026-08-30-requirement-cleanup.md`).
> Retired in place; cite REQ-056, not this file. This requirement's non-goal on
> "which state a held page occupies while publishing is off" was promoted to a
> criterion on the survivor (REQ-056 criterion 10) under ruling T1.

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer who has turned autopilot on, I want a ceiling on how much
can ever be published without me and a way to stop it in one action, so that
handing over the keys is never a risk I cannot reverse immediately.

## Rationale
Ceilings that hold whatever the customer or the plan has set are what make
unattended publishing safe, and the stop must be immediate — a stop that takes
effect "next cycle" is not a stop. BUILD.md §9 fixes both: "Autopilot hard limits
regardless of settings: ≤1 publish/day, ≤8/week; **Fix never automates**; pause
is one click and instant", with BUILD.md §14.7 keeping them "independent of the
monthly cap".

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given pages are publishing in either mode, when they publish, then no more
   than one publishes in any calendar day and no more than eight in any calendar
   week, counted in the time zone the customer set, whatever their settings or
   plan would otherwise allow; the two ceilings hold independently of each other.
2. Given the customer switches publishing off — the switch on the closed
   settings list, REQ-070 criterion 1 — when they do so, then it takes one
   action and no publish attempt begins for any page after the moment the switch
   is recorded — including for one already approved or already in review — until
   they switch it back on; the only page that can still reach a destination is
   one whose attempt was already under way at that moment, which criteria 3 and
   4 govern.
3. Given a page whose publish attempt is already under way, or a page that failed
   and is due to be retried, when the customer switches publishing off, then no
   publish attempt begins for it after the moment the switch is recorded — not
   the retry it was due, nor any later automatic attempt — for as long as
   publishing is off, and it is held with the pages in criterion 5 rather than
   dropped, published, or brought to rest as needing the customer (REQ-056
   criterion 4).
4. Given a publish attempt was already under way at the moment the switch was
   recorded, when its outcome is known, then the customer is shown that outcome:
   a page that did reach its destination is shown as published, with the record
   REQ-056 criterion 6 requires, and a page that did not is shown as held — so
   what the customer reads after switching off never says nothing was published
   while a page went out, and never says a page went out that did not.
5. Given pages were held while publishing was switched off, when the customer
   switches it back on, then those pages resume under the ceilings in criterion
   1, in the order they were held, and none is dropped.
6. Given publishing is switched off, when the customer opens the app, then that
   state, its effect on the schedule, and how many pages are held by it are
   visible without opening Settings.

## Non-goals
- Raising the ceilings by plan, by request or by setting — criterion 1 carries
  that promise as behaviour; this line only points at it.
- Catching up by publishing more than criterion 1 allows once publishing
  resumes — criterion 5 carries that promise as behaviour; this line only
  points at it.
- Where the switch that turns publishing off lives, and the rest of what
  Settings offers — REQ-070 criterion 1.
- The publishing mode, the veto window, the publish time and what the screen
  says about each — REQ-073.
- What else switching publishing off does: it stays off until the customer
  switches it on themselves after unpublishing everything (REQ-079 criterion 5),
  and a reconnected destination releases no held page while it is off (REQ-074
  criterion 3).
- Pages held because the product stopped its own work, rather than because the
  customer stopped it — REQ-092.
- Which state a held page occupies while publishing is off, and the transitions
  open to it — REQ-056; criteria 3 and 4 here fix only that no attempt starts,
  that nothing is dropped, and that the customer is told what the one attempt in
  flight did.
- Automating Fix-family items — that promise lives in REQ-047.
