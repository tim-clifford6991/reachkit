# REQ-049 — Only targets the customer could realistically win are queued

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer starting from nothing, I want the pages written for me to
target searches I have a real chance at, so that my daily page is an investment
rather than a lottery ticket against the biggest sites in my market.

## Rationale
Right-sizing is what makes one page a day compound for a small site: a target is
queued only where at least one site already on its first page is small enough to
displace, the rule still returns targets for a customer who ranks for nothing,
and the customer can see for each planned page how winnable it was judged to be.
Source: `BUILD.md` §7 ("a Write target qualifies only if its top-10 contains at
least one domain whose ranked count ≤ max(500, 5× customer's)"; bands
Winnable / Reach / Not-yet) and §6.6 (the same threshold "keeps winnable targets
non-empty at ranked = 0"). The band words and what each promises are the
customer's to be told; the two counts behind them — the qualifying bar and the
tighter bar that separates Winnable from Reach — are recorded parameters carried
in this file's `rests-on` (rule 1.1), so tuning a number is not a change to what
is promised. The tighter bar's floor is 100, not 500: at 500 the two expressions
are equal for any customer ranking below 250 searches, so every queued target
banded Winnable and Reach never appeared for the cold-start founder this product
is for.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a candidate target for a new page, when it is assessed, then it
   qualifies only if at least one domain currently in its top ten ranks for no
   more than the greater of 500 searches and five times the customer's own
   measured count.
2. Given an assessed target, when its winnability is recorded, then it carries
   exactly one of three bands: Winnable, where a domain in its top ten ranks for
   no more than the greater of 100 searches and twice the customer's own measured
   count — a site roughly their size already ranks there; Reach, where it passes
   criterion 1 but no domain that small is present; and Not-yet, where it fails
   criterion 1. A Not-yet target is never queued for a page.
3. Given a customer who ranks for nothing, when targets are assessed, then
   qualifying targets are still produced.
4. Given the customer inspects a planned page in the day detail (REQ-044
   criterion 2), when the reason it was chosen is shown, then it names that
   page's winnability band.
5. Given any two customers, when the same target is assessed for both, then the
   threshold applied is identical for both and follows only from measured counts.

## Non-goals
- Promising a ranking position or a timeframe.
- A customer-adjustable difficulty threshold, or any per-customer tuning of it.
- Third-party difficulty scores bought from a vendor.
