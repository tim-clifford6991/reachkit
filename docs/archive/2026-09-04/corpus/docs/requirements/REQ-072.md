# REQ-072 — The customer decides who they are compared against

> Superseded by REQ-071, which absorbed this requirement's acceptance criteria
> verbatim (2026-08-30; see `requirements/history/REQ-071-2026-08-30-requirement-cleanup.md`).
> Retired in place; cite REQ-071, not this file.

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer, I want to add and remove the competitors I am measured
against, so that the gap the product shows me every Monday is the gap against
the companies I actually lose deals to.

## Rationale
Every rival comparison the customer reads is only meaningful if the set is
theirs, so the customer must be able to name and drop the competitors they are
measured against — including by typing one the product never suggested, since a
founder whose own site ranks for nothing still knows who they lose deals to
(BUILD.md §4.7, "**Competitors** (chips ×5, add/remove)"; §6.1, `COMPETITORS_MAX`
5; §6.6, "the customer can always type a rival manually — a cold-start founder
knows their competitors even when no dataset does"). An account with no rivals at
all is a legal, runnable state and not a floor this screen defends: BUILD §6.6's
cold-start law, REQ-027 criterion 5 completing setup with none selected, and
REQ-029 releasing every founder into the app however thin the measurement came
back all establish it, so removing the last competitor costs the customer the
rival comparison and nothing else.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a customer on Settings, when they open the competitors card, then
   every domain currently being compared against them is listed, and each can
   be removed.
2. Given fewer than five competitors are set, when the customer adds one —
   either a domain the product suggests or a domain they type themselves — then
   it joins the set; given five are already set, when they try to add another,
   then it is refused with one written line saying a competitor must be removed
   first.
3. Given the customer changes the set, when the first weekly re-measurement that
   begins after the moment of the save runs (REQ-065 criterion 1), then every
   rival comparison in the product shows exactly the current set, and a removed
   domain appears in none of them.
4. Given a customer whose own site ranks for nothing and for whom no competitor
   was suggested, when they open the competitors card, then they can still type
   a competitor and are never blocked by the absence of suggestions, and a
   rival whose site is new, thin or unreachable is accepted all the same.
5. Given the customer submits a value that is not a domain name that resolves
   in DNS, or is their own domain, or is a domain already in the set however it
   is typed, when they submit it, then it is not added, no slot is consumed,
   and one written line says why.
6. Given exactly one competitor is set, when the customer removes it, then the
   removal is accepted and the account goes on running with none: one written
   line says that no rival comparison will be shown until they add one, every
   other measurement and the daily page continue unchanged, and adding a
   competitor later restores the comparison at the next re-measurement
   (criterion 3).
7. Given any domain the customer has named as a competitor, when it is added,
   measured against, or removed, then ReachKit sends that domain and the people
   behind it no message and makes no contact of any kind, and nothing ReachKit
   publishes or serves names the customer as measuring them.

## Non-goals
- How competitor suggestions are derived, scored or ranked (BUILD §6.6 — engine).
- More than five competitors, or per-competitor settings of any kind.
- Confirming the initial set at setup (REQ-027).
- Comparing against a named competitor's individual pages, keywords or content.
- Notifying or contacting a competitor in any way (BUILD §17, "community
  outreach of any kind") — criterion 7 carries that promise; no feature here
  reaches a competitor at all.
- Restating what the product shows between a saved change and the re-measure
  that acts on it — REQ-071 criterion 5 states that convention once, for every
  Settings change (rule 2.4).
