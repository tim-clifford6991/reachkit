# REQ-042 — Overview shows how far ahead each rival still is, and never fakes a ratio

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer, I want to see how far ahead each competitor I confirmed
still is and whether that distance is shrinking, so that I can tell whether I am
catching up and not just moving.

## Rationale
Absolute progress is not the customer's question — closing the gap on the rivals
they named is — and the module must stay truthful for a customer who ranks for
nothing, which is the state every new customer starts in and where a ratio
against zero would be an invented number. Source: `BUILD.md` §4.5 (per-rival name
and falling line, "Every line pointing down is the gap shrinking") and §6.6
(at a customer count of 0 render the rivals' absolute numbers with `you: 0`,
never a ratio; the ratio module unlocks at ranked ≥ 10).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given confirmed competitors and a current measurement, when the customer opens
   Overview, then each competitor is shown by name with the size of the gap
   between them and the customer and how that gap has moved over time, and no
   site the customer has not confirmed as a competitor appears in the module.
2. Given the customer appears in no searches, when the gap module renders, then
   what it shows moving over time is the rival's own count, and it is not
   described as the gap shrinking.
3. Given the customer appears in fewer than 10 searches, when the gap module
   renders, then the rivals' absolute numbers are shown alongside the customer's
   own number and no ratio is displayed.
4. Given the customer appears in 10 or more searches, when the gap module
   renders, then the ratio is shown together with its previous value; where the
   customer was below 10 at the previous measurement and no previous ratio
   exists, it is shown with that measurement's absolute counts instead.

## Non-goals
- Suggesting or discovering competitors the customer might add — where they
  confirm the list is REQ-070's; criterion 1 fixes only that this module shows
  nothing outside it.
- Backlink, local or per-country comparisons.
- Sparkline geometry, series colours and label placement — design system.
- A composite "competitiveness" score.
