# REQ-014 — The founder can correct the market when the report guessed it wrong

> Superseded by REQ-002, which absorbed this requirement's acceptance criteria
> verbatim as its criteria 4–10 (2026-08-30; see
> `requirements/history/REQ-002-2026-08-30-requirement-cleanup.md`). Retired in
> place; cite REQ-002, not this file. The `REVIEW(conflict with REQ-026)` line
> below was deleted as stale, not carried forward — it quotes text criterion 6
> no longer holds; the reasoning is in the history entry.

- Priority: Should

## User story
As a founder whose site was read as the wrong kind of business, I want to say so
and get the report re-measured against my actual market, so that I am not
dismissing the product over a verdict about someone else's category.

## Rationale
The market is inferred from the founder's own homepage, so when the inference is
wrong every number below it is wrong too and the founder's only rational
response is to leave — they must always be able to see which market they were
measured in, say it is wrong, and read the report again measured against the
right one. Because the report is public and the control is open to whoever opens
it, a stranger must never be able to leave a domain's public report
mis-categorised: the domain's owner, on the proof of control REQ-002 names, can
always overturn what a stranger did. Why the free report allows exactly one
correction is a cost bound, held in `registry/evidence/REQ-014.md`. Source:
`BUILD.md` §6.7 step 5.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given any completed report, when it renders, then the category the report
   measured is shown, together with a control to correct it — on every report,
   not only where the market looks wrong. On a domain whose owner has set its
   category (criterion 6) the category is still shown, but no correction control
   is offered to anyone who has not proved control of the domain; one written
   line says the domain's owner set it and points at the proof of control
   REQ-002 criteria 6 and 7 name.
2. Given a report whose twelve searches share no common top-ten domain across at
   least three of them, when it renders, then one written line states that the
   market may have been read wrongly and points at that control.
3. Given a founder who submits a corrected category, when the correction is
   accepted, then the market set, the twelve questions and the report's
   measurements are re-derived from the corrected category and the report
   re-renders from them.
4. Given a corrected report, when any later visitor opens that domain's report
   address, then they see the corrected report — the correction replaces the one
   stored report for that domain (REQ-012 criterion 1).
5. Given a report whose one correction has already been used, when any visitor
   attempts a further correction, then they are told in writing that a free scan
   allows one correction and that this one has been used, and the corrected
   report stays readable; where two corrections are submitted before either has
   been applied, the first accepted becomes the stored report and every other is
   refused the same way.
6. Given a visitor who has proved control of the domain in the way REQ-002
   criterion 7 names — a correction being one of the requests that criterion
   covers, needing no removal to be asked for — when they submit a correction,
   then it is applied and becomes the stored report even where the scan's one
   correction was already used by someone else; that category then holds for
   that domain's free reports — every later free scan of it is measured against
   that category — and no correction by anyone who has not proved control of the
   domain is accepted for it again, on that scan or any later one, until someone
   who has proved control submits another. It binds the free path and nothing
   else: the market a paying account is measured in is the one its founder
   confirms at setup (REQ-026) and may replace afterwards (REQ-071 criterion 1), which no
   proof of control over a public report displaces or is displaced by.
7. Given a domain re-scanned into a new stored report, when that report is
   opened, then one correction is available again where the re-scan was offered
   because the previous report was 7 days old or older (REQ-012 criterion 4),
   and not where it was offered because that report was incomplete (REQ-012
   criterion 3) — a re-measurement of an incomplete report carries the
   correction already spent on it, so re-scanning is never a way to buy a
   further one. Where someone who proved control has set the domain's category
   (criterion 6), no correction is offered to anyone who has not, on any later
   free scan.

## Non-goals
- No editing of individual questions, searches or rivals on the free report.
- No repeated or unlimited corrections on the free path for anyone who has not
  proved control: one accepted correction per free scan, and after a proven
  owner has set the category, none.
- No confirming or freezing of the market on the free path; the category is
  confirmed after payment, at setup (`BUILD.md` §6.7 step 5).
- The full enumeration of which re-scan routes mint a fresh correction: the
  blueprint's, derived from criterion 7's promise that ageing past 7 days does
  and re-measuring an incomplete report does not.
