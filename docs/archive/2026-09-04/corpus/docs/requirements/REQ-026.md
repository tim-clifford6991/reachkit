# REQ-026 — The founder settles the market at setup, and the rivals that derive from it

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder at setup, I want to see the market we think I am in, correct it, and
then confirm who I am actually competing with in it — including rivals no dataset
knows about — so that everything measured and written for me, and the comparison
I am shown every week, is aimed at my actual buyers.

## Rationale
The market is the root of everything downstream — the search set, the questions,
the rivals, and every page written from them — so a wrong one makes the whole
product wrong in a way the founder cannot see later, and the rivals are derived
from it on the same screen in the same act. The founder must see the market we
inferred for them, be able to replace it, and know that what they confirmed is
what the product uses; where nothing was inferred they state it themselves rather
than being shown a guess dressed as a measurement, and setup does not proceed
without one. From that market they must be offered rivals they can accept or
reject one by one, be able to type a rival no dataset knows, never be able to
compare themselves against themselves, and never be held at setup by the absence
of suggestions — up to the five the product tracks (`BUILD.md` §4.3, §6.1, §6.6,
§6.7). What decides whether a founder confirms or states is whether the product
holds a completed report for the address the account will use, never how the
purchase was made — the same key REQ-021 criterion 11 turns on — so a founder who
scanned their own site and then bought from a price surface confirms what was
inferred for it rather than retyping it. Resolving in DNS is a test that a rival's
domain exists, never that its site is live or substantial (REQ-071 criterion 5).
The freezing of the question set after confirmation belongs to the measurement
requirement that owns the question set. Placement: JN-002 steps 5 and 6.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a founder at setup for whose site address the product holds a completed
   report — whether or not that report was the one their purchase came from —
   when the market card renders, then it shows the one market inferred for that
   address, in plain buyer language, and the founder can change it before
   submitting.
2. Given a founder who does not recognise the market shown, when they replace
   it, then what they entered is the market the product uses from then on, and
   it is visible to them in the app afterwards.
3. Given a setup where the product holds no completed report for the site
   address the account will use — nobody having scanned it, or the buyer having
   replaced the domain their report measured, so that the account stands as one
   with nothing measured yet (REQ-021 criterion 12) — when the founder reaches
   the market card, then it is empty and asks them to state their market in
   their own words, with nothing pre-filled and nothing presented as inferred or
   measured.
4. Given the founder has submitted setup, when the deep pass runs and every time
   the product uses their market afterwards, then it is the market they
   confirmed or stated, and it changes only when they change it themselves at
   the market card REQ-071 criterion 1 offers, taking effect when REQ-071
   criterion 7 makes it effective — never by re-inference, at setup or at any
   later measurement.
5. Given a founder who has stated no market, when they try to submit setup, then
   setup does not proceed and one written line says the market is needed.
6. Given a founder who changes the site address on this screen after the market
   card has rendered (REQ-021 criterion 6), when they change it, then any market
   inferred for the domain they replaced is cleared, the card then stands by the
   address they gave — criterion 1's inferred market where the product holds a
   completed report for it, criterion 3's empty card where it does not — and a
   market they typed themselves is kept exactly as they left it.
7. Given a founder at setup whose market is known — inferred from a report that
   measured the domain the account will use, or stated by them on the same
   screen (criterion 3) — when the card renders or the market is first stated, then
   it offers suggested rivals drawn from that market, each of which can be
   accepted or rejected individually; and where nothing has been measured for
   the domain the account will use — a purchase with no report behind it, or a
   buyer who replaced the domain the report behind their purchase measured — the
   market is the one the founder states and no rival found for the replaced
   domain is offered as theirs (REQ-021 criterion 12).
8. Given a founder whose real rival was not suggested, when they type its
   domain, then it is added and treated identically to a suggested one; and a
   value that is not a domain name that resolves in DNS, or is their own site,
   or is already in the set however it is typed, is not added, consumes none of
   the five, and one written line says why — the same rule that governs the same
   action after setup (REQ-071 criterion 4).
9. Given a founder selecting rivals, when they have five, then no sixth can be
   added and the limit is stated on screen rather than silently enforced.
10. Given a founder who has not yet stated a market, when the card renders, then
    it says it is waiting on their market, never that no rivals were found; and
    given a market that is known, when suggestions have been sought for it and
    none came back, then one written line says none were found.
11. Given a founder with no competitors selected — none were suggested, or they
    rejected every suggestion — when they submit setup, then setup completes.
12. Given a founder who has already accepted or typed rivals and then changes the
    site address on this screen (REQ-021 criterion 6), when they change it, then
    every rival that came from suggestions for the replaced domain is cleared
    from the set, a rival they typed themselves is kept, and the address they
    have just given is refused as a rival on the terms criterion 8 states.
13. Given the founder has submitted setup, when the deep pass runs and every time
    the product compares them afterwards, then the set compared against is
    exactly the rivals they accepted or typed, no domain they did not choose ever
    joining it, and it changes only when they change it themselves at the
    competitors card REQ-071 criteria 2 and 3 offer.

## Non-goals
- Choosing from a fixed industry taxonomy — criteria 2 and 3 carry the
  customer's promise that the market is theirs to state in their own words.
- More than one market per site.
- Per-country or non-US-English markets (MVP is US Google / `en`).
- Re-deriving the market without the founder changing it — criterion 4 carries
  it.
- More than five competitors — criterion 9 carries the customer's promise that
  the limit is stated rather than silently enforced.
- Verifying that a typed domain is genuinely a competitor.
- Per-competitor settings, weights or notes.
- Adding a competitor the founder did not choose — criterion 13 carries the
  customer's promise for the set they leave setup with; after setup it is
  REQ-071 criterion 18's.
- How competitor suggestions are derived, scored or ranked (`BUILD.md` §6.6 —
  engine); criterion 7 states only that they come from the founder's market.
