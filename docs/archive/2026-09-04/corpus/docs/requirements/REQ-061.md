# REQ-061 — The content is the customer's — exportable and reversible at any time

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer, I want to be able to take every page with me and to
unpublish anything at any time, so that subscribing to ReachKit never puts my
own content out of my reach.

## Rationale
Content lock-in would make the product a liability rather than an asset, so the
customer can take every page with them and take any page down at any time,
including after cancelling, and nothing of theirs is destroyed before they have
a copy. BUILD.md §9 fixes the guarantees — "Export = Markdown + assets zip,
always available"; "Everything else = copy as Markdown/HTML (always shown)";
"published → unpublished (always available)" — and BUILD.md §4.7 fixes what the
danger zone must honour: "pages are exported to you first, never silently
destroyed".

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given any number of pages, when the customer asks to export, then they
   receive every page as Markdown with its assets, and this remains available
   after they cancel.
2. Given any draft or published page, whatever its destination, when the
   customer opens it, then its Markdown and its HTML are available for them to
   copy.
3. Given a page published to a destination ReachKit serves, when the customer
   chooses to unpublish it, then it is removed from that destination, and this
   action is available for any such page at any time.
4. Given a page delivered to WordPress, when the customer chooses to unpublish
   it, then the product stops treating it as one of their live pages and names
   the post in their own site for them to remove, since ReachKit never made it
   live.
5. Given the customer deletes their account or unpublishes everything, when the
   action runs, then their pages are exported to them first and are never
   destroyed silently.

## Non-goals
- Charging for, rate-limiting or gating export behind plan state.
- Migrating a customer's pages to another platform on their behalf.
- Deleting or editing posts inside the customer's own WordPress (REQ-060).
- Retaining published pages on a destination ReachKit serves after the customer
  unpublishes.
