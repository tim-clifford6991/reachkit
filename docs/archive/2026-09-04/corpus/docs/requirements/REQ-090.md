# REQ-090 — The report ends in an offer, and the scan behind it carries into checkout

> Superseded 2026-08-31 by REQ-021, which states one behaviour this file and
> REQ-021 could not state without citing each other: where the offer lives, what
> a purchase carries into setup, and what an account starts with when nothing was
> measured for its domain. All five criteria are carried verbatim on REQ-021 as
> its criteria 1, 2, 6, 12 and 3. Retired in place; cite REQ-021, never this
> file. Recorded in
> `requirements/history/REQ-090-2026-08-31-requirement-cleanup.md`.

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder who has just read my report and believes it, I want the report
itself to tell me what ReachKit costs and to start the purchase from what it
already knows about me, so that deciding to buy is one action and I never
re-enter what I have already given.

## Rationale
What must be true for the customer is that the report they have just read is
itself the place they can buy from: it states the price and what the
subscription does, one control begins checkout, and the domain that report
measured becomes the site their account is provisioned for, so a buyer who came
from a report is never asked to type a site address they have already given —
and a buyer who arrived by a colleague's shared link (REQ-001 criterion 7), for
whom that domain is the wrong one, can change it in the same place rather than
being bound to it, with nothing the
report measured for that other site following into their account. Source:
`BUILD.md` §4.1 module 6 — "**Pricing card**: €49/mo
+ four spec rows (1/day · weekly · weekly · 24h veto) + Start button + "Cancel
in one click."" — and §13, "Stripe Checkout from the report (scan id in
metadata)".

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a completed report, when the visitor reaches the end of it, then it
   presents one offer to subscribe with a single control that begins checkout.
2. Given the offer, when it renders, then it states the monthly price on the
   terms REQ-022 criterion 1 fixes, how often a page is written, how often
   measurement is repeated, how often the customer is told what moved, that a
   page can be
   stopped before it publishes, and that the subscription can be cancelled by
   the customer themselves.
3. Given a founder who begins checkout from a report, when their payment
   completes, then the account is provisioned for the domain that report
   measured, and setup shows them that domain to confirm or change rather than
   asking them to type it; when they change it, the account's site becomes the
   address they give, on the same terms as an address given with no report
   behind the purchase (REQ-021 criteria 4 and 5).
4. Given a buyer whose account's domain is not the one the report behind their
   purchase measured, because they replaced it, when they reach any setup or app
   screen before the deep pass for their own domain has completed, then nothing
   that report measured for the replaced domain — its score, its questions, its
   market or its rivals — is presented as their account's, and the account
   stands as one with nothing measured yet; what is offered from the market they
   state themselves, suggested rivals among it, is not a measurement of their
   site and remains available to them (REQ-021 criterion 3).
5. Given a scan that has not finished, when the visitor watches it run, then no
   offer to subscribe and no control that begins checkout is presented to them;
   the offer arrives with the report and never before it.

## Non-goals
- A second plan, tier, trial or discount at this surface (REQ-022); criterion 1
  fixes that this surface carries one offer.
- Any account, form or configuration between the control and payment (REQ-020
  criterion 1).
- An offer presented on a report whose scan has not finished (criterion 5).
- What else setup asks and the shape of that screen (REQ-025); criteria 3 and 4
  add to it only the domain the report already measured and what happens to that
  report's measurements when the buyer replaces it.
- Whether the replaced domain's report survives at its own public address — it
  does (REQ-001 criterion 11); criterion 4 governs only what the buyer's account
  presents.
- What each setup card then shows for an account with nothing measured yet: the
  empty market card is REQ-026 criterion 3's and the pre-measurement state is
  REQ-021 criterion 3's. Criterion 4 fixes only that the replaced domain's
  measurements are not this account's.
- The billing screen, how a cancellation is carried out, and refunds (REQ-076).
