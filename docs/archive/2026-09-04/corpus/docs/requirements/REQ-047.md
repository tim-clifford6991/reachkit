# REQ-047 — Every page proposed comes from measured evidence, states the test that would prove it worked, and targets only what the customer could win

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer starting from nothing, I want every page the product
proposes to trace back to something it measured, to target a search I have a real
chance at, and to tell me in advance what would count as it having worked, so
that my daily page is an investment I can judge rather than volume sold for its
own sake.

## Rationale
An opportunity is the unit the whole subscription rests on, so what it is, what
it carries, what would prove it worked and whether it is worth queuing all have
to hold at once: it exists because measured evidence produced it or it does not
exist at all; supply is the cap, so a day with nothing behind it stays empty
rather than being filled; it carries from the moment of its creation a success
test the customer can read in advance and that nothing may rewrite afterwards,
because a verdict is only trustworthy if its test predates the outcome; it is
queued only where a site small enough to displace already sits on its first page,
which is what makes one page a day compound for a small business; and the one
kind that asks the customer to unblock something is never turned into a page or
actioned for them. The band words and what each promises are the customer's to be
told; the two counts behind them — the qualifying bar and the tighter bar that
separates Winnable from Reach — are recorded parameters carried in this file's
`rests-on` (rule 1.1), so tuning a number is not a change to what is promised.
Placement: JN-003 steps 2 and 3, JN-005 step 4. Source: `BUILD.md` §7
(opportunity types as a closed enum of eight across the Write, Improve and Fix
families; every opportunity carries trigger and evidence — query, volume, rival,
URL, position; every opportunity carries an acceptance test, in the forms "top 20
for Q", "named on question P" or "gate passes"; "a Write target qualifies only if
its top-10 contains at least one domain whose ranked count ≤ max(500, 5×
customer's)"; bands Winnable / Reach / Not-yet; "Supply is the cap: never invent
an opportunity to fill a day"; the Fix type is "instruction only, never
generated, never automated") and §6.6 (the same threshold "keeps winnable targets
non-empty at ranked = 0"). No implementation exists to touch: this repo carries
no `src/` (`00-project.md`, "Greenfield. No source code on disk yet").

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a completed scan, when opportunities are derived, then each is of
   exactly one kind from a fixed set of eight — four that propose writing a new
   page (answering a question rivals own, a search the customer is absent from, a
   comparison, a page format the customer lacks), three that propose improving a
   page the customer already has (expand it, make it answerable, refresh it), and
   one that asks the customer to remove an access barrier — and an opportunity of
   any other kind cannot be stored.
2. Given an opportunity, when it is stored, then it carries the measured evidence
   that produced it: for the four that propose writing a new page, the search, its
   volume, and the rival page or position that shows the gap; for the three that
   propose improving a page the customer already has, the search, its volume, the
   page of theirs it names, and the measurement on that page that shows the
   shortfall — its own position, its thinness, or its staleness — with no rival
   required; for the one that asks the customer to remove an access barrier, the
   barrier found and the page of theirs it was found on. Every one of those values
   is one the product measured and stored, never one it composed (REQ-093
   criterion 4).
3. Given the available evidence has been exhausted, when upcoming days are
   filled, then no further opportunity is created and those days stay empty.
4. Given a new measurement produces qualifying opportunities, when the calendar
   is next rendered, then future days that were empty are filled from the new
   supply, in the order the ranking gives.
5. Given an opportunity that asks the customer to remove an access barrier, when
   it is presented, then it is presented as an instruction for them to carry out:
   no page is generated for it, it takes no publishing day and consumes no
   supply, and it is never actioned automatically in either publishing mode.
6. Given an opportunity is created, when it is stored, then it carries an
   acceptance test in exactly one of three forms: the target reaching the top 20
   for a named search, the customer being named in the answer to a named
   question, or a named access barrier being cleared.
7. Given a page in the calendar, when the customer inspects it, then that test is
   shown as part of why the page exists.
8. Given a published page is later judged, when its verdict is produced, then it
   is judged against the test recorded when its opportunity was created, and that
   recorded test is never rewritten — including where it can no longer be
   evaluated.
9. Given a candidate target for a new page, when it is assessed, then it
   qualifies only if at least one domain currently in its top ten ranks for no
   more than the greater of 500 searches and five times the customer's own
   measured count.
10. Given an assessed target, when its winnability is recorded, then it carries
    exactly one of three bands: Winnable, where a domain in its top ten ranks for
    no more than the greater of 100 searches and twice the customer's own measured
    count — a site roughly their size already ranks there; Reach, where it passes
    criterion 9 but no domain that small is present; and Not-yet, where it fails
    criterion 9. A Not-yet target is never queued for a page.
11. Given a customer who ranks for nothing, when targets are assessed, then
    qualifying targets are still produced.
12. Given the customer inspects a planned page in the day detail (REQ-043
    criterion 8), when the reason it was chosen is shown, then it names that
    page's winnability band.
13. Given any two customers, when the same target is assessed for both, then the
    threshold applied is identical for both and follows only from measured counts.
14. Given the thresholds criteria 9 and 10 state, when targets are assessed for a
    customer at any measured ranked count, zero included, then each of the three
    bands remains reachable: no customer's count makes Winnable, Reach or Not-yet
    impossible to assign.

## Non-goals
- An opportunity created to meet a daily or monthly quota.
- A language model deciding what counts as an opportunity, or inventing evidence
  for one.
- A ninth kind of opportunity; adding one is a change to this requirement.
- Backlink, local or per-country opportunities.
- A success test written or reworded by a language model after publication.
- Guaranteeing the test will pass.
- The verdict vocabulary and how a verdict is reported — REQ-063's.
- Per-customer tuning of what the tests may say.
- Promising a ranking position or a timeframe.
- A customer-adjustable difficulty threshold, or any per-customer tuning of it.
- Third-party difficulty scores bought from a vendor.
