# REQ-096 — Every tracked rival is sized once the deep pass has run, and a customer tracking one out of their league is told and offered a swap

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder starting from nothing, I want each rival I am measured against to
carry how big it is next to me, and to be told when one of them is far out of my
league and given a way to swap it, so that the distance I read every week is one
I could actually close rather than a permanent verdict on a company I will never
catch.

## Rationale
The rivals a customer is tracked against are chosen for how much of the market
they hold, so the biggest incumbent in a market is the first one offered — and a
founder who accepts it reads the same unmoving distance every week for the life
of the subscription, with nothing on the screen saying that the distance was
never closeable. What must be true is that each tracked rival carries its size
next to the customer's own, that the customer is told plainly when a rival is
out of their league, that the telling comes with a way to change it, and that
the product never quietly narrows the set it was told to measure: the customer
decides who they are compared against, and the product's job is to make that
choice an informed one. The sizing is a paid measurement that does not exist
until the onboarding deep pass has run and is refreshed on that pass's monthly
cycle, so setup cannot be held on it and the free public report cannot carry it
at all. The three band words are the customer's to be told and are not written
here: this requirement names the bands by the internal handles `near`, `middle`
and `far`, ordered by measured rival size, and requires that the customer meets
three distinct terms sharing none with the target winnability bands (REQ-047), so
one word never means two things in a single session. The two counts behind the
bands are recorded parameters carried in this file's `rests-on` (rule 1.1), so
tuning a number is not a change to what is promised. Where a
customer acts on the offer, the rival set is changed at the competitors card
REQ-071 criteria 2 and 3 give them, on that requirement's terms. Placement:
JN-005 step 3 ("Reads how far ahead each competitor they confirmed still is").
Evidence: `registry/evidence/REQ-096.md`; research on the DataForSEO endpoints and
competing products' measurement practices is in `registry/evidence/RESEARCH-dataforseo-endpoints.md` and
`registry/evidence/RESEARCH-competitor-visibility-measurement.md`. No implementation exists to touch:
this repo carries no `src/` (`00-project.md`, "Greenfield. No source code on
disk yet").

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a customer whose tracked rivals were settled at setup, when their
   onboarding deep pass completes, then each tracked rival carries a measured
   size — the count of searches that rival appears in — taken from that pass and
   held beside the customer's own measured count from the same pass; and no
   rival carries a size or a band before that pass has completed.
2. Given a tracked rival whose size has been measured, when its band is
   recorded, then it carries exactly one of three bands, ordered by measured size
   and named in this requirement by the internal handles `near`, `middle` and
   `far`: `near`, where the rival appears in no more than the greater of 100
   searches and twice the customer's own measured count — a rival roughly the
   customer's own size; `middle`, where it is not `near` and appears in no more
   than the greater of 500 searches and five times the customer's own count; and
   `far`, where it appears in more than that. Of any two rivals measured against
   the same customer count, the larger never carries a band ordered nearer than
   the smaller.
3. Given a tracked rival whose size was last measured a month ago or longer, when
   the product next measures for that customer, then that rival's size is
   measured again and its band re-derived from the current counts without the
   customer asking; and a rival the customer added after their deep pass is sized
   at the first such measurement after they added it.
4. Given a rival's band is shown to the customer, when they read it, then it
   carries the date that rival's size was measured; and where the size could not
   be measured again when it was due, the last measured band is shown with that
   earlier date and is never presented as a current measurement.
5. Given a customer reading how far ahead a tracked rival is, when that rival has
   been sized, then its band is shown with that distance; and where it has not
   been sized yet, one written line says its size has not been measured yet, and
   no band and no blank stands in its place.
6. Given a tracked rival banded `far`, when the customer reads their rivals,
   then one written line tells them that rival is far beyond what they could
   catch and why the distance to it will not move, and the same place offers them
   a single control that takes them to where they can replace that rival.
7. Given a tracked rival of any band, when any measurement, comparison or screen
   in the product uses the rival set, then that rival is measured, compared and
   shown exactly as every other one, and stays in the set until the customer
   removes it themselves: no band ever removes a rival, hides it, drops it from a
   comparison, or stops it being measured.
8. Given a customer who appears in no searches at all, when their tracked rivals
   are banded, then each still receives one of the three bands; and at every
   measured customer count, zero included, all three bands remain reachable — no
   customer's own count makes `near`, `middle` or `far` impossible to assign.
9. Given a rival's band is rendered anywhere in the product, when the customer
   reads it, then `near`, `middle` and `far` each render as their own distinct
   term, the same term for that band on every surface; and no term rendered for a
   rival's band is a term rendered for a target's winnability band (REQ-047
   criterion 10), so no single word names both how big a rival is and how winnable
   a target is.

## Non-goals
- Sizing, banding or warning about a rival at setup: the measurement does not
  exist until the deep pass has run (criterion 1), and nothing about a rival's
  size holds the founder at setup or conditions their arrival in the app.
- Any rival size, band or reachability statement on the free public report — the
  measurement is not taken on the free path, and no part of the free report
  changes because of this requirement.
- Removing, replacing, reordering or refusing a rival on the product's own
  judgement — criterion 7 carries the customer's promise that the set is theirs.
- Suggesting a specific replacement rival, or ranking candidate rivals by size.
- A customer-adjustable size threshold, or any per-customer tuning of the two
  counts criterion 2 states: the same boundaries apply to every customer and
  follow only from measured counts.
- Sizing a rival more often than criterion 3's cycle, or buying any measurement
  the product does not already take, to size one sooner.
- The wording of the three band terms, or of any line this requirement requires;
  criteria 5, 6 and 9 fix that they exist, that the three terms are distinct, and
  what each line must convey.
- Changing the terms a target's winnability band carries (REQ-047 criterion 10):
  criterion 9 asks only that a rival's three share none of them.
- Per-rival settings, notes or exclusions of any kind.
- Contacting, notifying or naming a sized rival anywhere ReachKit publishes.
