# REQ-040 — The app is three places, and the publishing state is visible from all of them

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer who opens ReachKit most days, I want the whole product to
be three destinations with the publishing state always in view, so that I never
hunt for where something lives and never wonder whether pages are going out
without me.

## Rationale
A customer living in the product week after week must be able to orient in one
glance, must be able to tell without opening anything whether something is
waiting on them, and must never be surprised that a page went out — or that one
did not — and because the shell states, on every screen, which site is being
measured and how long it has been measured, that count is a claim about the
customer's own history and must never overstate the evidence behind it. Source:
`BUILD.md` §4.4 (domain block carrying "accent dot, domain, `Week n ·
re-measured Mon`"; nav Overview / Calendar / Settings with a Calendar item count;
footer autopilot card carrying state, next publish time and toggle; no other
navigation). No implementation exists to touch: this repo carries no `src/`
(`00-project.md`, "Greenfield. No source code on disk yet").

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a signed-in customer on any app screen, when they look at the
   navigation, then exactly three destinations are offered — Overview, Calendar
   and Settings — and no fourth destination is offered anywhere in the app.
2. Given items in the calendar are waiting on the customer, when they look at the
   navigation, then the Calendar destination shows how many are waiting; when
   none are, it shows no count.
3. Given a customer on any app screen, when they look at the navigation, then the
   current publishing mode is shown together with the date and time of the next
   scheduled publish, in the customer's timezone.
4. Given no publish is scheduled — publishing is paused, nothing is approved, no
   page is planned, or ReachKit itself stopped the work — when the customer looks
   at the navigation, then in place of a time it carries one written line naming
   which of those is the case; where it was ReachKit that stopped, that line
   "names ReachKit's stop as the reason no publish is scheduled" (REQ-092
   criterion 7) and never one of the other three.
5. Given a viewport too narrow for the sidebar, when the customer opens any app
   screen, then the same three destinations remain reachable.
6. Given a site with at least one completed weekly measurement, when the customer
   looks at any app screen, then it states the domain being measured and how many
   weeks of measurement the site has, counted from the first weekly measurement of
   that domain — and where the customer has changed the domain, the count starts
   again at the first measurement of the new domain (REQ-071 criterion 13). That
   number is never larger than the number
   of weeks in which the site was measured: a week that ran and measured something
   advances it, a week that produced no measurement at all — one REQ-065 criterion
   3 states "has not been measured" — does not.
7. Given the domain has no weekly measurement yet, whether because none has ever
   completed or because the customer changed the domain, when the customer looks
   at any app screen, then no week count is stated and in its place the domain
   block carries one written line saying this domain has not been measured yet and
   naming the date its first measurement is due.

## Non-goals
- A fourth destination, a sub-navigation, or a per-screen menu. Screens reached
  from within a destination — the day detail, the draft view — are not
  destinations.
- Any control that tunes the engine (caps, cadences, question counts, model
  choices) anywhere in navigation.
- Switching between multiple sites.
- Per-stage counts inside the Calendar screen, which are REQ-043's.
- Sidebar dimensions, sticky behaviour, the domain block's accent dot and chip
  styling — those belong to the design system, not here.
- The weekly measurement itself, its cadence and the account it gives a week it
  could not measure — REQ-065's; criteria 6 and 7 only bound the count the shell
  states from it and what stands in its place.
