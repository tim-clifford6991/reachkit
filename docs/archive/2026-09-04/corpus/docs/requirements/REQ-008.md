# REQ-008 — Google presence is shown as share of the market's 12 biggest searches, beside each rival

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder whose site ranks for little or nothing, I want to see how many of
my market's biggest searches I appear in against each competitor, so that the
comparison still means something when my own number is zero.

## Rationale
A founder who ranks for nothing is the default customer, so their Google
presence must be shown as a share of a fixed market set of 12 searches — a
contrast that still means something at zero and still names rivals to compare
against — rather than as a figure keyed on rankings they do not have, which
would hand them a broken or empty card at the moment the product is meant to
prove its case. Every figure on this card, and every volume beside it, is
measured in one market — Google US in English — so the disclosure of that market
and the ban on presenting any figure as local to another country belong with the
figures they qualify. Rival size is not among the figures this card measures, so
the rivals shown are framed by what was measured and nothing further — that these
domains compete for the same 12 searches — and never by a forecast of what
entering this market would return to the customer. How rivals are derived is
mechanism for the blueprint. Placement: JN-001 step 5, which already exercises
this requirement and already names the rival contrast the framing line sits with;
no journey edit was needed. No implementation exists to touch: this repo carries
no `src/`. Source: `BUILD.md` §6.6 (the cold-start law and the free-report
contrast), §4.1 (Google search card), §2.5 (rivals are context, not alarms) and
§6.3a ("MVP is US-English only"); the fixed wording for criterion 7's line, and
the derivation behind criteria 9 and 10, are in `registry/evidence/REQ-008.md`; research on
the DataForSEO endpoints, competing products' measurement practices, and AI-answer visibility
is in `registry/evidence/RESEARCH-dataforseo-endpoints.md` and
`registry/evidence/RESEARCH-competitor-visibility-measurement.md`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a completed report, when the Google card renders, then it shows, for
   the customer and for each rival, in how many of the market's 12 biggest
   searches they appear in the top ten, each labelled with its name and its
   value rather than identified by colour alone.
2. Given a customer appearing in none of the 12, when the card renders, then 0
   is shown against the same denominator as every rival — never an error, a
   blank card, or a ratio.
3. Given the market set, when the card renders, then the customer's presence
   appears on the card exactly once — the top-ten count of criterion 1, labelled
   on their own bar — with no second, differently measured figure for how many of
   the 12 they appear in and no restatement of that count anywhere else on the
   card; and no total monthly search volume for the 12 searches is shown.
4. Given the searches the customer is absent from, when the card renders, then
   up to five of the biggest are listed, each with its monthly volume and the
   domain currently holding the top position.
5. Given a market whose 12 searches yield fewer than five rival domains, when
   the card renders, then every rival found is shown beside the customer, and
   where none was found one written line says so — never a card holding the
   customer's bar alone with nothing to compare it against.
6. Given the rivals shown beside the customer, when the card renders, then no
   rival's figure carries a severity, warning or problem label of any kind, and
   nothing on the card states that a rival appearing in a search is a fault of
   the customer's site. A rival's figure is a comparison; the only things the
   report names as problems, with a severity, are REQ-009's three.
7. Given a domain of any country or site language, when its report renders, then
   the report carries one written line that says both things: that the
   measurement was made on US Google in English, and that more countries are
   coming. It is the same words on every report, never varied, shortened or
   qualified by the domain's country or language, readable without the visitor
   acting on anything, and never conditional or dismissible.
8. Given any search volume, rival, ranking or AI answer shown on the report,
   when it renders, then it comes from that same disclosed market, and no part
   of the report presents a figure as local to another country or language.
9. Given a report on which at least one rival is shown beside the customer, when
   the Google card renders, then one written line sits with that comparison — on
   the same card, after the figures rather than before them, readable without the
   visitor acting on anything — and it says what those figures show and what
   follows from it: that these domains are competing for the same 12 searches the
   card measures, and that their presence is evidence those searches are worth
   competing for. Where no rival was found (criterion 5), the line is not shown.
10. Given that line, when it renders, then it claims nothing the report has not
    measured: it states no revenue, profit, traffic value or money amount,
    promises the customer no outcome — no earnings, no traffic, no result — from
    entering this market, and attributes to no rival any size, funding, headcount,
    customer count or ranking count. The only facts it rests on are the presence
    figures shown on the report.

## Non-goals
- No global ranked-keyword comparison (for example 12,400 vs 25) on the free
  report; that is a paid reveal (`BUILD.md` §6.6).
- No backlink, local, or per-country ranking data (`BUILD.md` §17).
- No market-total search volume, and no second statement of the customer's own
  presence beside it — the card compares presence, not market size; stated as
  criterion 3, not left to this list. The per-search volume beside each search
  the customer is absent from is criterion 4's and is unaffected.
- Rival strength is never rendered as an alarm state; rivals are context
  (`BUILD.md` §2.5) — stated as criterion 6, not left to this list. How that is
  styled is the design system's.
- No projection of what this market is worth to the customer — no revenue,
  earnings, traffic or return figure anywhere in the rival contrast — and no
  measurement of a rival's size, which the free report does not buy (`BUILD.md`
  §6.4's never-list); stated as criterion 10, not left to this list.
