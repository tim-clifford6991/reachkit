# REQ-071 — Changing what the site is measured as — domain, market or rivals — takes effect at the next re-measurement and never reads as movement

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a customer whose domain has moved, whose category was inferred wrongly, or
whose rival set is not the one they lose deals to, I want to correct what I am
measured as and be told what that restarts and when, so that the correction never
looks like my business collapsing and my weekly numbers stay comparable.

## Rationale
Three answers decide what every number in the product is a measurement of — the
domain, the market category and the competitor set — so each is the customer's to
correct, and each correction has to behave the same way: the screen says what it
restarts and names the date before they save; the change takes effect at the
first weekly re-measurement that begins after the moment of the save and never on
demand; the days between carry one written line naming which change is holding
them rather than reading as an empty market; and nothing measured under the old
answer is ever presented as the new answer's, or as movement across the change.
A rival set may be empty and the account still runs. Placement: JN-004 step 2
("Corrects the domain they are measured under, the market they are measured in,
and the rivals they are measured against"). Evidence:
`registry/evidence/REQ-071.md`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a customer on Settings, when they open the market card, then the
   category currently being measured is shown, can be replaced, and is
   accompanied by one written line stating that changing it rebuilds the search
   set and the twelve questions at the rebuilding re-measurement and not before,
   and that no new page is generated in the days between.
2. Given a customer on Settings, when they open the competitors card, then
   every domain currently being compared against them is listed, and each can
   be removed.
3. Given fewer than five competitors are set, when the customer adds one —
   either a domain the product suggests or a domain they type themselves — then
   it joins the set; given five are already set, when they try to add another,
   then it is refused with one written line saying a competitor must be removed
   first.
4. Given the customer submits a value that is not a domain name that resolves
   in DNS, or is their own domain, or is a domain already in the set however it
   is typed, when they submit it, then it is not added, no slot is consumed,
   and one written line says why.
5. Given a customer whose own site ranks for nothing and for whom no competitor
   was suggested, when they open the competitors card, then they can still type
   a competitor and are never blocked by the absence of suggestions, and a
   rival whose site is new, thin or unreachable is accepted all the same.
6. Given a customer changing the domain their site is measured and published
   under, when they enter a new address, then it is not accepted unless the
   product can reach it (REQ-021 criterion 9), and before they save, one written
   line states that measurement starts again against the new address at the next
   weekly re-measurement, names the date of that re-measurement, and states that
   what was measured for the previous address is kept and not carried across. The
   date that line names is the day the re-measurement criterion 9 makes the change
   effective at is due (REQ-065 criterion 1), and the change takes effect at that
   re-measurement and at no earlier one. Where that re-measurement does not run in
   its week (REQ-065 criterion 3), the customer reads the new due day that
   criterion states rather than a date that has passed, and the change takes
   effect at the re-measurement that then runs; they are never left holding a date
   the product has silently replaced.
7. Given the customer saves a different category, when the rebuilding
   re-measurement runs — the first weekly re-measurement that begins after the
   moment of the save (REQ-065 criterion 1) — then the
   search set and the twelve tracked questions are re-derived from the new
   category, the questions on screen carry the measurement date they belong
   to, and no re-measurement and no re-derivation of the question set is run
   before it.
8. Given the customer changes the competitor set, when the first weekly
   re-measurement that begins after the moment of the save runs (REQ-065
   criterion 1), then every rival comparison in the product shows exactly the
   current set, and a removed domain appears in none of them.
9. Given the customer saves a different domain, when the first weekly
   re-measurement that begins after the moment of the save runs, then the site is
   measured against the new domain from then on, and no measurement of the new
   domain is run before it.
10. Given any Settings change whose effect falls at a weekly re-measurement,
    when the customer looks at the product between the save and the first
    re-measurement that begins after the moment of the save, then the numbers
    shown are the ones last measured, dated, and unchanged by the edit.
11. Given a change to the market category or to the domain is saved, when the
    days before the re-measurement that acts on it pass, then no new page is
    generated from an opportunity derived under the category or domain being
    replaced, each of those days carries one written line saying which of those
    two changes is why and naming the date pages resume — never the empty-pipeline
    line REQ-043 criterion 3 gives a day with nothing to publish — and a draft
    already in review keeps the window it entered with, can still be stopped, and
    where it is not stopped it publishes under REQ-057's ordinary rule to the
    destination the site holds at that moment.
12. Given the question set was re-derived after a category change, when the
    customer views movement spanning the date the rebuilding re-measurement was
    taken (criterion 7; REQ-065 criterion 1, the date a measurement carries), then
    the screen states that the question set changed on that date, and the
    difference across that date is not presented as movement in any form — not as
    a change figure, and not as a joined line, a trend arrow, a rising or falling
    colour, a slope, or a direction, streak, badge or verdict stated in words:
    every chart or series that spans the date is broken at it, so no number, count
    or point measured under the old question set is joined to one measured under
    the new, and any direction, run of weeks or verdict the product states about
    this site is computed only over a span that does not contain that date — where
    every span available to it contains that date, it is not stated at all rather
    than restated over a shorter one. Where a number that carries a goal (REQ-041
    criterion 4) would otherwise have shown a change across that date, the goal is
    what it carries instead; and where a value carrying no goal (REQ-041 criterion
    4) would otherwise have been shown together with a previous value measured
    under the old question set, it is shown instead with the counts from its own
    measurement and the date they were taken, never with that previous value.
13. Given measurements taken for the previous domain, when the customer reads any
    number, chart or rival distance after the change, then each carries the domain
    it measured and the date it was taken, nothing measured for the previous
    domain is presented as a measurement of the new one, and the difference across
    the date the re-measurement that made the domain change effective was taken
    (criterion 9) is not presented as movement in any form, on criterion 12's
    terms — no change figure, no joined line, trend arrow, rising or falling
    colour or slope, and no direction, streak, badge or verdict stated in words:
    every chart or series that spans that date is broken at it and states that the
    domain changed on that date, no number, distance or point measured for the
    previous domain is joined to one measured for the new, a direction, run of
    weeks or verdict is computed only over a span that does not contain that date
    and is not stated at all where every available span contains it, a number
    carrying a goal carries the goal in place of a change, and a value carrying no
    goal is shown with the counts from its own measurement and their date rather
    than with a previous value measured for the previous domain. Any count of
    weeks the product states for the site counts from the first measurement of the
    new domain.
14. Given pages published before the change, when the domain changes, then each
    stays published where it was published and stays exportable (REQ-078), keeps
    the last verdict it received with that verdict's date, and receives no further
    working, too early or not working verdict for as long as the site's domain is
    not the one that page's recorded test was written against: it is marked as no
    longer judgeable (REQ-063 criterion 6), with one written line saying the
    domain changed.
15. Given the customer does not change the category, when any number of weekly
    re-measures run, then the twelve questions are the same twelve questions as
    the week before.
16. Given exactly one competitor is set, when the customer removes it, then the
    removal is accepted and the account goes on running with none: one written
    line says that no rival comparison will be shown until they add one, every
    other measurement and the daily page continue unchanged, and adding a
    competitor later restores the comparison at the next re-measurement
    (criterion 8).
17. Given any domain the customer has named as a competitor, when it is added,
    measured against, or removed, then ReachKit sends that domain and the people
    behind it no message and makes no contact of any kind, and nothing ReachKit
    publishes or serves names the customer as measuring them.
18. Given a customer after setup, when any re-measurement compares them, then the
    set compared against is exactly the rivals they accepted at setup or have
    added since, no domain they did not choose ever joining it — a domain the
    product suggests at the competitors card joins only when the customer adds it
    (criterion 3) — and it changes only when they change it themselves at the
    card criteria 2 and 3 offer.

## Non-goals
- Confirming or correcting the category at setup (REQ-026) or on the free
  report (BUILD §6.7 step 5).
- Any control over how many questions there are, how they are selected, scored
  or phrased — one of the engine parameters REQ-070 criterion 3 keeps off every
  screen.
- Editing an individual question, adding one, or removing one.
- Keeping history under both question sets side by side.
- How competitor suggestions are derived, scored or ranked (BUILD §6.6 — engine).
- More than five competitors, or per-competitor settings of any kind.
- Confirming the initial set at setup (REQ-026).
- Comparing against a named competitor's individual pages, keywords or content.
- Notifying or contacting a competitor in any way — criterion 17 carries that
  promise; no feature here reaches a competitor at all.
- Re-measuring on demand, at the moment of the save, or more than once in a week
  — REQ-065's non-goal; criteria 7 to 9 carry the customer's side of it.
- A second proof that the customer controls the domain: the change is admitted
  by criterion 6's reachability check, and publishing to the new domain still
  waits on the customer's own DNS record (REQ-059 criteria 1 and 2).
- Moving, redirecting, re-publishing or taking down pages already published under
  the previous domain.
- Carrying the previous domain's measurements forward as the new domain's, under
  any option offered to the customer.
- Measuring or holding more than one domain per account (REQ-070).
- Where these controls sit on Settings and what else that screen offers
  (REQ-070), and changing the address the customer signs in with (REQ-077).
- Whether a destination is ready to receive a page once criterion 11's hold ends:
  a hosted destination still waiting on the customer's DNS record holds its pages
  under REQ-059 criterion 2 and shows that state under REQ-074 criterion 1. This
  requirement states only when the change stops holding generation, never that
  nothing else can.
- Which pages carry no verdict at all across a domain change, and what a verdict
  line says for a page that never received one — REQ-063 criterion 6 owns the
  verdict and its written line; criterion 14 here states only the customer's
  promise that a page's old verdict is never restated as a judgement of the new
  domain.
