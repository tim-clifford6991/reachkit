# REQ-070 — Settings holds only what is the customer's to decide

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer, I want Settings to offer exactly the choices that are
mine and no others, so that I can change what I came to change without being
handed a knob that quietly breaks my own measurements.

## Rationale
A customer with no SEO knowledge must be able to open Settings, change the one
thing they came for, and leave without having touched anything that makes next
Monday's numbers uncomparable with last Monday's — so everything that decides
how the product measures and what it spends is identical for every customer and
is offered nowhere, and the screen carries only the customer's own three
answers plus the administration of their account (BUILD.md §4.7: "Settings
holds the three product answers plus account admin, and **nothing that tunes
the engine** — caps, cadences, question counts, model choices are code
constants"; §17, "Settings that tune the engine"). Two further answers belong
on that screen for the same reason: how their pages should sound and the claims
their pages must never make are constraints on content published under the
customer's own name, not engine parameters, and BUILD.md §14.6 makes them the
party accountable for it — "Customer is publisher of record: their domain,
their identity" — with both already held on the site record (BUILD.md §10,
`sites` — "voice_text, do_not_claim jsonb"). What each of the two then does to
a generated page is REQ-055's and REQ-053's; this screen owns only that the
customer can read and change them.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a customer on Settings, when the screen renders, then nothing beyond
   this list is offered for them to change: the market category, the competitor
   set, the domain the site is measured and published under, publishing mode,
   veto window, publish time, the time zone those times
   are stated in, whether pages publish at all, destination connections, the
   description of how their pages should sound, the claims their pages must
   never make, notification preferences, their name and sign-in address, and
   their billing details.
2. Given the same screen, when the customer looks at what it does besides
   change settings, then nothing beyond this list is offered: reaching their
   invoices, cancelling or resuming the plan, signing out, exporting their
   content, unpublishing every page, and deleting the account.
3. Given any measurement or spend parameter — the number of tracked questions
   and how they are selected, scored or phrased, the search locale and the
   country searches are run in, scan or publishing cadence, how many pages a day
   are written, cost caps, row limits, freshness windows, scoring weights, or
   which model writes a page — when a customer looks anywhere in the product,
   then no control over it is offered and its value is identical for every
   customer.
4. Given a request that attempts to set a value the screen does not offer, when
   it reaches the server, then it is rejected and no stored setting changes.
5. Given a customer changes any setting here, when it is saved, then it affects
   only their own site's measurements, schedule and publishing.

## Non-goals
- Any engine-tuning control: caps, cadences, question counts, row limits, model
  choices, scoring weights (BUILD §17) — criterion 3 carries that promise; this
  line names the class and adds no second one.
- Feature flags, experiment toggles, or a beta opt-in.
- Locale or country selection — the MVP measures on US Google for every
  customer (BUILD §6.3a), which is one of criterion 3's parameters: offered
  nowhere, identical for every customer.
- Managing more than one site from one account (BUILD §17, "multi-site").
- The first capture of these answers at `/setup`, which is JN-002's (REQ-025,
  REQ-026).
- Theme, layout or density preferences.
- What the voice description and the do-not-claim list do to a generated page —
  REQ-055's and REQ-053's, not this screen's.
- What switching publishing off does to pages already in flight, and how
  quickly it takes effect — REQ-056 criteria 9 to 13.
- What changing the domain, the market category or the competitor set does to
  measurement and history, and how quickly each takes effect — REQ-071's, which
  puts all three at the next weekly re-measurement and never on save (REQ-071
  criteria 7 to 9). This screen owns only that the controls are offered.
