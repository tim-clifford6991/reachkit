# REQ-029 — The wait after setup always ends in the app

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder who has just submitted setup, I want to see what is happening and
end up inside the product, so that paying and answering three questions always
leads somewhere — even when the measurement comes back thin.

## Rationale
This is the last moment where the founder can still feel they bought nothing, so
the wait must be legible and its exit unconditional: they see in written words
which step is actually running and no duration nobody has measured, they are let
into the app however the pass ends and even if it does not end, and a
measurement that found little says so rather than being filled in with something
invented (`BUILD.md` §4.3, §2.5, §7).
The release is also final: once a founder has been let in — whether the pass
ended, failed, or simply ran past ten minutes — no later visit puts them back
in front of the wait, so a pass that never reports itself finished cannot
strand the founder who returns a week later.
Whether a first draft is in the calendar on arrival is the generation
requirement's promise; this one owns the release, and every return to the
product after setup was submitted lands where this requirement says.
Placement: JN-002 step 9 ("Watches the deep pass run and arrives inside the app
— and, where it found less than a month of pages to write, is told that on
arrival rather than weeks later").

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given the founder has submitted setup, when they are on the progress screen
   while the deep pass is running — whether they stayed on it or came back to it
   within the window criterion 6 sets — then they see which step is under way in
   written words rather than a bare spinner, the step named is the step actually
   running, the screen shows them at least once every 30 seconds that the pass
   is still running, and nothing on the screen states how long the pass has
   taken or has left: no duration, no estimate, no countdown, no clock and no
   percentage of time.
2. Given the deep pass completes, when it ends, then the founder is taken into
   the app without any further action from them.
3. Given the deep pass degraded or could not complete part of its measurement,
   when it ends, then the founder is released into the app all the same, and one
   written sentence tells them what could not be measured; that sentence stands
   for as long as the most recent completed measurement of their site is one that
   fell short — naming what that measurement could not measure, never what an
   earlier one could not — and stops being shown as soon as the most recent
   completed measurement of that site is one that completed in full, the
   founder's own late-finishing pass or a weekly re-measurement alike.
4. Given the pass produced no opportunities at all, when the founder lands in
   the app, then the emptiness is stated plainly and no page, proposal, rival or
   number is invented to fill it.
5. Given a pass that fails outright, or that has not ended 10 minutes after
   setup was submitted, when that point is reached, then the founder is released
   into the app all the same, one written sentence tells them the measurement
   did not complete, and the wait is over for that founder for good — whatever
   the pass does afterwards, they are never put back on the progress screen; and
   where that pass does finish later, its results reach the app the founder is
   already in with nothing required of them, and that sentence stops being shown
   once they do.
6. Given a founder who has submitted setup, when they return — signing in
   minutes or weeks later (REQ-024 criterion 4, "returned to where they left
   off"; REQ-025 criterion 4, "not asked the three decisions again") — then they
   land in the app itself, with no re-run of the pass, and the screen they arrive
   at carries whichever of the two sentences criteria 3 and 5 promise its own
   criterion is keeping shown at that moment, rather than that sentence having
   been shown only at the moment of release. Each sentence begins and ends on its
   own criterion's terms and on no other's: criterion 5's sentence ending does not
   end criterion 3's, so where a pass released under criterion 5 finishes later
   and finishes degraded, the arrival screen carries criterion 3's sentence from
   that moment on. Where neither criterion is keeping its sentence shown, the
   screen carries neither. They are shown the progress screen only while their own
   pass is still running and fewer than the 10 minutes of criterion 5 have passed
   since they submitted setup, and past that point they land in the app whatever
   state the pass is in.

## Non-goals
- Holding the founder out of the app until a first draft exists.
- Showing a provisional or estimated score while the pass runs.
- Stating how long the pass, or the first page, will take — no such duration has
  been measured.
- Retrying or re-configuring the deep pass from the progress screen.
- Emailing the founder to come back later instead of releasing them.
- Which elements of the progress screen move to show the founder the pass is
  still running, and how a step's own progress is composed — the blueprint that
  owns that screen chooses both; criterion 1 asks only that the founder can see
  in words which step is running and that it is still running, and reads no
  duration.
