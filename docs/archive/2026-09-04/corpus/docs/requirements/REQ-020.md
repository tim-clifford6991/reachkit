# REQ-020 — Buying requires no account and no configuration first

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder who has just read my free report, I want to buy by paying, so that
I am not made to create an account or fill in forms before I have anything.

## Rationale
The founder arrives convinced but uninvested, and every screen between the
decision and the payment is a place to lose them; nothing the product needs
from them cannot be asked after the money has moved, at setup (REQ-025).
Payment is therefore the first thing asked and the only thing asked, no
password is ever chosen, and an address with no account is answered in writing —
truthfully, and without being sold to twice — rather than left at a silent dead
end; where no address has been given at all, as at a signed-out setup or app
URL, the founder is asked for one rather than shown a guess about a payment
nobody has named (`BUILD.md` §3, §13).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a founder on a surface that offers the product, when they choose to
   start, then the next thing asked of them is payment — no account creation,
   no password, no questionnaire and no product configuration is presented
   first.
2. Given a founder who leaves checkout without paying, when they return to the
   surface they came from, then no account, site or subscription exists for
   them and what they were reading is unchanged.
3. Given a founder who completes payment, when the payment succeeds, then they
   are told in writing that a sign-in link is on its way to the address they
   paid with, and are never asked to choose a password.
4. Given an address for which no account has ever been opened — never a customer
   whose account exists and whose paid-through date has passed, who is still
   sent a link and can still sign in (REQ-076 criterion 5) — when it asks for a
   sign-in link, then no link is sent and no access is granted, and one written
   line answers: where a completed payment is held against that address, that
   the payment was received and the account is being opened, with no further
   purchase offered (REQ-024 criterion 6); and otherwise, that there is no
   ReachKit account for that address, with the way to buy.
5. Given a setup or app URL opened with no signed-in account — a stranger, or
   the founder who paid and whose session has since ended — when the page is
   requested, then no access is granted and they are asked for their address so
   a sign-in link can be sent, and nothing is said about whether any account or
   payment exists until an address has been given (criterion 4).
6. Given a founder at checkout, when they are asked for anything, then it is
   only what taking the payment needs — their card details, the address the
   sign-in link and receipt go to, and the billing details REQ-022 criteria 5
   and 6 record — and their company, their role, and what they mean to use the
   product for are asked nowhere before the payment completes, no name being
   asked beyond what taking the card payment itself requires.

## Non-goals
- A separate sign-up step, a password, or social login anywhere on this path —
  criteria 1 and 3 carry the customer's promise; the emailed link as the only
  credential afterwards is REQ-024's.
- A free trial, a freemium tier, or discount codes at checkout — the single
  plan is REQ-022 criterion 3's.
- Sales-assisted purchase, quotes, or invoicing outside Stripe Checkout.
