# REQ-051 — No invented people appear in a generated page

> Superseded by REQ-050, which absorbed this requirement's acceptance criteria
> verbatim (2026-08-30; see `requirements/history/REQ-050-2026-08-30-requirement-cleanup.md`).
> Retired in place; cite REQ-050, not this file.

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer who is the publisher of record, I want no page to carry an
author, biography or persona that does not exist, so that nothing published in
my name misrepresents who wrote it.

## Rationale
A fabricated byline, invented quotation or made-up testimonial is a
misrepresentation the customer carries the consequences for, so a generated page
attributes itself to the customer or to nobody, and every person it names and
every remark it reports is one the reader can check in a source the page links.
Source: `BUILD.md` §8 ("No invented people: no
generated bylines/bios/personas. Customer identity or none") and §14.2.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a draft is generated, when it is queued for review, then the only author
   it may attribute itself to is the customer — the business name or
   account-holder name recorded for their site — and it carries no other byline,
   no author biography and no persona; and every person it names or quotes other
   than the customer themselves is named in a public page the draft links. The
   customer's own attribution needs no such link.
2. Given the site has no recorded business name or account-holder name to
   attribute to, when a page publishes, then it publishes with no author
   attribution rather than a generated one.
3. Given a draft is generated, when it is queued for review, then it carries no
   testimonial, review, endorsement or reported customer remark, whether or not
   it names a speaker, unless the draft links a public page carrying that same
   remark.
4. Given a generated draft that fails any of criteria 1 to 3, when the pipeline
   finishes, then it is not queued for review and the item follows the hard-rule
   recovery path (REQ-053 criterion 3).

## Non-goals
- Author profiles, team pages or contributor management.
- Removing or rewriting a person, quotation or testimonial the customer
  themselves adds during an edit — criteria 1 and 3 bind what ReachKit generates.
- Where the customer's name is attached to a published page and how it renders —
  the blueprint's; criteria 1 and 2 fix only that it is theirs or nobody's.
