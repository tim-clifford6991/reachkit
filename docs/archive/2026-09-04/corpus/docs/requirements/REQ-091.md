# REQ-091 — A domain that ranks for nothing still gets a working product

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder whose site ranks for nothing — the customer this product exists for
— I want every screen, measurement and proposal to work on my first day, so that
the product proves its case instead of showing me blanks where my numbers should
be.

## Rationale
Cold start is the default customer, so what must be true is that no derivation,
dataset or sentence in the product assumes the customer already has presence,
and that a module holding nothing on their first day is on the screen telling
them what will fill it, rather than blank or absent until it has data; the
surface-by-surface promises (REQ-008, REQ-041, REQ-047, REQ-026, REQ-071,
REQ-029) each hold one screen, and this holds the law across every screen the
product renders, including screens added later. Mail is not one of those screens: REQ-064 is the single home for what a
mail states when a section is unmeasurable or measured-empty, so the law reaches
the inbox in that requirement's terms and is not restated here. Source:
`BUILD.md` §6.6 — "**The cold-start law: every derivation in the product must
work for a domain that ranks for nothing.** Cold start is the default customer
… so no dataset, module, or sentence may assume presence"; "Every
empty-at-cold-start module states what fills it: "appears after your first pages
rank", never a blank"; and "Score: SearchPresence 0 → Presence floors at 1 → a
clean cold-start site scores ~10–15, Invisible."

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a domain with no measured rankings and no AI citations of its own, when
   any scan, derivation, proposal or generation runs for it, then it runs to
   completion and returns its result — which may legitimately be empty (REQ-029
   criterion 4; REQ-047 criterion 3) — and no step fails, blocks, or is skipped
   for want of prior presence.
2. Given that same domain, when any screen the product renders for it is
   displayed — the report or any screen inside the app, including screens added
   later — then no blank, dash or placeholder value stands anywhere a value
   would sit were the customer already present, and no module is hidden,
   dropped or collapsed for holding nothing yet; each such place, and each such
   module — including one with no value position at all, such as a list with no
   entries or a chart with no series — carries exactly one written line, and that
   line tells the customer they have no presence yet and what will fill it. Where
   a sibling requirement already fixes the wording for a place, that fixed line is
   the one line and must itself say both things; where none does, this criterion
   is what the line must say. A domain that simply has no presence yet is never
   accounted for as a measurement that could not be taken (REQ-004 criterion 8).
3. Given that same domain, when any screen the product renders for it is
   displayed, then nothing stands in the place of what the customer does not
   have: no estimate, benchmark, cohort or category average is presented where a
   measurement of their own domain would sit, and no rival, target, question or
   opportunity is manufactured to fill a list that came out empty. Each such
   place carries criterion 2's written line instead.

## Non-goals
- Whether an absence is a measured zero or a measurement that could not be
  taken, and how each is stored and how each is shown — REQ-004 criteria 6 to 12
  are the single home for that rule, and a cold start is never hidden behind it
  (REQ-008).
- What a mail says when a section is unmeasurable or measured-empty — REQ-064
  owns the inbox; criteria 2 and 3 reach the screens the product renders.
- Substituting an estimate, benchmark or cohort average where the customer has
  no data of their own (criterion 3).
- Producing rivals, targets, questions or opportunities where none exist
  (criterion 3) — finishing with no rival is legal (REQ-026 criterion 11,
  REQ-071 criterion 16), as is a day with no page (REQ-047 criterion 3).
- Removing, hiding or deferring a module until the data that fills it exists;
  criterion 2 keeps it on the screen carrying its written line, and it holds
  values from the moment there are values to hold.
- How rivals, targets or questions are derived — engine, not promise
  (`BUILD.md` §6.6, §6.7).
- The wording of a place's line where a sibling requirement already fixes one for
  it — Overview's chart before the first weekly measurement (REQ-041 criterion
  3), a calendar date carrying no page (REQ-043 criteria 3 and 5). That line is
  the place's one account and criterion 2 adds no second one; criterion 2 still
  fixes what it has to say, so a fixed line that names no presence and no filler
  fails criterion 2 rather than escaping it.
