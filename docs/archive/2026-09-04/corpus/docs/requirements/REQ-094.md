# REQ-094 — The free report shows the market it measured in and lets one reader correct it

- Priority: Must

## User story
As anyone reading a free report — the founder whose own site was read as the
wrong kind of business, or the owner of a business somebody else scanned — I want
the report to show which market it judged the domain in and to let me correct it,
so that every number underneath is a measurement of the business that actually
exists.

## Rationale
The market is inferred from a homepage, so when the inference is wrong every
number under it is wrong too, and a reader who cannot see which market was used
cannot tell a measured verdict from a guess. The report must therefore show the
category on its face while saying so plainly where the scan never reached one,
offer one correction to whoever is reading while the report is still current, say
that the searches themselves disagree with the category — and say instead that
coherence could not be judged where too few searches were measured to judge it —
re-measure against the corrected market rather than re-labelling the old numbers,
and come to rest: a reader gets one correction that worked, or two attempts that
did not, and then the report stands. The coherence threshold and its floor, the
one retry, the age at which the offer ends, and the conditions that refuse a
correction before it runs are derived in `registry/evidence/REQ-094.md`; the cost
bound behind one correction per scan and the source clause it comes from are in
`registry/evidence/REQ-014.md`. A correction reads only the AI answers Google had
already cached, so criterion 8 is what keeps the corrected card's count readable
for what it is; why the correction buys no further AI answers is
`decisions/ADR-094.md` and `blueprints/BP-028.md` decision 4, cited and not
restated. Placement: JN-001 step 7 and JN-006 step 2.
Source: `BUILD.md` §6.7 step 5 ("Coherence check + correction"), whose "re-runs
steps 2–5" is why criterion 3 re-measures the market and everything resting on it
and leaves the domain's own pages alone. No implementation exists to touch: this
repo carries no `src/`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given any stored report less than 7 days old, when it renders, then the
   category the report measured is shown on its face — whether the report measured
   everything or only part of it (REQ-001 criterion 12), and not only where the
   market looks wrong — together with a control to correct it wherever a
   correction is still available (criteria 5 and 7). Where the scan never
   determined a category at all — it stopped early (REQ-003 criteria 5 and 11), or
   could not read the pages a category is inferred from (REQ-004 criterion 6) —
   no category is named and none is guessed in its place, what REQ-004 criterion 6
   or 9 requires of that unmeasured input stands there instead, and the control is
   offered there too: correcting it supplies the category the scan never reached.
   Given a stored report 7 days old or older, when it renders, then the category
   it measured is still shown and no correction control is offered; at that age the
   reader's route is the re-scan REQ-001 criterion 15 offers, whose report carries
   a correction of its own (criterion 6).
2. Given a report that measured three or more searches, when it renders, then one
   written line states that the market may have been read wrongly and points at
   criterion 1's control, exactly when no single domain appears in the top ten of
   as many of those measured searches as the threshold — a quarter of the searches
   the report measured, rounded up, or two, whichever is the greater. That
   threshold is three where twelve searches were measured, and is never the whole
   number measured, so the same share of the market is asked for at every
   denominator (REQ-006 criterion 1). Given a report that measured fewer than
   three searches — which a market that yielded that few (REQ-006 criterion 1) or
   a scan that stopped early (REQ-001 criterion 14) can produce — when it renders,
   then no line says the market may have been read wrongly, and one written line
   instead says that coherence could not be judged and points at that same
   control.
3. Given a reader who submits a corrected category, when the correction is
   accepted, then the market set, the questions, and every figure measured from
   them — the AI answers on criterion 8's terms, the Google presence, and the
   drivers and score that rest
   on those — are measured again against the corrected category and the report
   re-renders from them, while what the scan read from the domain's own pages is
   not re-read and does not change. That re-measurement is the free scan it
   corrects still measuring: it spends no further scan allowance and opens no
   second spend ceiling (REQ-003 criteria 6 and 11), and its own wait is bounded
   as any scan's wait is, timed from the moment the correction was accepted
   (REQ-003 criteria 1, 2 and 5) — the time the reader took to decide counts
   against no bound. The corrected report is one scan's report throughout,
   carrying one measurement date, the moment that scan's measurement finished;
   every figure under that date comes from that one scan and none from another
   (REQ-004 criterion 12), and the figures carried forward unchanged from the
   domain's own pages were read less than 7 days before the correction started
   (criterion 1).
4. Given a corrected report, when any later visitor opens that domain's report
   address, then they see the corrected report — the correction replaces what was
   stored for that domain on REQ-001 criterion 17's terms — and no control or
   address returns anyone to the pre-correction report.
5. Given a report whose one correction has been accepted and has produced a
   corrected report, when any visitor opens it, then no correction control is
   offered, one written line says a free scan allows one correction and that this
   one has been used, and the corrected report stays readable. Given a correction
   already running for that report, when a second is submitted, then it is refused
   in writing as a correction already under way rather than as one already used,
   and whoever was refused is left where the running correction leaves them: the
   corrected report if it produced one (criterion 4), or the control still
   available if it did not (criterion 7).
6. Given a domain re-scanned into a new stored report — the re-scan an incomplete
   report offers (REQ-001 criterion 14) or the one its age earns (REQ-001
   criterion 15) — when that report is opened, then one correction is available
   for it: one per free scan, and a re-scan is a new free scan, whatever the
   report before it measured or failed to. Nothing but a new stored report renews
   that one: a correction never earns another correction of its own (criterion 5),
   and the single retry a correction that produced no report earns (criterion 7)
   is spent by being taken, whether or not it succeeds — so past that retry a
   second correction always costs a second scan, and the only scans on offer are
   the ones REQ-001 criteria 13 to 15 offer.
7. Given a correction that ran and produced no report, when the reader returns to
   the report address, then the report stored before the correction is still there
   under its own measurement date and its original category (REQ-001 criterion
   17), the reader is told in writing that the correction did not complete — in
   the one written line REQ-001 criterion 16 shows while it shows one, never a
   second line beside it — and the correction may be submitted once more on
   criterion 1's terms, that retry being the one control REQ-001 criterion 16
   admits there. Given that retry also ran and produced no report, when the reader
   returns, then the report stands — its original category, its own measurement
   date, and one written line saying the correction could not be completed — and
   no correction control is offered for that report again. Given a correction
   refused before it ran, because free scanning is paused or switched off or a
   scan is already running for that network (REQ-003 criteria 7 and 8), when the
   reader is told which limit was met, then neither attempt has been used and the
   control still stands: nothing was measured and nothing was spent. A correction
   whose re-measurement stopped early does produce the stored report — incomplete,
   on REQ-003 criterion 11's terms — and that correction has been used.
8. Given a corrected report, when its AI-answers card renders, then one written
   line states that its AI-answer count covers only the AI answers Google had
   already cached for the corrected market's searches, and that line is shown on
   every corrected report whether or not that count differs from the one the
   report before it carried. A search whose AI
   answer the correction did not read is shown as one the correction did not
   measure — never as a search on which no AI answer appeared, and never as a
   place the customer was absent from (REQ-006 criteria 2 and 10).

## Non-goals
- No repeated or unlimited corrections on the free path: one accepted correction
  per free scan, and one retry where a correction runs and produces no report —
  stated as criteria 5 and 7.
- No correction of a report 7 days old or older; at that age the route is the
  re-scan REQ-001 criterion 15 offers, stated as criterion 1.
- No proof that whoever corrects a report controls the domain, and no correction
  by a proven owner that outranks a stranger's or binds later free scans — both
  deferred to v1.1 with the proof of control they rest on (REQ-002).
- No editing of individual questions, searches, or rivals on the free report.
- No confirming or freezing of the market on the free path; the category is
  confirmed after payment, at setup (REQ-026), and changed afterwards in Settings
  (REQ-071 criterion 1).
- No re-reading of the domain's own pages on a correction; Foundations and
  Answerability are measured once per scan (criterion 3).
- No further AI answers bought for a corrected report; the corrected card counts
  what Google had already cached and says so — stated as criterion 8, not left to
  this list.
