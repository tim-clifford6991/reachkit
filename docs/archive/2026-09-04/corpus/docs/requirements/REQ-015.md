# REQ-015 — The free funnel says so in writing when it hits a limit

> Superseded by REQ-003, which absorbed this requirement's acceptance criteria
> verbatim as its criteria 6–12 (2026-08-30; see
> `requirements/history/REQ-003-2026-08-30-requirement-cleanup.md`). Retired in
> place; cite REQ-003, not this file. Of the two `REVIEW(...)` lines below, the
> first died with the seam the merge closed; the second stands, restated with
> current citations, on REQ-003.

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder who has scanned a few domains in one sitting, who arrived while the
free funnel was full, or whose scan ran into the free path's spend ceiling, I
want to be told plainly which limit I met and what happens next, so that I do
not read a bounded product as a broken one.

## Rationale
The free path spends real money on every scan, so it is bounded — and a founder
who meets one of those bounds must be told plainly, in writing, what happened
and how long it lasts, must never be shown another visitor's scan, and must
never meet a limit as a hang, a crash, or a half-built page they cannot read as
a limit. A run that reaches its spend ceiling mid-scan is the same promise from
the other side: it gives back what it measured rather than throwing away the
wait; the evidence that a spend ceiling degrades a scan rather than failing it
is in `registry/evidence/REQ-015.md`. Source: `BUILD.md` §11 (the free path's
bounds and the fail-open / fail-closed split).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a visitor who has already started five free scans from the same network
   within the last hour — every way a scan starts counting against that
   allowance (REQ-003 c1), except a re-measurement after a market correction,
   which belongs to the scan it corrects and consumes nothing further — when
   another scan would start for them, then it does not start, they are refused
   with one written line saying how long until they may scan again, and no
   partial report is shown.
2. Given a scan is already running from the visitor's network, when another scan
   would start for them, then no second scan starts; if the running scan is of
   the domain they asked for they are returned to it, and if it is not they are
   refused in writing, told how long until they may scan, and are never shown a
   scan or a report of a domain they did not ask for.
3. Given the day's free-scan ceiling has been reached, or scanning has been
   switched off, when a visitor tries to scan, then one written line tells them
   free scanning is paused — stating how long until it resumes when the day's
   ceiling is the cause, and promising no time when scanning has been switched
   off — and every already-stored report stays readable.
4. Given the scan limiter itself is unavailable, when a visitor starts a scan,
   then the scan proceeds.
5. Given lead capture is unavailable, when a visitor submits their email address
   for the free page, then the submission is refused in writing and no
   confirmation of delivery is shown.
6. Given a free scan that reaches its spend ceiling while it is running, when it
   ends, then only optional measurements are skipped — optional meaning no
   driver of the score depends on them, so that skipping them never changes the
   score and never withholds it — and the visitor is shown the report of
   everything measured up to that point, the skipped parts reading as not
   measured (REQ-005), with one written line stating the scan stopped early and
   the report is incomplete — never an error, a hang, or a refusal.
7. Given a visitor refused under criterion 1 or 2 at the report address of a
   domain with no stored report, when the address loads, then it shows that
   refusal in writing and starts no scan — never a blank page, a 404, or an
   error (REQ-002) — and once the limit has passed the same address starts a scan
   for them, unless that domain's report was removed at its owner's request
   (REQ-002 criterion 8, "starts no scan for anyone"), when it never does and
   what loads is what REQ-012 criterion 7 states.

## Non-goals
- No waitlist, queue position, or captcha on the free scan.
- No account, sign-in, or paid tier that raises the free allowance.
- No spend, cost or cap figure shown to a visitor.
- No effect of these bounds on a paying customer's scans (`BUILD.md` §11 bounds
  are the free path's).
- The order in which a report address's removal, cooldown and limit checks are
  evaluated: the blueprint's, derived from this requirement's promise that a
  visitor who meets a limit is always told so in writing and from REQ-002
  criterion 8's, that a removed domain is never scanned again for anyone.

## Open questions

- [ ] REVIEW(gap): criterion 7 covers only a visitor refused under criteria 1 or
      2. A visitor opening the report address of a domain with no stored report
      while the day's ceiling is reached or scanning is switched off (criterion
      3) has no criterion saying what loads there or that no scan starts —
      criterion 3's Given is a visitor who "tries to scan", and REQ-002 c3 no
      longer maps address states, naming this requirement as one of the owners.
- [ ] REVIEW(gap): criterion 6 has no case for the spend ceiling being reached
      while a measurement a score driver depends on is still outstanding. "Only
      optional measurements are skipped" then leaves the build to either exceed
      the ceiling or drop a driver, and REQ-005 c4 contemplates a driver being
      among the work cut off at this very ceiling.
