# REQ-043, REQ-056, REQ-057, REQ-062, REQ-063 — reverted `approved` → `in-review` by ADR-084, 2026-09-01 (architect, `/decide`)

The owner ruled on 2026-09-01 that content is published complete rather than as
a draft, at every destination including the customer's own WordPress. ADR-084
records the ruling and supersedes ADR-082. `decide.md` binds the architect to
send back through the gate every requirement whose promise the ruling changes:
"a ruling that changes a promise is not a quiet edit; the requirement it touches
goes back through the gate."

**No criterion text was edited by this pass.** Wording is the
requirements-analyst's and approval is the owner's; ADR-084's Consequences names
what each requirement must now say differently, and nothing more was done here
than the status field.

## Moved

| REQ | Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| REQ-043 | approved | in-review | The ruling asserts a promise the approved text does not carry — that the calendar is where a page is found again with its status, a link to the customer's CMS and an unpublish action. Criterion 2 carries the status and criterion 9 permits the action; neither makes the day panel that place, and no requirement promises a per-page link into the customer's own WordPress. |
| REQ-056 | approved | in-review | Criterion 6's second arm ("where the destination received the page for the customer to publish themselves, a written line saying so in place of an address") has no destination left to describe. Criterion 16's second outcome becomes unreachable in practice. |
| REQ-057 | approved | in-review | Criterion 9 governs the telling for "a destination that receives it as a draft for the customer to publish themselves (REQ-060 criterion 1)" and now describes nobody. |
| REQ-062 | approved | in-review | The non-goal "Verifying a page delivered to a destination as a draft the customer must publish themselves — it has no live address (REQ-060)" is false; criterion 1 now reaches WordPress pages. |
| REQ-063 | approved | in-review | The non-goal "one delivered to a destination as a draft they never made live (REQ-060) — neither is live to be judged" is false; WordPress pages now receive weekly verdicts. |

## Already in-review, unmoved

REQ-060 and REQ-079 were already `in-review` from the review cycle of
2026-09-01. Both are inside this ruling's blast radius — REQ-060 criteria 1 and
2 are the criteria overturned, and REQ-079 criterion 4's two WordPress
populations invert — and ADR-084 names what each must now say differently.

## Assessed and deliberately not moved

- **REQ-074** — a WordPress credential that can create posts but not publish
  them is a new *cause*, not a new *state*: it reads as `failing`, one of the
  three states criterion 1 already closes over, with the Reconnect action
  criterion 2 already offers (ADR-084 Decision 3). Criterion 4's "pages already
  published to it stay live" becomes more true, not less.
- **REQ-028** — criterion 5's "held as a draft they can read" is ReachKit's own
  `in_review`, not a WordPress post status.
- **REQ-075** — criterion 2's "a published page on the calendar with its live
  URL" becomes true of WordPress pages too. Strengthened, not broken.
- **REQ-045, REQ-059, REQ-064, REQ-070** — no sentence in any of them turns on
  whether a WordPress post is live.
- **REQ-029, REQ-041, REQ-064, REQ-071** are `in-review` or `approved` for
  reasons unrelated to this ruling and are untouched by it.

## Owed elsewhere

**JN-003 step 6** reads "Finds the page live the next morning on their own
domain, **or waiting as a draft in their own WordPress** …". The second clause is
now false. Journeys are the requirements-analyst's directory and an owner gate;
this pass did not touch JN-003 and records that it is owed.
