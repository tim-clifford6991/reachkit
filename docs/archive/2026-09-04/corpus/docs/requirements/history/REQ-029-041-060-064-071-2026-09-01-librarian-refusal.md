# REQ-029, REQ-041, REQ-060, REQ-064, REQ-071 — status refused, 2026-09-01 (librarian)

Five requirements, one clause, one blanket approval. `status: approved` →
`status: in-review` on each. Recorded, not enforced (rule 3.3): this repository
has no remote, no CI status check, no branch protection and no pre-commit hook —
`.git/hooks/` holds only the shipped `.sample` files.

## Clause that failed

**Rule 3.4, final sentence** — "A draft carrying open `REVIEW(...)` lines is not
presented as ready."

Each of the five carries at least one unchecked `- [ ] REVIEW(...)` line, and
each line was **already in the file at the moment of approval**. All five were
approved in one status-only commit, `011869d docs(reqs): owner approves 32
requirements and JN-005`; the text at that commit carries the counts below.
Rule 3.1's own precedent is a verifier refusing a blanket approval from the
owner, and this is one.

| id | open review lines at 011869d | kind |
|---|---|---|
| REQ-029 | 1 | `REVIEW(gap)` — criterion 5 states when its "measurement did not complete" |
| REQ-041 | 1 | `REVIEW(ambiguity: criterion 4)` — "headline number" is never defined, and the goal obligation makes the scope load-bearing |
| REQ-060 | 1 | `REVIEW(ambiguity)` — criteria 2 and 4 name no surface for the one written line |
| REQ-064 | 3 | two `REVIEW(conflict …)` naming REQ-057 and REQ-074/REQ-079/REQ-025, one `REVIEW(gap)` |
| REQ-071 | 1 | `REVIEW(untestable: criteria 12 and 13)` — "leaving the limb with no pass condition for most of the numbers those criteria cover" |

REQ-071's is the sharpest: an acceptance criterion its own reviewer records as
having no pass condition cannot be the source a work order's test plan quotes
verbatim, and five work orders (WO-089…WO-093) quote it.

REQ-064's two conflict lines are also rule 2.3 violations independent of this
one: a conflict is a `blocked-by` edge naming the other artifact, and neither
REQ-064 nor REQ-057, REQ-074, REQ-079 or REQ-025 carries one. BP-053 is the only
node in the corpus that recorded this conflict as an edge
(`blocked-by: [REQ-064]`), and it recorded it downstream of the requirement that
declares it.

## What is *not* claimed here

The blueprints cut from these five did not fail their own gate: BP-036, BP-038,
BP-048, BP-053 and BP-056 each certified against a requirement that was
`approved` when they were written, and four of the five answer the review line
structurally in their own `## Decisions` and say so in `## Status grounds`. The
refusal is of the approval, not of their reasoning. Its downstream consequence —
§3's blueprint gate now unmet for those nodes — is reported to the architect and
the owner, not written into the architect's files.

## What would clear the refusal

Per requirement, one of:

- the reviewer's line is discharged and deleted (rule 2.1: answered questions
  are deleted once ruled), then the owner approves; or
- the analyst folds the answer into the criterion text, then the owner approves;
  or
- for REQ-064's two conflict lines specifically, the conflict is converted to a
  `blocked-by` edge on the artifact that declares it (rule 2.3) and routed to
  `/decide`, as REQ-056's conflict was and as ADR-081 then settled.

No criterion was edited by this refusal. The librarian does not own requirement
text (§4).
