## REQ-029 — two `REVIEW(ambiguity)` lines folded

Criterion 3's end had two limbs that disagreed when a *later* measurement also
fell short: the "most recent completed measurement is the one that fell short"
limb had stopped being true while the "a later measurement completes in full"
limb had not fired, so a build could keep showing the onboarding pass's list of
unmeasured parts after a differently-degraded weekly re-measure. Both limbs now
test the same thing — the most recent completed measurement — and the sentence
names what *that* measurement could not measure, never what an earlier one could
not. Derived from BP-036 decision 3 ("The degraded sentence is a projection of
the current report, never a stored flag"): a projection of the current report can
only name what the current report lacks.

Criterion 6 conditioned the sentence on the original pass ("where their pass
degraded, failed or did not complete"), a condition true forever, while criterion
3 now ends it — so the same arrival screen was required to carry the sentence and
forbidden to. Criterion 6 now defers to criteria 3 and 5 for both the start and
the end of the sentence and states no condition of its own.

A `Placement:` line was added to the rationale. Placement was already real —
JN-002 step 9's `exercises` names REQ-029 — but the requirement's own prose did
not say so, and its siblings all do (rule 5.7).

## REQ-041 — one `REVIEW(gap: criterion 4)` folded

Criterion 4's closing clause classified criteria 10 and 11's counts as context
values and left criterion 11's ratio unclassified — so a build had to either mint
a fifth pinned goal nobody ruled or render a headline bare. Ruled: the ratio is a
context value, not a headline number, and the gap module leads with no headline
number at all. Derived, not chosen — one ratio is shown per confirmed competitor
(`design/components.md`, `RivalSparkline`: "Per rival: name, falling series,
endpoint … Two arms: absolute (rivals' counts beside the customer's own) and
ratio"), and criterion 4 allows a module "exactly one and never two", so up to
five per-rival ratios cannot be one module's headline. Consistent with BP-038
decision 1 ("a headline number is the value passed to a module's `headline`
slot … Four modules lead with a number, so there are four headline numbers and
four goals"). No goal is invented and nothing renders bare: criterion 11 already
pairs the ratio with its previous value.

   names "the date the rebuilding re-measurement ran (criterion 7)" and criterion

   re-measurement in both cases, never the save, which this requirement keeps
   deliberately apart everywhere else.

   criterion 4's definition of which numbers carry goals; with no edge, a change

   The two neighbours are the planner's and the architect's to update; this
   requirement declares no `blocked-by`, because nothing about it is blocked — the
