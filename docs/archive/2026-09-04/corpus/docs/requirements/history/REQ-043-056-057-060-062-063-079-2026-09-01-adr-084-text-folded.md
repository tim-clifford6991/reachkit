## What changed

**REQ-060** — user story ("land in my own site as drafts" → published on their
own site); rationale rewritten, dropping the transcribed `BUILD.md` §9 "posts as
draft" clause the ruling overturned and citing the ruling (ADR-084) as source;
criterion 1 inverted (publicly readable from the moment delivery completes, no
further action of theirs); criterion 2 rewritten — `published` now means the
same at both destinations; the "no live address", the waiting-in-WordPress
apparatus and "sends the customer no mail of its own" are gone (the address on
the record is REQ-056 criterion 6's and the `published` mail is REQ-062
criterion 5's, rule 2.4); non-goals "Publishing a page live on WordPress on the
customer's behalf" and "The 24-hour liveness verification …" deleted, replaced
by one non-goal closing a draft-delivery *setting* (ADR-084 alternative 3).
Criteria 3, 4, 5, 6 and the `rests-on` row untouched.

**REQ-056** — criterion 6's second arm (a written line in place of an address)
deleted; the address arm is now unconditional. Criterion 16 is **unchanged**,
including its second outcome — ADR-084 Decision 4 keeps the arm whose population
is now empty, and narrowing the criterion to today's population is the deletion
its warning block forbids. Placement quotes updated to JN-003's new step 6 and
step 9.

**REQ-057** — criterion 9 **repurposed, not deleted**: the residual promise is
that the telling names the site the page goes to and says it will be published
and readable there, never that it will wait for the customer to publish it. That
promise is not carried by criteria 1, 7 or 8, which name the page, the reason,
the interval and the action but never the destination. Placement quote updated.

**REQ-062** — the false non-goal deleted; replaced with a non-goal that ReachKit
changes nothing inside a site it does not serve because a check failed there.
Criteria unchanged: criterion 1 was written against "a destination that serves
it publicly" and now reaches WordPress with no new rule.

**REQ-063** — the non-goal's draft clause deleted; the unpublished-page clause
stands. Criteria unchanged.

**REQ-079** — criterion 4's naming clause was scoped to "every post left in
their own site *for them to remove*", which was the never-made-live population
and is now the empty one; it now names every post ReachKit created that is still
in their own site, whichever of the two arms it fell in. Both arms kept.
Criterion 6's mail no longer says the posts "stand there as drafts" — that
sentence counted the never-live population; it now carries the state criterion 4
leaves them in, so it stays true whichever arm each post took.

**REQ-043** — one new criterion 12 (count 11 → 12, at rule 2.1's soft budget):
the day detail is where a delivered page's stage, a way through to the page
where it now lives, and unpublish are all reachable without another screen, and
for a WordPress page that way through opens the post inside their own WordPress
and not only its public address. This is the one genuinely new promise in the
ruling. One non-goal added (no editing at the destination from this view); one
rationale paragraph added; `depends-on` gains REQ-060.

**JN-003** — step 6's "or waiting as a draft in their own WordPress" replaced;
step 9 now names finding a page again on the calendar and gains REQ-043 in
`exercises`, which is what makes REQ-043 criterion 12's placement real (rule
5.7). Status dropped to `in-review`.

## Assessed and deliberately not edited

  pre-delivery state for a page whose destination is not connected, not a post

- **REQ-061 criterion 4** ("since ReachKit never made it live") — false under the
  ruling, but REQ-061 is `status: superseded`; its criterion 4 was rehomed to
  REQ-056 criterion 16 on 2026-08-31. A superseded requirement is retired in
  place, not rewritten.
- **REQ-074, REQ-075, REQ-045, REQ-064** — assessed, nothing turns on whether a
  WordPress post is live (REQ-075 criterion 2 and REQ-074 criterion 4 are
  strengthened, as the earlier entry recorded).
