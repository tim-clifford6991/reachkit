# REQ-056 — ADR-081 folded into criterion 16, 2026-09-01 (requirements-analyst)

Answers the librarian's refusal of 2026-09-01 (`REQ-056-2026-09-01-librarian-refusal.md`),
steps 1 and 2 of its "What would clear the refusal". Status left `in-review`;
approval is the owner's.

## Changed

- **Criterion 16** now states both populations ADR-081 Decision 1 separates,
  where before it stated only the never-live one. A page ReachKit itself
  published live in the customer's WordPress is returned to draft there with
  nothing else about the post changed; a page ReachKit created but never made
  live is named for removal and nothing is written into the site for it. The
  last sentence carries ADR-081 Decision 2's consequence in customer terms — a
  page the customer made live themselves is not a page ReachKit made live.
  ADR-081 Decision 2's discriminator (`made_live_by_us` on the publication row)
  is mechanism and stays in BP-045; the criterion states only the outcome.
- **Non-goal** "Deleting or editing posts inside the customer's own WordPress
  … criterion 16 names the post for the customer to remove and touches nothing"
  was false once the was-live arm exists. It now excepts the one field the
  was-live arm writes and routes the rest to criterion 16 as behaviour.
- **The open `REVIEW(conflict with REQ-079 criterion 4)` line was deleted**, not
  answered in place: rule 2.1, an answered question is deleted once ruled, and
  ADR-081 (`accepted`, 2026-08-31) is the ruling.

## Not changed

The `returned_to_draft` population is empty today — REQ-060 criterion 1 stands
("never made live by us"), so no WordPress page this product can create is live.
Criterion 16's was-live arm is written for a population that has no members yet
and is not narrowed to match today's implementation; ADR-081's Consequences and
its opening warning are the record of why.
