# REQ-079 — ADR-081 folded into criterion 4, 2026-09-01 (requirements-analyst)

Answers the librarian's refusal of 2026-09-01
(`REQ-079-2026-09-01-librarian-refusal.md`), step 1 of its "What would clear the
refusal". Status left `in-review`; approval is the owner's.

## Changed

- **Criterion 4** said, unscoped, that "a page ReachKit published to WordPress
  is returned to draft there". It now names both populations and points at
  REQ-056 criterion 16 as their home: a page ReachKit itself published live is
  returned to draft, a page it created but never made live is named for removal
  and never written into. The unreachable-destination clause is unchanged.
- **Non-goal "Deleting content from the customer's own destination"** carried
  the whole scoping rule in its body, where nothing derives a test from it. The
  promise now lives in criterion 4; the non-goal keeps only what is genuinely
  out of scope — deleting anything, and touching content ReachKit did not create.
- **Non-goal "Whether a mail may be sent at all"** quoted REQ-064 criterion 6
  and REQ-064's non-goals with text neither carries, and called the register
  eight kinds where the mail blueprint's table (BP-016) holds nine. The second
  copy is removed rather than corrected: the non-goal now cites REQ-064
  criterion 6 for the rule and REQ-064's non-goals for the register's home,
  quoting neither.
