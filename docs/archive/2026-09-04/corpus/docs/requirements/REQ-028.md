# REQ-028 — How and where pages publish, chosen at setup with working defaults

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder at setup, I want to say whether pages go out on their own and where
they publish, so that the product can start today without me having to connect
anything first.

## Rationale
This is the only decision at setup that touches the founder's own web presence,
so it must be answerable in one click and must never make finishing setup wait
on DNS or a WordPress login: the founder says whether pages go out on their own
and where they land, defaults that work are already chosen for them, and a
destination they have not connected yet holds their pages rather than
redirecting them somewhere they did not choose (`BUILD.md` §4.3, §9). What the
two modes mean once pages exist — draft by default, the veto window, the
autopilot rate limits — belongs to the publishing requirements.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a founder at the mode card, when it renders, then autopilot is
   pre-selected, copilot is one click away, and each states in one written line
   what it means for them — pages publish after a review window they can stop,
   versus only when they approve.
2. Given a founder at the destination card, when it renders, then a blog on
   their own domain is pre-selected, and the DNS record they will need is shown
   to them once their site address is known; where they have not yet given one,
   one written line says the record appears once they have entered their site
   address, rather than a blank or a placeholder standing where it will sit
   (REQ-091 criterion 2, "no blank, dash or placeholder value stands anywhere a
   value would sit").
3. Given a founder who uses WordPress, when they choose it, then they can defer
   connecting it and setup still completes.
4. Given a founder who has set up neither DNS nor WordPress, when they submit
   setup, then setup completes and the product still produces their first page.
5. Given a founder whose chosen destination is not connected — the DNS record
   not pointed, or WordPress not linked — when their first page is ready, then
   it is held as a draft they can read, nothing is published to any other
   destination, and they are asked again for what is missing.

## Non-goals
- Connecting WordPress, or verifying the DNS record, during setup.
- Veto-window length, publish time, brand voice or do-not-claim at setup —
  those live in Settings.
- Any destination other than the hosted blog or WordPress.
- More than one destination chosen at setup.
