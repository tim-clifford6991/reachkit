# REQ-064 — Emails state only what was measured — a missing number omits its section, never prints 0

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer who mostly meets ReachKit in my inbox, I want its emails to
leave out what it could not measure rather than show me a zero, so that every
number I read is real and a bad week is never disguised as a good one, or the
reverse.

## Rationale
A missing measurement rendered as 0 is a false statement about the customer's
business and destroys trust in every other number in the same mail; the inbox is
the surface where a machine-written sentence would be least detectable, so the
line REQ-093 draws between ReachKit's own voice and delivered page content holds
in mail exactly as it does on screen; and a customer the product mails daily must
be able to quiet what recurs without losing the mail that carries their only key
(REQ-075). Placement: JN-005 step 1, "Opens Monday's email and reads what moved
this week". BUILD.md §12 fixes the omission rule and the shell: "`weekly` (score
delta, AI answers delta, pages verdicts, next 3 — all values conditional: a
missing number omits its section, never prints 0)" and "Emails (Resend, one
shell, plain-text alt, no LLM prose)". No implementation exists to touch: this
repo carries no `src/`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a section of an email depends on a value that could not be measured,
   when the mail is composed, then that section is left out entirely and no
   zero, dash or placeholder appears in its place — except that where REQ-065
   criterion 4 states the week was only partly measured, the mail names in one
   written line which of its sections could not be measured, rather than dropping
   them silently.
2. Given a value was measured as zero, when the mail is composed, then it is
   shown as zero, because a measured zero is a result.
3. Given a section of an email is a list that was measured and came out empty,
   when the mail is composed, then the section states that empty result in one
   written line; and where every conditional section of a mail is omitted or
   empty, the mail is still sent and states in one written line that there was
   nothing to report — unless the week was not measured at all (REQ-065
   criterion 3), when it states instead that the week could not be measured and
   when the next measurement is due, or the week was only partly measured
   (REQ-065 criterion 4), when criterion 1's line naming which sections could
   not be measured stands in its place. In neither of those cases does the mail
   say there was nothing to report.
4. Given any email the product sends, to a customer or to anyone else, when it is
   composed, then the line REQ-093 draws holds in the mail exactly as it does on
   screen and binds by the surface rather than by who is reading: every sentence
   ReachKit speaks in its own voice is a written string in the product, and
   model-produced text appears in a mail only on the conditions REQ-093 criteria
   2 and 3 admit, identified as they require and never as the product's own
   statement about the reader or their market.
5. Given any email the product sends, when it is delivered, then it carries a
   plain-text alternative.
6. Given any mail the product sends, when it is sent, then the occasion it was
   sent on is one a requirement in this corpus states, and that requirement
   states whether the customer may stop that mail; a mail on any other occasion
   is never sent, and the product sends no marketing, newsletter or promotional
   mail at all.
7. Given a kind of mail the customer may stop, when any send of that kind
   occurs, then they have a way to stop it, that way reaches this send too, and
   using it stops every send of that kind (REQ-075); and given a kind they may
   not stop, when they have switched off everything they can, then every send of
   that kind still reaches them. Whether a mail can be stopped is settled by its
   kind and is the same answer for every occasion of that kind: a send is never
   unstoppable on the ground that it repeats, or that what it repeats about is
   something outstanding on the customer's own account, and a kind is never
   stoppable for some of its sends and not others. The one send that departs from
   the kind it belongs to is the one REQ-057 criterion 7 makes unsuppressible,
   where that mail is the whole of the customer's telling that a page is going
   live and no interval in which to stop it exists.
8. Given a search volume sent outside the report — in the giveaway email or any
   follow-up (REQ-010) — when it is shown, then one written line states that
   measurement was on US Google in English, so no measured figure travels without
   that disclosure.

## Non-goals
- The register of kinds, which is bookkeeping rather than promise: which
  requirement names each kind's occasions and where each kind's stoppability is
  recorded are one table the mail blueprint holds and keeps in step with this
  corpus — a cross-artifact check no single requirement's prose can carry. It
  registers at least `magic-link`, `first-page`, `draft-ready`, `published`,
  `weekly`, `nurture` (bounded follow-up to a lead who has not subscribed),
  `setup-reminder` (bounded reminders to a paying customer whose setup is
  unfinished, stopping when they submit it) and `account` (any other mail sent
  because something on the customer's own account needs them). Each kind's
  stoppability is recorded there once for the kind, which is the grain criterion
  7 requires and never per send. Adding a kind is a change to that table; what
  the customer is owed is criterion 6's, not the table's.
- Marketing, newsletter or promotional sends — criterion 6 carries that promise
  as behaviour; this line only points at it.
- An emailed notification that a free scan's report is ready — ruled out by
  REQ-003's non-goal.
- Payment mail Stripe sends on its own account — receipts, failed-payment
  notices and card-expiry warnings — which is not ReachKit speaking.
- Dunning for refunds, chargebacks and failed renewals, deferred past v1 by
  owner ruling.
- Which mails a customer may switch off, where the toggles live and what the
  unsubscribe link reaches — REQ-075; the one send no setting suppresses is
  REQ-057 criterion 7's, and criterion 7 above carries it as behaviour rather
  than leaving it to this line.
- Customer-configurable email templates.
- Generated prose on a screen rather than in a mail — REQ-093.
- What each mail says, which belongs to the requirement it serves (REQ-010,
  REQ-024, REQ-025, REQ-057, REQ-060, REQ-062, REQ-063,
  REQ-074, REQ-076, REQ-077, REQ-079).
- An in-app notification centre, SMS or push.
