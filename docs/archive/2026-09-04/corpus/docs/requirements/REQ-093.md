# REQ-093 — The product speaks in written words; only the page it writes is generated

- Priority: Must

## User story
As anyone reading a ReachKit surface — a customer, or a stranger reading a free
report — I want everything the product tells me about a business to be words its
makers wrote, so that a model's paraphrase is never mistaken for a measurement
of that site — and so that when I do read generated text, I can tell it is a
page being written.

## Rationale
What must be true for the reader is one line, and every neighbour quotes it
rather than restating it in its own words: **every sentence ReachKit speaks in
its own voice is a written string in the product, and model-produced text
appears only as the content of a page ReachKit wrote or has proposed, or as the
wording of one of the twelve tracked questions, always shown with what
identifies it as that and never as the product's own statement about the reader
or their market.** The line binds by the surface the text appears on, never by
who is reading it or who it was written for: it reaches every surface the
product renders or sends — a screen and a mail alike — and every person it
reaches on one, a paying customer and equally a stranger reading a free report,
a lead reading a follow-up, a recipient who has no account and never will. This
requirement is the single home for that line — on screen and in mail alike
(REQ-064) — and it holds on surfaces added after it;
criteria 1, 2 and 3 state its three parts and are cited one at a time. The two
admissions beyond a written page are there because the reader meets
model-worded text outside one: a page ReachKit has proposed but not yet written,
shown as proposed, and the twelve tracked questions, which the search shown with
them identifies (REQ-006 criterion 9). Source:
`BUILD.md` §0.3 — "**No generated prose in the UI.** LLM output appears in
exactly one place: draft page content, always labelled. Every sentence in the
interface is a written string in the codebase. No LLM-written summaries,
narrations, or insights, anywhere" — and §6.7 step 4, "the LLM only words the
question". Criterion 2's reach to a page only proposed is §7's and §4.1 module
5's, not a widening of §0.3: an opportunity's target is a "proposed slug+title"
(§7) typed by Haiku (§6.3, "Haiku ×~4 for opportunity typing"), and §4.1 module 5
renders that title to a stranger as "title of page 1 of N" — the one surface on
which a reader meets a model's words for a page that does not exist yet (REQ-010
criterion 1). A proposed page has no description in the source, so criterion 2
admits none.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given any text the product renders or sends to a person — whether or not they
   are a customer: a verdict, an explanation, a label, a heading, an empty state,
   an alert, or anything else it says in its own voice — when they read it, then
   it is a written string in the product and no part of it was produced by a
   language model, unless criterion 2 or criterion 3 admits it. The kinds named
   here are illustrative, not a closed list: the criterion holds for every text
   the product renders or sends, on every surface it speaks on — a screen and a
   mail alike — including surfaces added after this requirement.
2. Given text a language model produced — the body, title, slug or description
   of a page ReachKit wrote, or the title or slug of one it has
   proposed and not yet written — when it appears on any surface the product
   renders or sends, a screen and a mail alike, to any reader whether or not they
   are a customer, whoever the page was written or proposed for and whether or
   not that person is a customer either, including before that page exists, then
   it appears as that page's content,
   identified as the page it belongs to and, where the page is not yet written,
   identified as proposed; and never as the product's own statement about the
   reader or their market.
3. Given the wording of one of the twelve tracked questions, which a language
   model produced, when it renders on any surface — the report and the app
   alike, and a set re-derived after a category change (REQ-071 criterion 7) as
   much as the first — then it is admitted on condition that the search it was
   derived from is shown with it, in the fuller form REQ-006 criterion 9 fixes
   wherever that requirement governs, and it is never presented as the product's
   own verdict about the reader or their market.
4. Given the product explains a number, verdict or reason, when the explanation
   renders, then every value inside it was read from a measurement the product
   stored for that domain, carrying the date of the scan it came from — never
   estimated, never recomputed for display, and never produced by a language
   model — and the words around those values do not change with who is reading.
5. Given the product's language models are unavailable, when anyone opens a
   ReachKit surface, then every word in the product's own voice renders
   unchanged, and page content already written still renders from what was
   stored.

## Non-goals
- Labelling the draft under review as generated content, which is REQ-045's.
- Which mails exist and what each says (REQ-064); criteria 1, 2 and 3 govern
  model-produced text in mail exactly as they do on screen, and neither the
  reader nor the person a page was written for narrows them.
- How the twelve questions are selected, what their provenance line carries, and
  when a set is re-derived (REQ-006, REQ-071).
- Translating or localising interface copy.
- Text the reader writes themselves — a brand voice description, an edited
  draft — which is theirs (REQ-045, REQ-055).
- Where a label sits and how generated content is styled or badged — design
  system.
