# REQ-021 — Where the offer lives, what a purchase carries into setup, and an account with nothing measured yet

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a founder deciding to buy — from the report I have just read, or from a price
surface with no report behind it — I want the purchase to carry in whatever has
already been measured for the site my account will use and to ask me plainly for
whatever has not, so that I never re-enter what I have given and I am never shown
a measurement of a domain nobody measured.

## Rationale
Buying is one behaviour with two entrances, and what the customer meets after
paying turns on one fact: whether the product holds a completed report for the
address the account will use. What must be true for them is that the offer reads
the same wherever it is met and never appears before the report it sits under is
finished; that a purchase made from a report provisions the domain that report
measured, shown to confirm or change rather than retyped; that a purchase with no
report behind it asks for the site address at setup rather than guessing it from
payment details, and refuses an address it cannot reach before the money is
spent rather than after; and that nothing is ever presented as measured for a
domain nobody has measured — including the domain a buyer replaced. Whether a
purchase has a report behind it is a fact about the purchase, never about what
the buyer has run before, so a founder who ran a report and then bought from a
price surface is asked for their site address like any other. An address accepted
at setup is not a life sentence: Settings changes the site's domain afterwards
(REQ-070 criterion 1), and what that change re-measures, and when, is REQ-071's
(criterion 9). A founder refused at criterion 9 is never trapped: Settings,
cancellation and export stay open with setup unfinished (REQ-025 criterion 5;
REQ-076 criterion 3). Source: `BUILD.md` §4.1 module 6 — "**Pricing card**:
€49/mo + four spec rows (1/day · weekly · weekly · 24h veto) + Start button +
"Cancel in one click."" — §13, "Stripe Checkout from the report (scan id in
metadata)", and §4.3. Placement: JN-002 steps 1, 4 and 5.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a completed report, when the visitor reaches the end of it, then it
   presents one offer to subscribe with a single control that begins checkout.
2. Given the offer, when it renders, then it states the monthly price on the
   terms REQ-022 criterion 1 fixes, how often a page is written, how often
   measurement is repeated, how often the customer is told what moved, that a
   page can be
   stopped before it publishes, and that the subscription can be cancelled by
   the customer themselves.
3. Given a scan that has not finished, when the visitor watches it run, then no
   offer to subscribe and no control that begins checkout is presented to them;
   the offer arrives with the report and never before it.
4. Given a surface that offers ReachKit away from any report, when a founder
   reaches it, then it carries exactly one offer to subscribe, states the price
   on the terms REQ-022 criterion 1 fixes and what the subscription does on the
   same terms the offer at the end of a report states (criterion 2), and
   one control on it begins checkout with no account, sign-in, password or form
   asked first (REQ-020 criterion 1).
5. Given a founder on a price surface with no report behind it, when they buy,
   then the purchase completes on the same terms as one made from a report.
6. Given a founder who begins checkout from a report, when their payment
   completes, then the account is provisioned for the domain that report
   measured, and setup shows them that domain to confirm or change rather than
   asking them to type it; when they change it, the account's site becomes the
   address they give, on the same terms as an address given with no report
   behind the purchase (criteria 8 and 9).
7. Given a purchase made with no report behind it, when the founder reaches
   setup, then setup asks for their site address before its other decisions and
   does not proceed without one, and the field is empty: no domain taken from
   the address they paid with, or from their payment details, is pre-filled or
   offered to them as their site.
8. Given the founder has given their site address, when they submit setup, then
   the deep pass runs against that domain and the account behaves from then on
   exactly like one bought from a report.
9. Given a founder entering their site address at setup, when the address is
   not one the product can reach — reaching being that the address is a domain
   name that resolves in DNS, the same test a rival domain meets (REQ-026
   criterion 8), so a site that is new, thin, unbuilt, coming-soon or blocking
   our fetcher is reached and accepted — then setup says so in one written line
   and does not accept it until an address it can reach is given.
10. Given a founder who has paid and whose own site address setup refuses
    (criterion 9), when they are refused, then the same written line names one
    way to reach a person (REQ-024 criterion 5) and tells them they can cancel
    without finishing setup; the address they typed and every answer they have
    already given are still there when they return; and nothing about their
    account requires setup to be finished before they can reach Settings, cancel
    or export their content (REQ-025 criterion 5; REQ-070 criterion 2; REQ-076
    criterion 3) — so
    a founder this refusal stops can always either get help or leave.
11. Given a purchase made with no report behind it, when the founder is at setup
    or in the app before their site has been measured, then no score, report or
    question result is shown for their domain and nothing on screen is presented
    as a measurement of it; what is offered from the market they stated —
    suggested rivals among it — is not a measurement of their site and remains
    available (REQ-026 criterion 7).
12. Given a buyer whose account's domain is not the one the report behind their
    purchase measured, because they replaced it, when they reach any setup or app
    screen before the deep pass for their own domain has completed, then nothing
    that report measured for the replaced domain — its score, its questions, its
    market or its rivals — is presented as their account's, and the account
    stands as one with nothing measured yet.

## Non-goals
- Guessing the domain from the buyer's email address or payment details —
  criterion 7 carries the customer's promise that nothing is pre-filled.
- Asking for the domain during checkout — REQ-020 criterion 6 fixes what
  checkout may ask.
- Any account, form or configuration between the control and payment (REQ-020
  criterion 1).
- Running a free report on the buyer's behalf before setup; criterion 11 carries
  what the founder must not be shown until their own site is measured.
- More than one site per account.
- A second plan, tier, trial or discount on any surface that offers ReachKit
  (REQ-022 criterion 3); criteria 1 and 4 fix that each carries one offer.
- Changing the site's domain after setup, and the Settings surface that does it
  — REQ-070's and REQ-071's, cited in the rationale, not restated here.
- What else setup asks and the shape of that screen (REQ-025); criteria 6 and 12
  add to it only the domain the report already measured and what happens to that
  report's measurements when the buyer replaces it.
- Whether the replaced domain's report survives at its own public address — it
  does (REQ-001 criterion 11); criterion 12 governs only what the buyer's account
  presents.
- What the setup market card shows for an account with nothing measured yet —
  REQ-026 criterion 3's.
- The billing screen, how a cancellation is carried out, and refunds (REQ-076).
- A person setting a site address as the ordinary way anyone reaches setup:
  criterion 10 is the way out for the founder criterion 9 refuses, not a route
  offered to buyers whose address is accepted.
- How quickly the person criterion 10 names settles the address, and whether that
  route ends in a finished setup at all: settling it is manual work outside the
  product, on which no criterion here can be failed. What the founder is owed
  while it is open is criterion 10's, and leaving without waiting for it is
  REQ-076 criterion 3's.
- Anything else the surface criterion 4 requires may carry, and how the terms it
  states are laid out — the blueprint that owns that surface decides both;
  criterion 4 asks only that the buyer there is told what a buyer at the end of
  a report is told.
