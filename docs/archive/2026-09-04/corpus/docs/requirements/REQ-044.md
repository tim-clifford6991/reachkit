# REQ-044 — Selecting a day says why that page exists and what can be done about it now

- Priority: Must

## User story
As a paying customer with no SEO knowledge, I want selecting a day to tell me
why that page is on my calendar and offer only the actions that make sense for
it right now, so that I can decide in seconds without learning a discipline.

## Rationale
A customer who cannot say why a page is being written for them cannot judge it,
a page presented without its evidence reads as guesswork, and an action offered
that cannot succeed teaches them not to trust the panel. Source: `BUILD.md` §4.6
(day panel: "Why this page" — search / asked / answered-today-by / you /
done-when — plus stage-appropriate actions, one provenance line, and today
selected on open).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given the customer opens the Calendar, when the day detail first renders, then
   today is the selected day.
2. Given a selected day holding a page, when the detail renders, then it states
   the search the page targets, who currently answers that search, where the
   customer stands on it, how winnable that search is judged to be, and what
   would count as the page having worked.
3. Given a selected day, when the detail renders, then every action it offers can
   be carried out in that page's current stage, and no action is offered that
   would be refused because of that stage.
4. Given any measured value shown in the detail, when it renders, then it carries
   the date of the measurement it came from.
5. Given a selected day holding no page, when the detail renders, then it states
   which kind of empty day it is, carrying the one account REQ-043 criterion 5
   gives that date and no other, whatever emptied it, and it offers no action that
   would publish or approve a page.

## Non-goals
- Editing the target search, slug or title of a planned page from this view.
- Comments, assignment or approval chains.
- Panel width, stickiness, badge tone and typography — design system.
- Which kinds of empty day exist and which line each carries — REQ-043 owns the
  date's one account; criterion 5 fixes only that the panel repeats it and offers
  no action on it.
- Presenting the detail as a drawer or modal is a design decision, not a
  requirement.
