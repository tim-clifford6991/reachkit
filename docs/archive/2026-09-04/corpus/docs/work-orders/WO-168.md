# WO-168 — The day panel: today on open, why this page exists, and only the actions its stage can carry

## Goal
Render the selected day's panel — today when the calendar opens — stating why the
page exists with every measured value carrying its date, or the date's one empty
account, offering only the actions the projection returns, and rendering both of
criterion 12's ways through — each offered, or refused in its place — from the
record alone.

**Conflict (rule 2.3): REQ-043 criterion 10 rewritten 2026-09-03 (commit 6a6e2bb)** — criterion 10 changed from "every measured value shown in the detail carries the date of the measurement it came from" to "one written line states the date they were measured, and that date is not repeated separately beside each value" — the shared-provenance-line reading, not per-value dates. The goal's specification of rendering "every measured value carrying its date" (line 47: "each measured value rendered through `renderMeasured` with its measurement date") contradicts the new criterion 10 requirement. The five "Why this page" evidence facts are the measured values criterion 10 addresses, and REQ-096 criterion 4's rival band carries a date as well — six values in total that criterion 10 now forbids rendering with individual date copies. Evidence: `requirements/history/REQ-043-2026-09-03-criterion-10-disambiguated.md`.

## Steps
1. **Precondition (rule 7.3):** signed preview, and it now has more to carry.
   The preview must show, for one delivered page: the stage, the address under
   each of its two labels, the recorded check outcome with its date, both ways
   through offered, one way refused with its line in the link's place, and a
   second way absent altogether. `components.md` registers `DayPanel` with a
   "required heading node, required account node, actions slot" and **no slot
   named for the ways through** — whether they sit in the actions slot, in the
   record block, or in a slot of their own is the design-guardian's to rule on
   the preview, not this work order's to assume. As of 2026-08-31
   `sdlc-factory/docs/design/tokens.md` is `Status: ready` and `components.md`
   registers 24 components including `DayPanel` (custom surface, required
   heading node, required account node, actions slot, **no default action**) —
   every row `proposed`, reaching `approved` only when a preview carrying it is
   signed off. The preview, not the registration, is the gate.
2. Write the failing tests first (§8, "Watch it fail first").
3. Select today on open, where "today" is the site-local day from
   `sites.time_zone` — a customer in UTC+13 opening at 01:00 local sees their
   own today, not the server's yesterday.
4. Render every measured value through `renderMeasured` so an unmeasured value
   shows the dash and its written line rather than a zero, and render the
   measurement date beside it.
5. Render the winnability band through `BAND_LABELS.winnability`, keyed
   `band.winnability.winnable`, `band.winnability.reach`,
   `band.winnability.notYet` — never a literal word. The three keys are indexed
   by BP-040's hyphenated handles (`'not-yet'` → `notYet`); the rendered third
   term is `Not yet` without the hyphen (BP-019 decision 6). The handle and the
   word are two different spellings of one band and both are correct where they
   are.
6. Render only the actions `actionsFor` returns; add no action of this
   component's own, and render no publishing or approving control on an empty
   day. Unpublish is one of those projected actions and needs no special case —
   it appears for as long as `published`→`unpublished` is open (criterion 12's
   "for as long as it is published") and stops appearing when the edge closes.
6a. Render the two ways through by **destructuring** `WayThrough`, never by
   testing `href` for emptiness. A refusal renders `copy(because)` where the
   link would have been; an absent second way renders nothing. The three
   refusal grounds are `unpublished_by_us`, `page_not_found` and
   `no_admin_address`, and **there is no fourth** — a `could_not_confirm` is
   not a refusal ground and its way is offered, with the outcome and its date
   rendered beside the page's state (ADR-085 Decision 4). The two render as the
   same grey line and mean opposite things; that is the mistake this component
   is most likely to make.
6b. Render nothing that asserts the page is live now. The offered arm's line is
   "this is where the page was put"; the address's own label comes from
   `RecordedAddress.label` and is one of two owner-owed keys, chosen by the
   page's current state and never by this component (REQ-056 criterion 6).
7. Mutation check, four ways: hard-code an Approve control into the panel and
   confirm the empty-day test fails; render a band word literally and confirm the
   `BAND_LABELS` test fails; render a refused way as an omission rather than a
   line and confirm the in-place case fails — that is the mutation that turns
   criterion 12's "says so in place of that way" into silence; and render
   `insideTheirWordPress === null` as a refusal line and confirm the absent-way
   case fails, because telling a hosted customer that a way into their WordPress
   leads nowhere is a sentence about a thing they do not have.

**Discriminates (mutation check, constitution §8).**
- **Rendering a refused way as an omission** — dropping the link and saying
  nothing — passes every "no broken link" instinct and fails the in-place case.
  Criterion 12 requires the detail to say so *in place of* the way; silence
  leaves the customer to guess why a way they saw last week is gone.
- **Rendering an absent second way as a refusal** fails the absent-way case. A
  hosted customer would be told a way into a WordPress they do not have leads
  nowhere.
- **Treating `could_not_confirm` as a refusal ground** renders identically to
  the correct behaviour for `page_not_found` and fails the still-offered case.
  It is the merge ADR-085 exists to stop, arriving at the surface where the two
  grey lines actually sit side by side.
- Hard-coding an Approve control fails the empty-day case; rendering a band word
  literally fails the `BAND_LABELS` case.

## Out of scope
- The day read and the action projection themselves (WO-166), the grid
  (WO-167), the empty-date resolver (WO-165).
- **Deciding whether a way through is offered, and forming either address** —
  WO-258 and WO-259. This component destructures a `WayThrough` and renders it;
  it computes no ground and builds no URL.
- **Fetching either address, now or ever**, and any claim that the page is
  still there. The panel speaks only from `PageRecord`; the only look at a
  published page there has ever been is the one check at 24 hours.
- The draft view behind "Read the full page" (WO-169, WO-170, WO-171, WO-172, WO-173).
- Presenting the panel as a drawer or a modal, panel widths, stickiness and
  badge tone — design system (REQ-043's non-goals).
- Any customer-visible string, including the three band words; keys only.
