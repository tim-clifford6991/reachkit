# WO-166 — The day read: why this page exists, its provenance, the two ways through, and actions projected from the transitions

- Branch: wo/WO-166-calendar-day
- Estimate: ≤1 day

## Blocked-by — REQ-043 criterion 12 against criteria 4 and 11, and BP-039 decision 1
> Raised 2026-09-02 by the planner (rule 2.3: a conflict is an edge and a state,
> not a file). **This is not a request for a decision about wording; it is two
> criteria of one requirement that cannot both hold for the same date, and the
> plan for `readDay` cannot be written until one of them gives.**

REQ-043 criterion 12 opens on **"a page ReachKit has delivered to a
destination"** and requires that its day's detail let the customer *"read which
stage that page is in (criterion 2)"* and, where ReachKit's own record says a
way through leads nowhere **because *"it unpublished the page itself (REQ-056
criterion 7)"***, that the detail *"says so in place of that way"*.

A page ReachKit unpublished is in REQ-056 criterion 1's `unpublished` state —
the only edge into it is `published`→`unpublished` (criterion 2), so every such
page was delivered and was live. Three things then hold at once and cannot:

1. **BP-039 decision 1** maps `unpublished` to `null` in `STAGE_OF`, stated in
   the blueprint's own words: *"`skipped` and `unpublished` occupy no date,
   because REQ-043 criterion 4 names 'a page that can no longer go live' as a
   cause that empties a date."* So its date carries **no page**.
2. **REQ-043 criterion 11** then governs that date: *"Given a selected day
   holding no page … it states which kind of empty day it is, carrying the one
   account criterion 5 gives that date and no other."* An account, not a page.
3. **REQ-043 criterion 12** requires that same date's detail to carry the page,
   its stage, its record and a refusal in place of a way through.

`DayDetail.page.stage` is a `Stage`, and `Stage` has no arm for a page that
occupies no date, so an unpublished page's detail is **unrepresentable** in
BP-039's own interface. `WayThrough`'s `because: 'unpublished_by_us'` arm is
therefore unreachable by construction — the one arm of the three that this
blueprint added on 2026-09-01 for the case the requirement names first.

**What would clear it** — the architect's, not this planner's (REQ-043's own
non-goal hands "which publish state is shown as which stage" to the blueprint,
and constitution §4 puts type members and boundaries with the architect):
either `unpublished` gains a stage and stops emptying its date, or `DayDetail`
carries a delivered page's record on its date independently of `STAGE_OF` and
criterion 11's account, or criterion 12's `unpublished_by_us` ground is reached
from a surface that is not the day panel. Each is a different promise to the
customer and each changes a different file below.

**What is not blocked by it.** WO-258's `wayAsVisitor` is a pure function over a
`PageRecord` and decides its two reachable refusal grounds correctly whatever
page the panel ends up carrying; it is not held. WO-259 is held for a different
reason of its own. Criteria 8, 9, 10 and the empty arm of
this work order are unaffected and are re-stated below so that this work order
is executable the day the conflict is ruled, without a second re-extract.

## Goal
Read a selected day into its page's evidence — search, question, who answers it,
where the customer stands, winnability, what would count as working — with every
measured value carrying its date, carry BP-045's whole page record and both ways
through beside it without making a single request, and project the offered
actions from BP-015's transition table.

## Steps
1. Write the failing tests first (§8).
2. Read the day's page and its opportunity in one row-plus-join; carry
   `doneWhen` **verbatim as recorded**, never re-derived. `why.winnability`
   carries BP-040's **handle** — `'winnable' | 'reach' | 'not-yet'`, hyphenated,
   as REQ-047 c10 spells it — and never a word. The ruled rendered term is
   `Not yet`, unhyphenated, and it lives in BP-019's registry: do not
   "fix" either spelling to match the other; each is correct where it is
   (BP-019 decision 6, BP-040's discharged `rests-on`).
3. Attach `provenance.measuredAt` and return every measured value as
   `Measured<T>` so the panel can render its date.
3a. For a delivered page, call `pageRecordFor(draftId)` once and put the result
   on `record` **unchanged**. Do not project fields out of it, do not spread it
   into the page object, and do not make `verification` optional on the way
   past: the whole promise of criterion 7 is that a surface cannot get the state
   without the outcome, and every one of those three is a way to hand a future
   surface the state alone.
3b. Compute the two ways through with WO-258's `wayAsVisitor(record)` and
   WO-259's `wayInsideTheirWordPress(record)`, and return them on `asVisitor`
   and `insideTheirWordPress`. `readDay` decides nothing about either itself —
   one function per way, one place each, so the panel and any later surface
   cannot disagree about whether a way is offered. `insideTheirWordPress` is
   `null` at a destination ReachKit serves, and **an absent second way is not a
   refused one**: the panel renders them differently (WO-168).
   **`could_not_confirm` is not a refusal ground**: the record says nothing
   against the page, so the way is offered and the detail claims only that this
   is where the page was put, never that it is there now (ADR-085 Decision 4).
4. Implement `actionsFor` as an intersection with `TRANSITIONS` (BP-039
   decision 2) — never a hand-written per-stage list, which is a second copy of
   the state machine.
5. For a day with no page, return `empty` from `accountFor` and an empty action
   projection.
6. Unpublish needs no special case: `published`→`unpublished` is an open edge
   for as long as the page is published, so it appears through `actionsFor` by
   the same mechanism every other action does and stops appearing when the edge
   closes (criterion 12's "for as long as it is published"). Assert it; add no
   second path for it.
7. Mutation check, three ways: hand-list one stage's actions and confirm the
   projection test fails when a transition edge is removed; add a `HEAD` request
   before returning `asVisitor` and confirm `no-fetch.test.ts` fails; and treat
   `could_not_confirm` as a refusal ground and confirm the day test's
   still-offered case fails — that third one is the merge ADR-085 exists to
   stop, and it renders identically to the correct behaviour.

**Discriminates (mutation check, constitution §8).**
- **Adding a `HEAD` or `GET` before returning `asVisitor`** fails
  `no-fetch.test.ts` and nothing else. It reads as making the link trustworthy;
  it is the second look at a published page that REQ-062's non-goal forbids, and
  its failure mode is a network blip rendered to the customer as a page that is
  gone.
- **Treating `could_not_confirm` as a refusal ground** fails the still-offered
  case and nothing else. It renders as the same grey line as `page_not_found`
  and takes a page the record says nothing against and tells the customer the
  way leads nowhere (ADR-085 Decision 4).
- **Projecting fields out of `PageRecord` instead of carrying it whole** — the
  tidier-looking `record: { state, verification }` — passes every runtime test
  and fails the whole-record assertion. It is the change that lets the next
  surface take the state without the outcome, which is the promise criterion 7
  makes about surfaces that do not exist yet.
- Hand-listing a stage's actions fails the projection test the moment an edge is
  removed; a second copy of the state machine is what criterion 9 exists to
  prevent.
