# WO-263 — The page record — the address under the label its current state earns, and the one check's outcome inseparable from it

- Branch: wo/WO-263-page-record
- Estimate: ≤1 day

## Goal
Ship `src/lib/publish/record/`: the one read behind every surface that states a published page's standing, returning the page's state, the address under the label its **current state** earns, the unpublish outcome and the one check's recorded outcome with its date — as a single value with no optional fields, so a surface cannot obtain the state without them.

```ts
// src/lib/publish/record/index.ts — REQ-056 c6 and c16, REQ-062 c7. Read-only.
/** How an address may be spoken of, decided by the page's **current state**, not
 *  by what ReachKit once did to it (decision 8). Two keys, not one string and
 *  not a boolean: the pair is a customer-visible distinction and both sentences
 *  are the owner's (REQ-093). This node names the keys and supplies the address;
 *  it mints neither sentence. */
export type AddressLabel =
  | 'record.address.publiclyReadableAt'   // while the page stands `published`
  | 'record.address.wasPublishedAt'       // from the moment it moves to `unpublished`

export type RecordedAddress =
  | { offered: true;  label: AddressLabel; url: string }
  /** REQ-056 c6's last sentence: "Where ReachKit never made the page live at its
   *  destination, no address is offered at all and the record says so in place
   *  of one." Disjoint from the unpublished case by construction — it is decided
   *  from `made_live_by_us`, and the `published`/`unpublished` split above is
   *  decided from the state. */
  | { offered: false; because: 'never_made_live'; copy: 'record.address.neverMadeLive' }

export interface PageRecord {
  draftId: string
  state: State
  opportunityId: string
  targetQuery: string
  measuredAt: Date                 // the measurement behind the page (c6)
  mode: 'approved' | 'autopilot'   // c6's "approved or published automatically"
  address: RecordedAddress
  /** REQ-056 c6 and c16: the outcome c15 or c16 named for this page, and **no
   *  other account of what is at that address**. Non-null exactly when
   *  `state === 'unpublished'`. */
  unpublishOutcome: UnpublishResult['outcome'] | null
  /** REQ-062 c7. BP-049's whole `VerifyOutcome`, `checkedAt` included, or the
   *  disposition where no check has run. **Not optional, and not separable from
   *  `state`** — a surface cannot obtain the state without it, which is how
   *  criterion 7's "no surface presents the page as live without also carrying
   *  what ReachKit saw when it looked" holds on surfaces that do not exist yet
   *  (ADR-085 Decision 5). */
  verification: VerifyDisposition
}

/** The one read behind every surface that states a published page's state:
 *  the day panel (BP-039), the draft view (BP-044) and Overview (BP-001) all
 *  call this and none of them re-derives liveness from a column. */
export function pageRecordFor(draftId: string): Promise<PageRecord | null>
```

**The label is decided by the page's current state, and that is a bug fix, not a
refinement** (BP-045 decision 8). REQ-056 criterion 6 used to split on whether
ReachKit *made* the page live — a fact about the past — and hung its "never an
address that does not resolve to that page" guard on that split. A page ReachKit
published and then took down stayed in the first arm and was shown "the address
it is publicly readable at" **for a page that is not**. The two arms were not
disjoint. The criterion's 2026-09-01 fold moves the split onto the page's
**current state**, and the fixture that moves one page through the
`published → unpublished` edge is what holds it: an implementation splitting on
`made_live_by_us` passes every static fixture and fails that one.

**The guard has changed meaning with it, and the new meaning is the honest one.**
It is no longer a promise to verify that the address resolves — **ReachKit cannot
verify it and never looks again** — but a ban on claiming liveness at an address
ReachKit itself stopped serving or asked the customer's site to stop serving.

**Three keys, and the middle one is not a tense variable.** BP-045 decision 8
rejects "one copy key with the tense interpolated": it makes a customer-visible
distinction a variable substitution and puts half a sentence in this node. It
also rejects a boolean `isLive` on the existing publication read — that is the
liveness assertion REQ-062 criterion 7 and REQ-060 criterion 2 both forbid, made
from a column rather than from an observation.

**`verification` is not optional, and that is the whole mechanism — ADR-085
Decision 5.** REQ-062 criterion 7 is a law over surfaces, not a field: wherever
any surface states a page's state, it carries the recorded outcome and its date
alongside. It is enforced here by there being **one place to read a page's
observed state**, returning the outcome and the state **inside the same value**
with neither field optional. **A surface cannot obtain the state without the
outcome, because they are one object.** That is the same mechanism BP-019's
`Measured<T>` uses for REQ-004 and BP-054's `WorkStop` uses for REQ-092: the
promise holds on surfaces that do not exist yet, because the type will not let
them render without it. A rule each surface follows is a rule two of them will
stop following — and there are three (BP-039, BP-044, BP-001).

**`page_not_found` and `could_not_confirm` reach this record as two values and
must leave it as two — ADR-085 (landmine).** They render as the same grey line
and have opposite consequences: `page_not_found` stops the page being shown as
live (REQ-060 criterion 2), `could_not_confirm` leaves the page's publish state
**unchanged — still shown as it was** (ADR-085 Decision 4's table, middle rows).
This module is one of the four places that table is read, and the merge it
forbids will be proposed by whoever is looking at two identical grey lines on a
day panel. The paired fixture in the file plan is what fails when they do; the
type-level half is WO-206's.

## Steps
1. Read **ADR-085** Decisions 4 and 5, then BP-045 decisions 8 and 9. This module is where two landmine files meet.
2. Create `record/index.ts` with the block above verbatim. **Read-only**: no write, no transition, no fetch, no repair.
3. Decide the address label from `state` — `published` → `publiclyReadableAt`, `unpublished` → `wasPublishedAt`. **Do not read `made_live_by_us` for the label.**
4. Decide the `offered: false` arm from `made_live_by_us` alone, and return an arm that carries **no `url` field at all** — not an empty string and not a null. A surface must be unable to render an address it was not given.
5. Populate `verification` from `dispositionFor` and pass the **whole** `VerifyOutcome` through, `checkedAt` included. Project nothing, flatten nothing, and add no boolean derived from it.
6. Make every field of `PageRecord` required. Add no optional field and no second read path that returns a state without a verification.
7. Write the moved-fixture label test first — one page carried through `published → unpublished` — and watch it fail against an implementation splitting on `made_live_by_us` (constitution §8).
8. Write the `@ts-expect-error` optionality fixtures and the destructuring proof before the implementation, and confirm they fail while the type is incomplete.
9. Mutate, and confirm each fails: make `verification` optional (the optionality fixtures compile and fail); collapse `page_not_found` and `could_not_confirm` into one shape (the paired fixture fails); decide the label from `made_live_by_us` (the moved fixture fails); return `url: ''` on the never-made-live arm (the no-address assertion fails).

## Out of scope
- **Every surface.** The day panel (BP-039), the draft view (BP-044) and Overview (BP-001) call this function; making them call it is their work orders' and not this pass's — the first `rests-on` records the gap honestly rather than claiming the criterion is discharged corpus-wide.
- Writing anything. This module is read-only: no transition, no column write, no repair. `unpublish_outcome` is written by WO-214, `made_live_by_us` and `live_url` by WO-213, `verify` by WO-234.
- Running or scheduling a check — WO-232, WO-234. **And looking again at a published page** — forbidden, not merely out of scope: ReachKit's one look is the 24-hour check and REQ-062's non-goal closes every other.
- Classifying an answer, or deciding a site's condition — WO-261, WO-262.
- **Every customer-visible string.** Three keys are named here — `record.address.publiclyReadableAt`, `record.address.wasPublishedAt` and `record.address.neverMadeLive` — and **all three sentences are owner-owed** (second `rests-on`, REQ-093, constitution §1's rights table). Nothing here mints one, and the pair in particular is a customer-visible distinction the owner decides.
- Any resumption path for a page marked no longer judgeable, and any weekly per-page fetch. **REQ-063 criterion 7 was withdrawn outright on 2026-09-01 and its number deliberately not reused**; anything derived from it is dropped, not implemented (BP-051's status-grounds addendum).
