# WO-050 — The stopped-state sweep: every route rendered twice, and no internal cause anywhere in it

- Branch: wo/WO-050-stopped-sweep
- Estimate: ≤1 day

## Goal
Build ADR-010 point 3: render every enumerated route twice — stop present and
stop absent — and assert that every next-publish sentence came from
`nextPublishStatement`, that under a stop it names the stop and no other cause,
and that no rendered text matches the internal-cause vocabulary.

## File plan
| File | New/Modify | Change |
|---|---|---|
| `tests/presentation/stopped/vocabulary.ts` | **NEW** | The internal-cause vocabulary REQ-092 criterion 8 forbids, as a declared, reviewable list with one comment per entry: cap, spend, a currency amount, an error text marker, a system status detail, a stack frame. Nothing is matched that is not on this list, and nothing on this list is matched loosely. |
| `tests/presentation/stopped/budget.ts` | **NEW** | `export const STOPPED_SWEEP_BUDGET_MS = 60_000` — BP-054's NFR parameter, declared here and nowhere else. A comment records that exceeding it is fixed by sharding on the surface glob, never by raising the number. |
| `tests/presentation/stopped/sweep.test.ts` | **NEW** | Renders every enumerated route twice and asserts criteria 3, 7 and 8 at the surface, plus the alert-count invariant and the prepared-pages assertion for criterion 5. Emits the coverage statement and asserts the wall clock against the budget. |
| `tests/presentation/stopped/__fixtures__/routes/**` | **NEW** | The synthetic route tree: clean routes that must pass, and one violating route per rule that must be flagged by name — a next-publish sentence written at the call site instead of through `nextPublishStatement`, a route naming the paused cause alongside a stop, a route printing a spend amount, a route printing an error string, a route that keeps the stop statement rendered when `stop` is `null`, and a route that counts the stop statement against REQ-041 criterion 5's two alerts. |

## Interfaces
**Consumes** (test-time only — ADR-010 `## Consequences`: reading another node's
tree "creates no import and no cycle; no `depends-on` edge is drawn for it"):
- `enumerateRoutes()` from WO-047. This WO writes no second route enumerator
  (rule 7.2); ADR-010 point 3 says this sweep renders "the same enumerated
  routes".
- `surfaceTree()` from WO-044, for the mail templates under `src/lib/mail/**`,
  which are surfaces criterion 7 reaches ("a mail's statement of what publishes
  next") and which are not routes.
- `COPY_META`, `COPY`, `CopyKey` from WO-041 — the sweep derives its own scope
  from the `law: 'next-publish'` and `law: 'stopped-work'` tags, not from a list
  (ADR-010 point 4).
- `WorkStop`, `stoppedWorkStatement`, `nextPublishStatement`, `dayAccount` from
  WO-049.
- BP-015's publish state machine, read at test time for the criterion 5
  assertion.

**Exposes:** nothing. This is a leaf suite.

## Steps
1. Create `vocabulary.ts` and `budget.ts`.
2. Create the fixture route tree under
   `tests/presentation/stopped/__fixtures__/routes/` with the six violating
   routes listed in the file plan.
3. Write `sweep.test.ts`. For each enumerated route and each mail template,
   render twice: once with a non-null `WorkStop` and once with `null`. Two
   describes throughout: one over the fixture tree (must flag exactly the known
   violations, by file) and one over the real tree (must flag nothing).
4. Derive the next-publish scope from `COPY_META`: every key tagged
   `law: 'next-publish'`. Assert every rendered occurrence of one of those
   sentences was produced by a `nextPublishStatement` call — traced by stubbing
   that function and comparing the set of sentences it returned against the set
   of tagged sentences found in the rendered output. A tagged sentence present in
   the output that the stub never returned is a call-site line and fails by
   route and key.
5. Assert that under a stop, the rendered output contains
   `copy('next-publish.stopped')` and none of the other four `next-publish` keys.
6. Assert that no rendered text under a stop matches any entry in
   `vocabulary.ts`.
7. Assert the stop statement is absent from every route rendered with a `null`
   `WorkStop` — criterion 3's "it stops stating it once the work resumes". There
   is no dismissal flag, no cached banner and no "seen" state to clear; a route
   that renders the statement with a null stop fails by route.
8. Assert the app-landing route states the stop with a non-null `WorkStop`, and
   that REQ-041 criterion 5's alert count on that route is **unchanged** between
   the two renders — the stop statement is not one of the at-most-two alerts
   (BP-054 `## Error & edge behavior`, sixth bullet).
9. Assert criterion 5 against BP-015's transition table: applying a stop produces
   no state transition on any prepared page, and no page is dropped or marked
   published. This suite asserts; BP-015 implements.
10. Emit the coverage statement: routes enumerated, mail templates swept, routes
    rendered twice, `next-publish` keys in scope, vocabulary entries checked,
    violations found. Assert both describes' wall clock against
    `STOPPED_SWEEP_BUDGET_MS`.

## Test plan
| Acceptance criterion (verbatim from REQ-092) | Test |
|---|---|
| 3. "Given ReachKit has stopped its own work and today's page has not been produced, when the customer opens the app, then the screen they land on states it, without their having to open a calendar day or a settings screen, and it stops stating it once the work resumes." | `sweep.test.ts` → **"the app-landing screen states the stop"** and **"the statement disappears when the stop clears"**: with a non-null `WorkStop` the landing route's output contains `stoppedWorkStatement`'s three lines; with `null` no route's output contains any of them. Discriminates: the fixture's route that caches the banner must be flagged; a dismissal flag or cached snapshot leaves the statement present with a null stop and fails by route. |
| 3. (same, verbatim as above) | `sweep.test.ts` → **"the stop is not one of Overview's two alerts"**: the alert count on the landing route is byte-identical between the stopped and unstopped renders. Discriminates: the fixture route that counts the statement as an alert must be flagged. |
| 5. "Given ReachKit stopped its own work while pages were already prepared for the customer, when the work resumes, then every prepared page is still there and publishes on the normal schedule (REQ-056 criterion 8), and none is dropped or marked published." | `sweep.test.ts` → **"a stop writes no transition"**: with prepared pages in every stage BP-015's table admits, applying and clearing a stop leaves every page's state, count and scheduled date unchanged, and none is marked published. Discriminates: any transition written on the stop path changes a state and fails by page id. This suite asserts a property of BP-015's table and implements none of it. |
| 7. "Given ReachKit has stopped its own work, when any statement of when the next page publishes renders — the navigation's among them, its line being REQ-040 criterion 4's — then it names ReachKit's stop as the reason no publish is scheduled, and gives no other reason for it, whether or not that other reason is also true of the account at that moment." | `sweep.test.ts` → **"every next-publish sentence came from nextPublishStatement"** (scope derived from `COPY_META`'s `law: 'next-publish'` tag, not a list) and **"under a stop only next-publish.stopped renders"**. Discriminates: the fixture's call-site sentence and its paused-alongside-a-stop route must each be flagged by file; deleting either rule leaves its violation unflagged and the fixture describe fails. |
| 8. "Given the customer is told ReachKit did not do the work, when they read that statement wherever it renders, then it names no internal cause: no cap or spend amount, no error text and no system status detail appears anywhere in it." | `sweep.test.ts` → **"no rendered text under a stop matches the internal-cause vocabulary"**: every route and every mail template rendered under a stop is matched against `vocabulary.ts`. Discriminates: the fixture's spend-amount route and its error-text route must each be flagged by file and match. |

Additional test this WO owns:
- `sweep.test.ts` → **"the sweep states its own coverage"**: the emitted report
  names routes enumerated, mail templates swept, `next-publish` keys in scope and
  vocabulary entries checked; a run over an empty root reports `0` for each
  rather than emitting nothing (rule 5.5). This is the state on the day this WO
  lands, and the report is what makes it visible.
