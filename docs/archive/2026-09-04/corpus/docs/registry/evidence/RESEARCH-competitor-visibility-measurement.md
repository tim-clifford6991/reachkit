# Evidence — how competing products measure and act on search and AI-answer visibility, and what maps to ReachKit's closed dataset list

Sources: the public documentation, methodology pages and changelogs of
Semrush, Ahrefs, Profound, Peec AI, Otterly and SE Ranking, plus Google's
own Search Console announcement, every page fetched and read on
**2026-09-03**. Compared against `BUILD.md` §6.2, §6.3 (the closed dataset
list), §6.4 (the never-pull list) and `DATA-COSTS.md` §6.

Bears on: BP-008, BP-011, BP-025, BP-026, BP-038, BP-050, BP-052; REQ-006,
REQ-008, REQ-041, REQ-065, REQ-096.

Every quotation is verbatim from the page named, on the access date. Where
a sentence draws a conclusion for ReachKit it is marked "derived".

## 1. What is measured — the vocabulary every product shares

Six products publish the same four primitives, under slightly different
names. Each is quoted once from the page that defines it.

**Mention.** Semrush, https://www.semrush.com/kb/1594-ai-seo-metrics
(accessed 2026-09-03): "Mentions: The total number of prompts in which a
brand is included in AI responses. Each mention represents visibility in a
unique AI query." Ahrefs, https://ahrefs.com/blog/brand-radar-methodology/
(accessed 2026-09-03; page states "Updated: February 26, 2026"): "We store
the raw responses, and users can then search this corpus to surface
citations (linked URLs) and mentions (string matches) for any term."

**Citation.** Semrush (same page): "Citations: The number of AI responses
that cite your domain as a source." Profound,
https://help.tryprofound.com/articles/3443229936-answer-engine-insights-overview
(accessed 2026-09-03): "A citation is a reference or link to a specific
webpage, article, or resource within an answer engine response."

**Visibility (a percentage of responses).** Profound (same page): "The
visibility score is expressed as a percentage, calculated by dividing the
number of responses that include your brand by the total number of
responses that include at least one brand. For example, if your brand
appears in 5 out of 10 responses, your visibility score would be 50%
(5/10)." Peec AI, https://docs.peec.ai/metrics/brand-metrics/visibility
(accessed 2026-09-03): "Visibility Score = (Number of responses mentioning
your brand / Total responses) × 100". Semrush Prompt Tracking
(https://www.semrush.com/kb/1594-ai-seo-metrics): "A zero-percent visibility
means that the domain isn't ranking in the top citations for any of these
prompts; and a 100-percent visibility means that the domain keeps the first
citation for all of these keywords."

The two denominators differ: Profound divides by responses that named at
least one brand; Peec divides by all responses. ReachKit's REQ-006 divides
by searches on which an AI answer appeared, a third denominator, stated on
the card (derived).

**Share of voice.** Profound (same overview page): "The share of voice is
expressed as a percentage, calculated by dividing the number of responses
that mention your brand by the total number of all brand mentions across
all responses." Semrush: "Share of Voice: Percentage of mentions your brand
receives in AI-generated answers compared to competitors in your market."
Ahrefs: "It calculates AI Share of Voice (SOV) based on how often brands are
mentioned or cited in responses from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Microsoft
Copilot, and Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode."

Beyond the four: **position** (Semrush: "Average Position: Where a citation
of your domain typically appears in AI-generated responses for tracked
prompts (e.g., first citation, second citation, etc)"; Peec's docs index
lists "Position: How your brand ranks in AI responses"), **sentiment**
(Profound: "Sentiment is Profound's evaluation of how your brand is
portrayed in AI-generated responses"), and a **composite score** (Semrush,
https://www.semrush.com/kb/1596-visibility-overview-report, accessed
2026-09-03: "AI Visibility Score (0–100): A normalized benchmark showing
your brand's overall share of presence in AI responses.").

## 2. How the answers are collected

**Real product interfaces, not vendor APIs.** Semrush,
https://www.semrush.com/kb/1607-semrush-ai-visibility-data (accessed
2026-09-03): "Prompt responses are captured from real requests and not via
any APIs of LLMs"; "The Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit analyzes responses
generated by the latest model of ChatGPT in search mode." Ahrefs
(methodology page): "All prompts run through the free, publicly available
web interfaces of ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, and other supported
platforms to reflect typical user experiences." Ahrefs' one exception,
https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/11064852-how-to-use-brand-radar
(accessed 2026-09-03): "Claude — Anthropic's AI assistant, tracked via API
(Claude Sonnet 4.6 with web search). Available for custom prompts only;
each check consumes 8 checks."

Derived: ReachKit's ChatGPT column comes from DataForSEO's LLM Scraper,
which the vendor describes as "results from ChatGPT Search mode"
(`RESEARCH-dataforseo-endpoints.md` §4.2) — the same surface Semrush names.
The LLM Responses API (Perplexity, deferred) is an API answer, which is the
collection method the incumbents avoid for the engines they can scrape.

**Where the prompts come from.** Two schools.

- *Search-derived corpora.* Ahrefs: "Queries are collected from Google's
  'People Also Ask' corpus and Ahrefs' 110 billion keyword database (28.7
  billion keywords tracked with positive search volume), then expanded into
  related sub-questions using two different systems: PAA and Fanout".
  Semrush: "We source billions of real prompts from AI search clickstream
  data and Google's keyword dataset for AI Overviews. These are organized
  into meaningful Topics, with duplicates removed and phrasing
  simplified—while always preserving the original intent and semantics."
  Semrush on why topics, not prompts: "Unlike keywords, individual prompts
  are often too specific and unique to measure directly. That's why Semrush
  calculates topic-level volume in our Prompt Research report."
- *Customer-defined prompts.* Profound: "Prompts in Profound are the queries
  Profound automatically sends to answer engines on a daily basis." Otterly,
  https://otterly.ai/llm-info/ (accessed 2026-09-03): "Users define search
  prompts (questions real customers ask). OtterlyAI runs these prompts across
  supported AI experiences. OtterlyAI analyzes responses for brand mentions
  and website citations and reports trends and opportunities."

Derived: ReachKit's twelve questions are the first school at small scale —
derived from Google search data (`keyword_suggestions`) and framed as
questions (`BUILD.md` §6.7) — with the customer's correction (REQ-094) as
the only hand-editing. No product documents a twelve-prompt portfolio;
Semrush's smallest bundle is "25 prompts for Prompt Tracking"
(https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit, accessed
2026-09-03) and Otterly's base plan "includes 10 keywords for monitoring"
(https://www.semrush.com/kb/1487-otterly-ai-search-monitoring, accessed
2026-09-03).

**Cadence.** Profound: "we run a portfolio of prompts against each AI
platform once a day, every day" (https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/is-once-a-day-enough,
dated "8 Jul, 2026", accessed 2026-09-03). Semrush Prompt Tracking: "This
data is collected by Semrush's Position Tracking tool querying your target
prompts on your target platform (and location) each day"; its brand
database: "Data is updated weekly". Ahrefs: "Question sets are updated and
tested in chatbots monthly, using a 90-day reporting window"; its index
table (help article): "AI Overviews | Every few days (matches Keywords
Explorer)"; "AI Chatbots (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.) | Once a month"; custom
prompts "can be adjusted to check monthly, or as frequently as daily".
Otterly (Semrush app listing): "Automatic updates: Get continuous, automated
updates to ensure you always have the latest insights".

Derived: the daily products are the customer-prompt school; the monthly
products are the corpus school. ReachKit's weekly re-measurement (REQ-065)
sits between them and matches neither incumbent's cadence exactly.

**Scale and price points published.** Ahrefs: "Monthly query volume
(approx.): ChatGPT – 14.5 million · Perplexity – 14.6 million · Gemini –
14.6 million · Copilot – 13.3 million · AI Overviews – 282 million · AI Mode
– 24 million · Grok – 13 million". Semrush: "a collection of over 317
million prompts and responses on ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, and
AI Mode"; "Price: $99 per month."; limits "300 daily queries in AI Analysis
reports and 1000 daily queries in Prompt Research · 25 prompts for Prompt
Tracking". Otterly: "The base plan for Otterly - AI Search Monitoring is
$27/month and includes 10 keywords for monitoring" and "10 search prompts:
$29/mo".

**Engines covered.** Ahrefs lists seven (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini,
Copilot, AI Overviews, AI Mode, Grok). Otterly: "Google AI Overviews/AI
Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot". Profound's
experiment ran "7 platforms (ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft
Copilot, DeepSeek, Google AI Mode, and Google AI Overviews)". ReachKit's
MVP battery is two engines plus AI Overviews (`BUILD.md` §6.2).

## 3. How the incumbents act on it — the changelog direction

Peec AI changelog, https://peec.ai/changelog (accessed 2026-09-03):

- "Jul 6, 2026": "When an AI engine answers one of your prompts, it quietly
  runs its own web searches first, then writes the answer from what it
  finds. Until now those searches were invisible, so you were optimizing for
  the answer without knowing what the model actually looked for to build
  it." "Query Fanouts is a new page that shows you exactly those searches."
- "Jun 17, 2026" (shopping): "Visibility is the share of AI shopping answers
  where your product appears. Win rate is the share of those answers where
  it lands first. Position is where it ranks when it shows up."
- "Aug 3, 2026": "Visibility tells you whether AI mentions your brand. It
  does not tell you what the model actually says about you."

Profound changelog, https://product.tryprofound.com/changelog (accessed
2026-09-03): "August 28th, 2026": "Prompt Volumes now spans 35 countries";
"June 26th, 2026": "Every week, Projects organize thousands of data points
across Visibility, Citations, Sentiment, Prompt Volumes, and connected
context in your Knowledge Base to surface the opportunities worth acting
on. Each Project comes with a data-backed brief and recommended tasks to
execute."

Ahrefs on what its numbers are: "Metrics are directional indicators, not
exact traffic counts – best understood as modeled visibility signals, and
not performance metrics." And: "Estimated Impressions weight mentions by
Google search volume to model potential exposure. This is a modeling
choice, not a measured relationship: we don't claim a validated link
between Google search volume and how often a query is asked inside an AI
tool." And: "We do not filter out hallucinated or malformed links, as they
reflect real model output."

Derived: the direction of travel in 2026 is from *whether* a brand is
named toward *what the engine searched for* (query fan-out), *what it says*
(perception) and *recommended tasks* — the last being what ReachKit already
does with its calendar (BP-039, BP-040) from a much smaller measurement.
The DataForSEO LLM Scraper's `search_results` array ("all web search
outputs the model retrieved when looking up information, including
duplicates and unused entries", `RESEARCH-dataforseo-endpoints.md` §4.2) is
the raw material for Peec's "Query Fanouts" page and is already inside the
payload ReachKit buys for the ChatGPT column.

## 4. Google's own report — first-party AI visibility, free

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports
(post dated "Wednesday, June 3, 2026"; accessed 2026-09-03).

- "Note: As of August 31, 2026, we've rolled out these insights to all
  websites worldwide."
- "The new Search Console reports are designed to give you dedicated views
  of your impressions within generative AI features on Search, such as AI
  Overviews and AI Mode, as well as generative AI features in Discover."
- The listed fields: "Impressions: How often URLs from your site appeared in
  generative AI features in Search and Discover." "Pages: Check which URLs
  appeared within AI features." "Countries: Understand your visibility on a
  country basis." "Devices: Identify the devices people are using when
  seeing your website (available for Search results)." "Dates: Monitor your
  performance over time with hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly
  granularity."
- "We're continuing to work with website owners to understand what insights
  and data would be most helpful to inform their strategies, such as adding
  additional metrics over time."

Derived: for a customer who connects Search Console, Google now reports
which of their URLs appeared in AI Overviews and AI Mode — observed, not
sampled, and at no vendor cost. The list of fields names impressions and
pages, not clicks and not the query. It is a customer-side dataset that
`BUILD.md` §6.3 does not admit and §17 does not forbid; admitting it is a
product-scope change (`OWNER-QUESTIONS.md`, Q3).

## 5. What maps to `BUILD.md` §6.3's closed list, and what does not

| Incumbent primitive | ReachKit source in §6.3 | Mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Mention (string match in the answer text) | "Brand mentions/citations = string match over references"; scraper `markdown` | maps — same method Ahrefs documents ("mentions (string matches)") |
| Citation (linked domain) | `ai_overview_reference.domain`; scraper `sources` | maps — field for field |
| Visibility % | REQ-006's "cited on N of the M searches that produced an AI answer" | maps with a different, stated denominator |
| Share of voice vs rivals | BP-026's share of the market's 12 searches; rival AI citations | maps at the card's level, per question, not per brand-mention count |
| Position within the answer | not in §6.3 | does not map; `references` are ordered, so a first-citation position is derivable at 0¢ if ever promised |
| Sentiment / perception | not in §6.3; would be an LLM call per answer | does not map; §6.2 rules "no own inference for AI visibility" |
| Prompt / AI search volume | AI Keyword Data — on §6.4's never-list until v1.1 | does not map |
| Query fan-out (what the engine searched) | scraper `search_results`, already in the payload | derivable at 0¢; no surface reads it |
| Daily cadence, thousands of prompts | 12 questions, weekly | does not map — see `RESEARCH-ai-answer-stability.md` for what that costs in stability |
| Five to seven engines | ChatGPT + AI Mode + AI Overviews | does not map by design (`DATA-COSTS.md` §6 item 5: a fourth engine needs its own price-book row) |
| Google's own AI impressions per URL | not in §6.3 | owner question |

## Why this sits here

None of this is a requirement: it is what other vendors measure and how,
kept as evidence so that the analyst and architect can cite one place when
a card's metric is named or challenged (rule 2.4). The one thing here that
would change what ReachKit promises — a first-party Search Console dataset —
is a question for the owner, not a line in this file.
