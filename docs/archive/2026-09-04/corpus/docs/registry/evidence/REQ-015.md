# Evidence — REQ-015 (why a free scan that hits its spend ceiling degrades rather than fails)

> REQ-015 was superseded by REQ-003 on 2026-08-30. This evidence now supports
> REQ-003 criterion 11 and is cited from REQ-003's rationale under this filename,
> which is kept so the citation does not rot.

Source: `BUILD.md` v1.1, 2026-08-28 (in this repo, at `/BUILD.md`).

- §6.5, the cost seam, verbatim: "Caps degrade (skip remaining optional work,
  mark scan `degraded`), never throw; money already spent is always ledgered."
- §10, `scans` table, verbatim: "status(running/done/degraded)" — the degraded
  outcome is a stored state of a scan, not an error path.

Why this sits here and not in the requirement: the cap amount, the seam that
enforces it and the `degraded` status value are cost-control and storage facts
(rule 2.1 — a REQ carries no vendor or implementation evidence). REQ-015
criterion 6 states only the observable promise the seam exists to keep — a scan
that meets its ceiling mid-run gives back the report of everything measured to
that point with one written line saying it stopped early, never an error, a hang
or a refusal.
