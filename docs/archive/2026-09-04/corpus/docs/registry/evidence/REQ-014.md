Source: `BUILD.md` v1.1, 2026-08-28 (in this repo, at `/BUILD.md`).

- §6.7 step 5, verbatim: "After the 12 SERPs return: if no domain appears in ≥3
  of the 12 top-10s, the market set is likely wrong (disjoint SERPs = incoherent
  market). The report then leads the header with the category chip and **"Not
  your market? Correct it"** — one correction per free scan re-runs steps 2–5
  (~4.2¢; worst case 10.5¢, still under the 12¢ cap)."
- §6.1, price book, verbatim: "`CAP_FREE / CAP_DEEP / CAP_WEEKLY / CAP_DRAFT` |
  12¢ · 150¢ · 40¢ · 45¢".
- §6.3, verbatim: "**Free scan** (total ~6.3¢, cap 12¢)".

Why this sits here and not in the requirement: the correction's cost and the
free scan's cap are cost-control facts (rule 2.1 — no vendor evidence in a REQ).
REQ-014 states only the observable behaviour — the category is always shown, an
incoherent market surfaces the correction, a correction re-derives the report,
and a second correction is refused in writing.
