# Evidence — REQ-008

Source: `BUILD.md` v1.1, 2026-08-28 (in this repo, at `/BUILD.md`).

## The locale disclosure criterion 7 requires

- §6.3a, verbatim: "**MVP is US-English only.** Every SERP, suggestion and volume
  uses Google US / `en` — one location constant (`SERP_LOCATION`), never
  per-customer derivation. Consequences stated, not hidden: volumes and rivals
  will be wrong for non-US markets; the free report footer carries one written
  line — *"Measured on US Google. More countries soon."* Locale derivation (site
  `lang` + TLD → country) is the designed v1.1 upgrade and changes no other part
  of the pipeline."
- §6.1, price book, verbatim: "`SERP_LOCATION` | Google US · en (MVP; §6.3a)".

Why this sits here and not in the requirement: the exact string is the owner's
copy (constitution §1, customer-visible strings) and it is settled in the source
above, so quoting it here keeps one home for the words. REQ-008 criterion 7
states only what the line must say — the market it was measured in, and that
more countries are coming — so a build that carries the first half and drops the
second fails the criterion without the criterion having to hold the copy itself.

## Why criteria 9 and 10 frame rivals instead of sizing them (owner ruling R2, 2026-08-31)

The free report cannot measure a rival's size, and the ruling settles that it may
not try:

- §6.4, the never-pull list, verbatim: "Never: SERP depth >10 · search operators
  (`site:` = 5×) · clickstream flags · `load_async_ai_overview` · Labs historical
  endpoints · **per-rival `ranked_keywords` on the free path** · a 4th engine ·
  per-draft re-probing · AI Keyword Data (v1.1 candidate only)." (Emphasis added;
  the wording is verbatim.)
- §6.1, price book, verbatim: "`RANKED_RIVAL_ROWS / COST` | 100 rows · 2.4¢",
  "`COMPETITORS_MAX` | 5", and "`CAP_FREE / CAP_DEEP / CAP_WEEKLY / CAP_DRAFT` |
  12¢ · 150¢ · 40¢ · 45¢". Sizing five rivals costs 5 × 2.4¢ = 12¢ — the whole
  free cap, before any other call. The dataset list for the free path is closed
  (§6.3), so no cheaper substitute is available either.
- §6.6, verbatim: "Free report contrast = *share of the market's 12 biggest
  searches* (top-10 appearances /12, AI citations /m) — meaningful at zero.
  Global ranked-count contrast (12,400 vs 25) is a paid reveal." Occupancy across
  the 12 SERPs is therefore the only rival signal the free report holds: it
  measures dominance in this market, not company size.

The owner's ruling, 2026-08-31, is what criterion 9 turns into a promise: a large
competitor appearing in these results is not a discouragement but validation that
the market has commercial weight — companies of that scale are competing for
these searches, which is evidence the searches are worth competing for.

Criterion 9 binds only the two things the report actually holds — these domains
compete for the same 12 searches, and that presence is evidence those searches
are contested — and criterion 10 is the honesty bound: the line may not reach past
the measurement into what the customer would earn. That keeps standing law 5 of
`sdlc-factory/docs/00-project.md` ("Every UI number traces to a stored
measurement with a date") and §14's posture intact, and it is why the owner's own
phrase "the customer is not in them" was *not* bound into the line: it is true of
a cold-start report but false of a report where the customer appears in some of
the 12, and criterion 1 already shows the customer's own count either way.

Criterion 10's ban on attributing size to a rival is the same ruling seen from the
other side: with `ranked_keywords` off the free path, any adjective of scale in
that line would be an invented measurement (constitution rule 1.2).

## Why the framing agrees with §2.5 rather than contradicting it

- §2.5, verbatim: "Red appears only for *the customer's problem being shown to
  them* (blocked, absent, 0/12). Rival strength is neutral gray, never red —
  rivals are context, not alarms."

That is the same posture at the level of colour that criteria 9 and 10 state at
the level of what is said: a rival's presence is context. Criterion 6 already
bans a severity label on a rival's figure; criterion 9 adds the written line that
says what the context means. Neither restates the other, and how the line is
styled remains the design system's.

## Why it lives on REQ-008 and not on REQ-006

The signal the framing rests on is occupancy across the 12 SERPs (§6.6, quoted
above), which is REQ-008's card. Rule 2.4 gives that claim one home; REQ-006's
AI-answers card names cited domains but states no rival contrast to frame, and it
already carries 13 criteria against rule 2.1's ~12 soft budget.

## Not in conflict with REQ-091 criterion 2

REQ-091 criterion 2's "exactly one written line" governs a *place where a value
would sit and does not*. The rival contrast holds values in every case criterion 9
covers (it is suppressed where no rival was found), so criterion 9's line is not a
second line in that criterion's sense.
