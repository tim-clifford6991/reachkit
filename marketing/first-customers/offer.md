# The Founding 50 offer

Goal (Hormozi): an offer so good people feel stupid saying no. Every line must be true on
production on the day it is said. The product items exist today; the bonuses are the owner's time.

## The value equation

**Value = (Dream outcome × Perceived likelihood) ÷ (Time delay × Effort and sacrifice)**

| Lever | Their worry | What the offer does |
|---|---|---|
| Dream outcome ↑ | "Buyers ask ChatGPT and hear my rival's name" | Named when buyers ask; new pages on your own site every week |
| Likelihood ↑ | "SEO never worked for me" | Free report as proof before paying; our own site as the example; the Monday measurement; a service guarantee |
| Time delay ↓ | "SEO takes six months" | Pages go live in week 1; the service promise (D2): first page live within 48 hours of connecting, or the founder does it with them; a Monday mail from week 1 |
| Effort ↓ | "No time, no know-how" | Setup done with them on a call; they never write a word; their job is one mail a week |

## Scarcity, urgency, guarantee (all must be true)

- **Scarcity:** 50 places, because the owner sets each one up by hand and the daily scan ceiling is
  real.
- **Urgency:** founding waves (D4): 15 places closing 6 Dec, 15 closing 20 Dec, 20 closing 31 Jan.
  Each wave opens with a date; when it closes, the bonuses close with it.
- **Guarantee** (one to one only, never on a public page):
  1. Service (D2): "If your first page isn't live within 48 hours of connecting, I get on a call
     and do it with you."
  2. Anti-guarantee (D2): "No refunds, because you see everything before you pay: your free report
     is the proof." This turns the no-refund rule into a reason to trust.
  3. Credit (D3, on hold): "No page live in your first 7 days → your second month is free." A
     Stripe coupon applied by hand; no refund.
