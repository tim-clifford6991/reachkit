# Playbook: build, pitch, resources, timeline

How the first-50 offer (`offer.md`) reaches the people in `icp-and-sources.md`. Owner = Tim;
agent = `growth-marketing` drafting in a pull request. Nothing is posted, sent or paid for without
the owner's go.

## 1. Build the offer (in order)

1. **Rule D1–D4** in the README. Without the setup promise and one end date there is no urgency
   or risk reversal to pitch. (D2 and D4 re-ruled 2026-11-20, #2981.)
2. **Prove delivery before promising it.** Close G1 (friendly businesses on subdomain connects only,
   refunded by hand; no WordPress sites in the first 50, D8) and start the G2 run of 7 of 7 days. Time both setups: that time
   is the "about 10 minutes, or a call" claim, or the claim changes.
3. **Write the proof.** Our own site's report and live pages are the one example we may show
   (SPEC §1 *Our own report only*). Capture screenshots: the score, the rivals named, a live page,
   a Monday mail.
4. **Fix the figures.** Check each "what it would cost elsewhere" line in `offer.md` against three
   real public price lists and keep the sources in this folder. Drop a line we cannot source.
   Done 2026-11-15 in `price-anchors.md`; re-check before the first send and monthly.
5. **Make the assets** in section 3.
6. **Dry-run the pitch** on two friendly owners. Note every question; each becomes an objection
   answer in section 2.
7. **Start outreach** (30 Nov 2026), warm contacts first.

## 2. The pitch

### The sequence (one lead)

| Step | When | What | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 | ACA opener (warm) or signal opener (cold) | A reply |
| 2 | On reply | Link to the free scan; they run it on their own domain | A scan |
| 3 | Within 24 h of the scan | 5-minute recorded walkthrough of their report | Belief |
| 4 | Same message | The first-50 offer and a link to book 15 minutes | A call or a payment |
| 5 | The call | CLOSER (below), then setup on the same call if they pay | Paid and connected |
| 6 | Within a week of setup | First page live; check it in `/app/review`. The product's first-live mail carries the link, so message them only if it is late (then call) | Activation |
| 7 | Day 14, day 30 | Check-in; ask for a referral and a quote | Retention, referral |

Follow up no-replies at day 3 and day 7, then stop. Hormozi: most sales come after follow-ups.

### Scripts (drafts; English only, D10)

The full set, with follow-ups, the call agenda and the evidence for each claim, is `scripts.md`
(R4). The short versions below stay as the summary; where they differ, `scripts.md` wins.

**Warm ACA opener**
> Hey {name}, saw {specific recent thing}, congrats. Quick question: when people ask ChatGPT for
> {their category}, do you know if it names you? I built a free scan that shows exactly who AI and
> Google send buyers to instead. Want to run it on {domain}? No signup.

**Cold signal opener** (LinkedIn connection note, then a message; no cold email for now, D11)
> Hi {name}, I asked ChatGPT "{exact question}" and it named {rival A} and {rival B}, not
> {company}, even though you {strong signal: reviews, ranking}. I built a free tool that shows where
> AI answers send your buyers and what to write to change it. Worth a look?

**After the scan (with the walkthrough video)**
> Here's a 5-minute walkthrough of your report. The short version: {one gap}. ReachKit writes and
> publishes pages to close it, up to 7 a week, on your own site. I'm setting up the first 50
> customers personally: set up in about 10 minutes, or we do it with you on a call. {stack in one
> line}, €49 a month, cancel any time. {left} of 50 spots left until 31 Jan 2027. Book 15
> minutes: {link}, or start here: {checkout}.

**Referral ask (day 30)**
> Glad it's working. Who else do you know who owns a business website and hates marketing? Send
> them this link, or introduce us. {Only while spots are left: "I'll set them up personally
> too."}

Full version: `check-ins.md`.

### The call: CLOSER (15 minutes)

| Step | Say |
|---|---|
| Clarify why they're here | "What made you run the scan?" |
| Label the problem | "So buyers ask AI and hear {rival}, and you have no time to write." |
| Overview past pain | "What have you tried? What happened?" |
| Sell the vacation | Their pages live, their name in answers, one mail a Monday. Not the machinery. |
| Explain away concerns | The objections below |
| Reinforce the decision | Pay on the call, set up on the call in about 10 minutes; each page goes live on its day, by itself, once your site is connected |

### Objections

| They say | Answer (true today) |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive" | Compare with the stack, not zero: in one survey of 213 freelance writers (data from about 2021), the most common rate for a 1,500-word post was $250–399 (`price-anchors.md` A1); a month of ReachKit is €49, and its pages are written by AI |
| "I'll think about it" | "What would you need to see? There are {left} of 50 spots left until 31 Jan 2027." Say only the real count |
| "SEO didn't work before" | The report is the proof before you pay; the Monday mail measures it every week |
| "I can do it with ChatGPT" | You can; the question is whether you will write and publish up to 7 pages every week |
| "Will Google penalise AI pages?" | "No. Google has said that using AI to write pages isn't against its rules. What it acts on is pages churned out in bulk just to game search, with nothing new in them, however they were written. ReachKit writes each page from facts on your own site and linked public sources. It holds back any page with an invented fact or one that repeats a page you already have. And it only writes a new page when it has something the top results don't already say." Then the written answer and the *Never say* list: `scripts.md` §7 (seo-master, #2693) |
| "I'm not technical" | Set up in about 10 minutes; we do it together on this call |
| "I'm on WordPress" | "Not among the first 50, I'm starting with sites that connect by subdomain. Can I come back to you later?" (D8, all 50). WordPress publishing is off on production today |
| "Can I get a refund?" | Before you pay (D2): you read your report and a first page before you pay; payments aren't refunded unless the law requires it; cancel any time and you're not charged again |

**Pause rule:** if a paying customer has no page live within a week of setup, or a P0 is open,
new outreach pauses until it is fixed. A stalled week waits; it is not pushed with more outreach.

## 5. What to measure weekly

Contacts, replies, scans, calls, paying, set up in about 10 minutes or on a call (README Progress
table), plus where each payer heard of us: the `?ref=` channel link, stored with the scan (README ›
Channel links, #2692), and asked by hand for a purchase that reads `(none)` (D7). The deciding
number until 31 Jan is every paying customer set up (in about 10 minutes, or on a call) with a
first page live within a week of setup: 100%.
