# Playbook: build, pitch, resources, timeline

How the Founding 50 offer (`offer.md`) reaches the people in `icp-and-sources.md`. Owner = Tim;
agent = `growth-marketing` drafting in a pull request. Nothing is posted, sent or paid for without
the owner's go.

## 1. Build the offer (in order)

1. **Rule D1–D4** in the README. Without a price lock, a guarantee and wave dates there is no
   urgency or risk reversal to pitch.
2. **Prove delivery before promising it.** Close G1 (one friendly business on a subdomain, one on
   WordPress, refunded by hand) and start the G2 run of 7 of 7 days. Time both setups: that time
   is the "15-minute call" claim, or the claim changes.
3. **Write the proof.** Our own site's report and live pages are the one example we may show
   (SPEC §1 *Our own report only*). Capture screenshots: the score, the rivals named, a live page,
   a Monday mail.
4. **Fix the figures.** Check each "what it would cost elsewhere" line in `offer.md` against three
   real public price lists and keep the sources in this folder. Drop a line we cannot source.
5. **Make the assets** in section 3.
6. **Dry-run the pitch** on two friendly owners. Note every question; each becomes an objection
   answer in section 2.
7. **Open wave 1.**

## 2. The pitch

### The sequence (one lead)

| Step | When | What | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 | ACA opener (warm) or signal opener (cold) | A reply |
| 2 | On reply | Link to the free scan; they run it on their own domain | A scan |
| 3 | Within 24 h of the scan | 5-minute recorded walkthrough of their report | Belief |
| 4 | Same message | The Founding 50 offer and a link to book 15 minutes | A call or a payment |
| 5 | The call | CLOSER (below), then setup on the same call if they pay | Paid and connected |
| 6 | Within 48 h | First page live; message them the link | Activation |
| 7 | Day 14, day 30 | Check-in; ask for a referral and a quote | Retention, referral |

Follow up no-replies at day 3 and day 7, then stop. Hormozi: most sales come after follow-ups.

### Scripts (drafts; EN first, DE once wave 3 opens)

**Warm ACA opener**
> Hey {name}, saw {specific recent thing}, congrats. Quick question: when people ask ChatGPT for
> {their category}, do you know if it names you? I built a free scan that shows exactly who AI and
> Google send buyers to instead. Want to run it on {domain}? A minute, no signup.

**Cold signal opener** (UK and US email, LinkedIn anywhere)
> Hi {name}, I asked ChatGPT "{exact question}" and it named {rival A} and {rival B}, not
> {company}, even though you {strong signal: reviews, ranking}. I built a free tool that shows where
> AI answers send your buyers and what to write to change it. Worth a look?

**After the scan (with the walkthrough video)**
> Here's a 5-minute walkthrough of your report. The short version: {one gap}. ReachKit writes and
> publishes pages to close it, up to 7 a week, on your own site. I'm taking 50 founding customers
> and setting each one up personally: {stack in one line}, €49 a month, cancel any time, price
> locked while you stay. Wave {n} closes {date}. Book 15 minutes: {link}, or start here: {checkout}.

**Referral ask (day 30)**
> Glad it's working. Who else do you know who owns a business website and hates marketing? I'll
> set them up personally too.

### The call: CLOSER (15 minutes)

| Step | Say |
|---|---|
| Clarify why they're here | "What made you run the scan?" |
| Label the problem | "So buyers ask AI and hear {rival}, and you have no time to write." |
| Overview past pain | "What have you tried? What happened?" |
| Sell the vacation | Their pages live, their name in answers, one mail a Monday. Not the machinery. |
| Explain away concerns | The objections below |
| Reinforce the decision | Pay on the call, connect on the call, first page within 48 h |

### Objections

| They say | Answer (true today) |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive" | Compare with the stack, not zero: one freelance page costs about what a month of ReachKit costs |
| "I'll think about it" | "What would you need to see? Your wave closes {date}; the bonuses close with it." |
| "SEO didn't work before" | The report is the proof before you pay; the Monday mail measures it every week |
| "I can do it with ChatGPT" | You can; the question is whether you will write and publish up to 7 pages every week |
| "Is AI content penalised?" | Hand to seo-master for the approved answer before wave 1 |
| "I'm not technical" | We connect it together on this call |
| "Can I get a refund?" | Anti-guarantee (D2): you saw everything first; cancel any time and you're not charged again |

waits; it is not pushed with more outreach.

## 5. What to measure weekly

Contacts, replies, scans, calls, paying, live within 48 h (README Progress table), plus where each
payer heard of us, asked by hand (D7). The deciding number for waves 1 and 2 is live within 48 h:
100%.
