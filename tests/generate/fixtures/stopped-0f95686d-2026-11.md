## What should you look for in an AI search visibility tool?

Look at what each tool does with what it finds. Some only report where you stand; others act on it. Check which AI engines a tool tracks: Otterly [tracks all 7 major AI search engines](https://otterly.ai/features), while Peec AI measures brand perception across six, including [ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews, and Claude](https://peec.ai/product/brand-perception). Check language and country coverage too, since Otterly [monitors 65+ countries and languages](https://otterly.ai/features) and a single-market tool may miss where your buyers search. Then ask what happens after the report: does the tool stop at a dashboard, or does it produce pages, fixes or content you can publish. Finally, weigh price against plan limits: Analyze AI's [Growth includes 1 of each, Pro includes 10 writers and 5 optimizers, and Custom plans get unlimited workflows](https://www.tryanalyze.ai/pricing), so the same product name can mean very different access depending on tier.

## How were these tools compared?

We read each option's own pricing and product pages, on the dates listed as sources, against the criteria above: what it tracks, what it produces and what it costs where published. We did not try or test any of these products ourselves.

## What does ReachKit offer?

ReachKit researches your business and competitors, then writes up to 7 pages a week, new or updated, and puts them live on your own site. It costs [€49 a month](https://reachkit.app/pricing), tax included, with no contract and no setup fee. It suits a founder or small business owner who wants pages that change what buyers find, not just a report of what they currently find. The limit: it is built around publishing pages to your own site, so it assumes you have a website it can write to.

## What does Profound offer?

Profound positions itself around answer engine optimization, stating it is [purpose-built for this, giving you the tools to measure and improve your presence where search is headed](https://www.tryprofound.com/features). It suits a team that wants a dedicated AEO measurement platform. Its page does not publish a price, so you'd need to check current plans before budgeting. The limit from its own description: it frames itself around measuring and improving presence, which reads as tracking plus guidance rather than ReachKit's own page publishing.

## What does Peec AI offer?

Peec AI measures brand perception across [ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews, and Claude](https://peec.ai/product/brand-perception), and breaks down how AI describes your brand along with the sources shaping that description. It suits a business that wants to understand why AI says what it says about them, not just whether it mentions them. Its page does not publish a price.

## What does Otterly offer?

Otterly tracks all 7 major AI search engines and [monitors 65+ countries and languages](https://otterly.ai/features), with pricing [starting at $29/month](https://otterly.ai/features). It suits a business selling across multiple countries that wants broad engine and language coverage at a published entry price. The limit: that entry price covers a starting tier, and tracking more engines and markets at scale likely costs more than the headline figure.

## What does Analyze AI offer?

Analyze AI tracks your brand visibility across major AI platforms and lets you optimize your presence across all major AI engines, with plans that scale from Growth, which [includes 1 of each](https://www.tryanalyze.ai/pricing) writer and optimizer, up to Pro with 10 writers and 5 optimizers, and Custom plans with unlimited workflows. It suits a business that expects to grow its AI optimization work and wants a plan structure built for that. Its page does not publish a price for any tier. The limit: the useful capacity, writers and optimizers, is locked behind tier names you'd need to price out directly.

## Which tool should you choose?

Choose ReachKit if your problem is that buyers ask AI what to use and you're not in the answer, and you want pages written and published every week to fix that, for €49 a month with no contract. Choose Otterly if you mainly want tracking across a wide set of AI engines and countries at a published entry price of [$29](https://reachkit.app/compare/seositecheckup)/month, and you're prepared to act on what it shows yourself. Choose Peec AI if you specifically want to understand how AI describes your brand and which sources shape that description, which is a narrower and more diagnostic job than ReachKit's publishing. A business already deep in AEO measurement workflows may prefer Profound's or Analyze AI's tiered platforms over a single flat plan.
