50 Years of Trusted Accountancy & Tax Advice

Providing award-winning Tax & Accountancy expertise in Manchester, London and across the UK

With 50 years of heritage, Alexander & Co is an ICAEW-regulated firm dedicated to delivering robust solutions for business owners. Our team of specialist Accountants & Tax Advisers provide the strategic insight and dependable tax advice required to navigate the complexities of today’s business environment.

Highly recommended by our long-standing clients, we are a leading independent accountancy firm with offices in Manchester and London. We have earned a reputation as one of the most dependable accountants and tax advisors for businesses and individuals in the North West and provide strategic support to clients across the UK and internationally.
